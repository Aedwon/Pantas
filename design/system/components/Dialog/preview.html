About Your Study Plan

Your study plan lists what to learn each day. It starts from your exam date and the subjects you pick.

If you change the exam date, the plan updates. Lessons you finished stay marked as done.

You can change your study days at any time in Settings. A day without a lesson is a rest day.

Mock exams are not part of the daily list. Take one when you feel ready.

Questions about the plan? Open Settings and choose Help.

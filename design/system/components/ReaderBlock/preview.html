Note
A fraction names a part of a whole. The bottom number tells how many equal parts there are. The top number tells how many parts you take.
Traps to Watch For
Do not add the bottom numbers. In 1/4 + 1/4 the parts stay the same size. The answer is 2/4, not 2/8.
Example
Work out x² + 3x when x is 2.
Solution
Put 2 in place of x. Then work out each part.2² + 3 × 2 = 4 + 6 = 10The answer is 10.

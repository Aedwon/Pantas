Adding Fractions

You cannot add 12 and 13 yet: the pieces are different sizes.

Make the Bottoms the Same

Find a bottom number both fractions can share. Here it is 6.

Note
Multiply the top and the bottom by the same number.
1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6

Check It

  • Five sixths is a little less than one.
  • It is more than one half.

Set --reader-scale on .reader (1 to 2). The paragraphs, the note, the stacked fractions and the display math all follow it, because each uses a scaled style or em (ruling 4). Below 1024px the reader body takes the phone style by itself (ruling 11).

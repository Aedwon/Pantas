Pantas: every screen, rendered

Every specced surface at 360 by 700, with the real type scale, tokens and components. Read each row left to right. The page stays dark; the toggle governs the phones.

Reading it. Board green appears at most once per screen and marks the single primary action. Where a screen has none, it has no primary action by design: the mock paper, the study plan, the tendency deck.
Amber marks a deadline or a fixed point and is used in exactly two places in the mock: the clock under one minute, and the proctor's last call. Rust marks a loss or a destructive action.
Motion is marked on the caption. Tick Play motion to run it.

## Summary

Recommendation: after the beta ends (15 November), do three low-cost applications. Register Pantas on the government startup portal (free). Apply to the Google for Startups Cloud Program for credits. Join QBO Innovation Hub (free). Together they should take a few hours. None of their pages mentions taking equity.

The large prize is the DOST-PCIEERD Startup Grant Fund (up to 5 million pesos for R&D). It is not open. Its last window closed 31 July 2026. No next date is published. Pantas is not eligible yet.

Main cost: finishing BIR registration, which is already pending. The grant routes need financial statements, so it is a practical prerequisite. An OPC is not required by any program I could verify.

Nearest real deadline: none. No program that fits Pantas has an open window today. Every next cycle shows "no date published".

Decision for you: approve the three post-beta applications. Ask a Philippine accountant whether a sole proprietorship qualifies for the grant routes.

## Notes

1. **PCIEERD Startup Grant Fund.** The 2026 call needed DTI or SEC registration for 1 to 7 years, an early-stage prototype, at least one year of financial statements, talks with 2 to 3 government offices on procurement, and a partner government letter stating a monetary counterpart. Priority areas excluded supply chain, fintech, agriculture and health. A DTI sole proprietorship was accepted in the 2022 call, which also asked for tax returns and financial statements (older source). The Norala SK pilot could become the government partner letter, but only if it is approved.
2. **Registration.** DOST-supported incubators are preferred applicants in the SGF call listing (T). Joining one may help.
3. **Entity.** No verified program requires an OPC. Filipino ownership and nationality rules apply to the grants. An accountant or lawyer must confirm whether a sole proprietorship qualifies, how grants are taxed, and whether BIR registration is required before earning or receiving funds.
4. **Google credits.** Firebase billing runs through Google Cloud. I could not confirm that credits cover Firebase or Gemini usage. The page also asks for "Pre-Seed to Series A funding" and an email address matching the public website, and it excludes educational institutions, consultancies and agencies. Pantas has no outside funding and is registered as "Online Tutorial Services", so ask Google whether it qualifies before relying on these credits. Acceptance is at Google's discretion.
5. **Policy gap.** The Philippine Innovation Act creates an Innovation Fund (Section 21) for innovations serving the poorest, run by the National Innovation Council. It sets no startup grant. The Filipinnovation Awards are the practical entry.

## Ranking (judgment: expected value against effort)

1. Google for Startups Cloud: real cost offset, no equity, a few hours.
2. startup.gov.ph registration: nearly free, and it is the gateway to DICT grants.
3. QBO membership: free; mentors and investor access.
4. PCIEERD Startup Grant Fund: highest value, but 2027 or later, and it needs BIR, filings and a government partner.
5. DICT Startup Grant Fund: moderate value, timing unknown.
6. Filipinnovation Awards and DICT Startup Challenge: watch for dates.
7. Anthropic: join the newsletter; apply when open.
8. Everything else: not worth effort now.

## Recommendation

Apply to Google first, on 16 November, once the beta shows a working product. Register on startup.gov.ph and QBO the same week. Finish the pending BIR registration, because it is a practical prerequisite for the grant routes. Defer the OPC until an accountant says a program or tax reason requires it. Prepare a one-page PCIEERD case for the next call, and watch the PCIEERD call page, DICT, and DEPDev for dates.

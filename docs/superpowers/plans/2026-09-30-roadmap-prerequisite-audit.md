## For approval

The proposed links below apply to whole topics. A prerequisite places all unread
lessons of that topic before the dependent topic. It does not block a topic when
its prerequisite is excluded from the student's scope or already covered.

There are 12 proposed links across 11 topics. The other 14 topics have no proposed
prerequisites. Each link needs a concept used in the dependent lessons and taught
in the prerequisite topic. Topic order alone is not evidence. An applied worked
example is distinguished from a general explanation of the underlying skill.

Approve or edit the proposed prerequisites in this table. The app map stays empty
until Aedwon or the assigned reviewer approves rows. No content or protected
package changes are proposed.

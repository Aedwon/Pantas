## Check summary

1. AC-1, RevenueCat blocked or replayed: blocking leaves only the SDK cache, whose old `requestDate` fails the "later than" rule; replay fails the nonce signature. Gap: `verifiedOnDevice` answers carry the device clock (L2A4-1).
2. AC-1, clock manipulation: rollbacks while the app runs are capped at 10 minutes in total. Rollbacks while the app is closed, followed by a restart and waiting, are invisible, so Pro outlasts the paid period plus 3 days in calendar time, capped by hours of use (L2A4-2).
3. AC-1, honest lockout: a once-wrong clock recovers after one fresh verified check. A forward device clock fed through `verifiedOnDevice` could lock an honest user out until real time catches up; L2A4-1's fix removes it.
4. "Fresh answer from RevenueCat's servers": not observable in the Flutter SDK. The replacement is `verified` plus `requestDate` strictly later than the stored trusted time (L2A4-1).
5. AC-2 and AC-7: the server filters known products; the phone rule as worded lets an unknown product cancel a known one, and the prod grant inventory from gate 3 is missing (L2A4-3).
6. Deploy steps: placement and approvals are right; no backfill for purchases made before the webhook went live (L2A4-4).
7. B7 review line vs SUBAGENTS Part 7b: consistent on tier 1 acceptance of money, entitlement, rules and data; the lead's own integration edits lack a reviewer (L2A4-5).
8. Tier 1 items under Part 7b (money and entitlement): L2A4-1 to L2A4-5 go to the lead; L2A4-2 needs Aedwon's decision.

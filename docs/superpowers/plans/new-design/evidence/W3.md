### Binding ambiguity

Task 7 says a pending purchase blocks another purchase. L2 W3 section 3 and the
approved pending board require the same behavior. The installed Flutter SDK
10.8.0 reports `paymentPendingError` on the initial attempt, but its public
CustomerInfo, SubscriptionInfo and Purchases interfaces do not expose a pending
order list or a pending-order cancellation/expiry query. Active entitlement
updates can confirm completion. An inactive CustomerInfo answer alone cannot
establish whether an unfinished payment still exists.

The plan does not specify the lifetime or recovery source of that block, including
an app restart or a pending payment canceled or expired before completion.
Choosing a memory-only block lets a restart lose the observed block. Saving the
block without a reliable resolution signal can keep a canceled payment blocked.
Neither behavior is stated in the approved plan. This is a contract ambiguity,
not a reason to fabricate SDK purchase state, use a timer, add another billing
client, or introduce a new backend integration without a lead ruling.

The existing controller's `_operationInFlight` guard covers only an outstanding
call and resets in `finally`; it cannot by itself represent an ongoing deferred
payment. The initial error's current classification is `store`, demonstrated by
a failing test. The worker has not added a pending block with an invented reset
rule.

Required lead action: define the pending-block lifetime and a trusted recovery
signal for completion, cancellation and expiry, including restart/account-switch
behavior. Record any necessary technical plan amendment before this part runs.

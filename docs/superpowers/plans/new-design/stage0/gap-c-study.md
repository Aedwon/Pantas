Findings worth reading first (facts, not decisions):
- "See all" on the Study map does nothing today: `BookmarksSection` is built with no `onSeeAll` (W:features/lesson/presentation/learn_tab_home.dart:249) and `showBookmarksSheet` has no caller outside tests. The bookmarks sheet is unreachable in the app.
- The topic ending page (route `/study/ending/:topicId`, W:app/router.dart:394) has no caller anywhere in `lib/`. The last lesson of a topic shows no pull handle (W:features/lesson/presentation/widgets/pull_to_advance.dart, `nextTitle == null`). Bookplate, "Still unread", Next block and "That's enough for today." are not reachable.
- Nothing opens the reader with `from=home`. Home rows push `/lesson/:id` (W:features/home/presentation/widgets/up_next_panel.dart:88,94), so the Home origin, the "2 of N" counter and the day rail described in S27 and SP do not exist in the app. The reader bar has no counter at all (W:features/lesson/presentation/widgets/reader_bar.dart).
- The in-lesson quiz is topic-scoped, so every lesson in a topic gets the same first five questions (W:features/quiz/domain/use_cases/start_inline_lesson_quiz.dart:55; QT section "Corrections", item 5).

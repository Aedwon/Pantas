Returning visitor splash (B5)

Returning visitors of the Pantas web app see this logo screen while Flutter loads. First-time visitors already get the approved Welcome screen. Three options below, each in phone and desktop, light and dark. Each loops with a replay button.

Recommendation: Option A

Logo only, centered, gentle fade. It paints first, needs no font, and promises nothing. On a fast load (about 1 second) there is nothing to cut short.

How long it stays

It disappears when Flutter's first frame is ready. Typical is 1 to 3 seconds. The loops here use 2.4 seconds as a stand-in, then fade to a sketch of Home.

Reduced motion

All three obey it. The logo shows at once with no fade, pop, or sliding line. The splash disappears without a fade. Use the checkbox below to preview it.

For your decision

  • Pick A, B or C.
  • Splash background is the app background (light #F5F5F7, dark #151515), not the Welcome's white. This avoids a flash when Home appears.
  • Approve the proposed wording: Proposed screen reader label "Pantas is loading" in all options, and in B the visible wordmark "Pantas".

Fidelity gaps

  • Frames are drawn in this page, not a real browser or Flutter. The "Home" sketch under the splash is a placeholder of cards, not the real Home.
  • The 2.4 second hold is a stand-in. Real duration is whenever Flutter's first frame is ready.
  • Logo shape, colors, Geist font and the pop animation are copied from the approved Welcome board and the built Welcome screen. Dark colors are the final dark tokens.
  • The wordmark in B is Geist Bold typeset text. No approved logo-plus-wordmark artwork exists. The real splash would load Geist from the app's font files, so the wordmark may show in a fallback font for a moment.
  • The line in C is a repeating slide. Flutter gives no real percentage, so it shows activity, not progress.
  • Fonts here come from Google Fonts. The real page uses bundled font files.

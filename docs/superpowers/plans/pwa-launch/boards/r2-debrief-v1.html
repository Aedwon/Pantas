Screen reader order

  1. Heading: Session finished.
  2. Score: 7 of 10 correct
  3. Question buttons 1 to 10 (correct or wrong)
  4. Heading: Next
  5. Link: Order of Operations practice. Practice this before going back to Algebraic Expressions.
  6. Button: PRO. 3 of your misses trace to an earlier lesson. See which. Opens the paywall at the diagnosis.
  7. Button: Dismiss this note
  8. Heading: Your mistakes, 3, then its rows

Tap targets and large text

  • The Free row is one control the full width of the card, at least 44 pt tall. At 130% text it grows to fit and nothing is cut off.
  • Dismiss is 44 by 44 pt, separate from the row, so a tap on the text never dismisses.
  • The PRO tag is part of the row label, not a separate stop.
  • Paywall entry: "See which" opens the Pro paywall scrolled to the diagnosis benefit ("See which lesson your misses trace to"). The paywall itself is not redrawn on this board.

Rules every state follows

  • The row or section appears only when the server traced misses to a prerequisite.
  • At most once per session, dismissible, never on two screens the same day.
  • Free sees the traced count and reason kinds only. Question numbers and mistake notes are Pro only.
  • Free still gets the fix through the plan.

What this board assumes

  • The gold PRO tag follows the plan. The app's current Pro tag is teal, so it would be a new variant.
  • A Pro prerequisite row opens that lesson. The within-lesson row opens the mistakes list.
  • Pro shows at most three mistake lines per row, then "and N more".
  • Prerequisite rows follow the server order (sorted by lesson id), not by miss count.
  • Entry animations from the live screen are not reproduced. The frames are still comps.

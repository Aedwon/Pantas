What an HTML page cannot show faithfully

  • Frame rate on a phone. This page runs in the browser's own renderer, not in Flutter. Dropped frames on iPhone Safari (item 16) only show on the device. The phone cost notes are rules of thumb, not measurements.
  • Flutter's own code. Current demos copy its duration, curve and distances. The page slide shown is the iOS and macOS default, which is what you see. Windows and Android browsers get a different default. Its edge shadow is close but not identical.
  • The Geist font and exact layout. The mini screens use the system font with the app's colors and sizes. Line breaks and small spacing differ.
  • Real wheel input in the app. Flutter on the web may report wheel notches and trackpad momentum differently from this page. The pull demo judges by timing only. Its numbers need a trial on a real mouse and trackpad before sign-off.
  • Layout jumps when the sidebar leaves (item 5). The demo removes the sidebar on the first frame and widens the page beneath. I did not trace every other layout change at that moment.
  • iPhone toolbar and rubber-band scrolling around the tab bar (item 11). They are not shown here.

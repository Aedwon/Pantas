# Completing the Lesson Illustrations

This log records the decision that follows the blocked open-asset sourcing
round. Earlier decisions remain in force unless this log changes them.

---

## 22 August 2026: exact Commons matches did not exist for 52 briefs

The approved plan assigned 56 lesson illustrations to open educational assets.
Two Flash sourcing batches and a Pro verification round found four exact,
license-compatible Commons assets. The other 52 briefs describe custom labeled
teaching diagrams. No single approved source matched them. The plan prohibited
redrawing, combining sources, and approximate substitutions, so the round
stopped.

Aedwon first understood "blocked" to mean that sourcing needed approval:

> "for the open source illustrations, when you say blocked you just mean you
> need my approval right? then okay you have my approval to source them. um,
> make sure proper credits are honored (well that depends on what the source
> wants)"

Sourcing already had approval. The missing decision concerned how to produce
an exact figure when no matching source exists.

| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| Keep the strict Commons-only rule | Preserves the original plan but leaves 52 lessons incomplete. |
| Adapt or combine Commons assets | Can meet some briefs but creates inconsistent visuals and complicated attribution and license compatibility. |
| Accept close substitutes | Finishes faster but weakens the connection between each lesson and its figure. |
| **Draw original figures in the existing Flutter figure system and keep exact Commons assets** | Adds painter and verification work but keeps the lesson briefs exact, the visuals consistent, and the licensing simple. |

**Recommendation:** use the hybrid route. Keep the four verified Commons
assets. Reclassify the other 52 rows as code figures. Do not copy or trace a
third-party diagram. Record authoritative factual references for scientific
verification. External image credits apply only to the four Commons assets.

Aedwon asked for the best route. After the recommendation, he accepted it with
an additional requirement:

> "okay but i'll have to very extensively check those created diagrams okay?
> make it easy for me as well, like how i can view lessons with the lesson
> author"

That requirement rules out a folder of disconnected previews. The diagrams
must appear inside their lessons at the exact marker position.

## 22 August 2026: structured review in Lesson Author

Two review approaches were put to Aedwon:

| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| In-lesson visual preview only | Small change, but review progress and requested corrections are not recorded. |
| **Structured review in Lesson Author** | More local tooling, but every figure has a durable approval state, notes, and a digest that makes approval stale after a visual change. |

The recommended structured workflow adds **Approve**, **Needs changes**, and a
notes field. It saves review progress in the repository. It also filters the
lesson list to the illustrations that still need review.

Aedwon's decision, verbatim:

> "sounds good to me"

The completion gate now requires Aedwon's approval for the current visual
digest of each of the 52 new code figures, the four Commons assets, and the
three founder assets. Missing founder files remain visible and pending. A
changed figure or image invalidates its old approval automatically.

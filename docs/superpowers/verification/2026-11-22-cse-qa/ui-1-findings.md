### Study and Quiz tabs (phone): Quiz title sits 40 px lower than Study title (Aedwon finding 1)
- Severity: major
- Evidence: Quiz `lib/features/quiz/presentation/quiz_tab_screen.dart:160-165` titlePadding top `PantasSpace.s44`, inside `PantasPage` `lib/core/ui/components/pantas_page.dart:432` scroll top padding `s2`, so the title starts 46 px under the safe-area top. Study `lib/features/lesson/presentation/learn_tab_home.dart:299-304` page padding top `s6`, then `SearchFieldBar` -> `lib/core/ui/components/search_field.dart:266-292` draws the "Study" title with no top offset, so the title starts 6 px under the safe-area top. Boards `mobile/project/ST_Map.dc.html` and `mobile/project/QZ_Tab.dc.html` (also QZ_Free, DK_Study, DK_Quiz) both open with the same empty row (`padding:6px 20px 0; height:44px`) and then the 34 px title, so both titles start at y 50 and the next element 14 px below.
- Gap: board = both titles at y 50. Code = Study 6, Quiz 46 (computed). Study is 44 px too high, Quiz 4 px too high, so they differ by 40 px, with Quiz lower. Root cause: the Study comment at learn_tab_home.dart:297 ("title starts 6 px below the top") misreads the board's empty 44 px row as a status bar, and `docs/ui-audit.md:2567` ("Study title ... 6 px lower. Quiz tab title starts at y 50") fixed the two tabs to different numbers. Other tab titles use their own offsets too (Settings `settings_home_page.dart:251` = 2+18 = 20, board ST_Root has `padding:20px`, correct for that board), so the rule is per board, not shared. No shared "tab title top" token exists.
- Assumption to confirm on a device: I treat board y 0 as the top of the safe area (the boards have no status bar, and pushed-page boards put their 44 px back row at the same place). If a measured screenshot shows the Study title at 6 px and the Quiz title at 46 px under the status bar, the 40 px gap is confirmed regardless of which absolute value is right.
- Platforms: phone; Free and Pro. Wide web: the two h1 headings match vertically (`web_study_layout.dart:160-163` and `quiz_tab_screen.dart:253`) but Study's h1 has a 4 px side inset (`web_study_layout.dart:161`) that Quiz's does not, so the words "Study" and "Quiz" start 4 px apart horizontally.
- Fix idea: one shared tab-title offset (50 px under the safe area, per ST_Map and QZ_Tab) used by both phone tabs; drop the 4 px inset on one web heading. Also check Home and Progress tabs against their boards (not my scope).

- Severity: major (26 phone call sites at 52 px, 23 of them also full width)

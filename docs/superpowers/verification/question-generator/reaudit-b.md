## Coverage and limits

- All five enabled type files, their complete matrix rule and independent matrix solver, and the solver registry were read. Each list received the 11-variant manual sample recorded above and the exhaustive structural re-derivation. The three source lessons were read from prod content to check lesson and key-point fit. No competing equally simple listed answer was identified. There are 97 tasks after dropping all outline names, before geometric normalization; the full 100 signatures are distinct. No open finding.
- Sized ray union: the two ray directions stay constant down their columns; each row has one size and combines both input rays. Its distractors copy the middle size or omit one input ray, with true notes. No competing listed answer was identified. The committed list has 72 distinct ruleKeys and 100 signatures; 28 extra variants reuse an exact ruleKey with a different constant cosmetic outline, as permitted by the plan. Indexes 0 and 70 are one such pair. This is limited variety, not a correctness failure.
- Ray intersection: all stored keys implement the common-ray operation correctly, and the three mistake figures implement union, first-only exclusive selection, and second-only exclusive selection. The notes name plausible errors in that intended operation and contain no fixed direction that becomes false in another variant. However, the directional-selection counterexample makes the answer nonunique. Its 100 shown feature tasks remain distinct even after removing outlines. The Blocker remains open.
- Corner reflection: the rule and every key swap left and right, preserve dot height, and preserve row outline. Copying the example location, holding the start location, and copying the example outline are plausible errors; their notes correctly describe all 100 variants. The explanation avoids calling the positions geometric corners. No competing listed answer was identified. The 8 dot-task variety finding remains Minor.
- Corner step: the stored operation and every mistake position are computed correctly. Holding the start, doubling the move, or reversing direction are plausible errors when described as movement among four positions. The explanation and note instead call them corners on non-four-cornered outlines, so the Major remains open. The dot arrangement repetition and missing bottom-right keys remain Minor.
- All sampled stems use realistic exam wording. Explanations address only the keyed operation, and all notes contain a named mistake, a reason it may look right, and a check. Outside the reported corner wording, no false shape, fixed number, em dash, spelling, fraction, or text-to-figure mismatch was found. Generated compass shorthand such as `NE+SE` matches the stored angles.

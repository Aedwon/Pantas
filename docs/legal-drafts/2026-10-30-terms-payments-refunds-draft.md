## Clauses that favor Pantas, and the legal limit each stays inside

1. **Refund limits (R2, R3).** Only unactivated or unconsumed full payments. No partial refunds on request. Limit: do not deny remedies the Consumer Act (RA 7394) and the Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967) give for defective, wrongly billed, or undelivered goods and services. The text keeps refunds for non-delivery and billing error, and the "to the extent the law gives you" lines keep statutory rights.
2. **Exam result and outage disclaimers (Terms 7).** "To the extent permitted by law." Limit: a disclaimer cannot cancel mandatory consumer protections. A consumer-protection warranty cannot be waived in advance.
3. **Liability cap at 12 months of payments (Terms 8).** Limit: Civil Code Arts. 1170 to 1172 and 2201 mean liability for fraud, bad faith, and gross negligence cannot be waived in advance, and courts may reduce unfair caps. The text carves these out.
4. **Suspension for abuse or chargeback fraud (Terms 6, P7).** Limit: only for a good faith belief of a breach. We refund prepaid time if we end the account for another reason. No forfeiture clause that a court would call unconscionable.
5. **Right to change prices and features (P3, Terms 5).** Limit: future purchases only. Paid time and passes are honored. Material Terms changes get 30 days' notice, as in your existing draft.
6. **Chargeback pause, with restore on a win (P7).** Limit: consistent with how the system behaves (restore at original expiry, unless refunded or revoked). We should not keep the money and the access removal both if the dispute fails.
7. **Governing law and venue (Terms 10).** Limit: venue stipulation is allowed in writing under the Rules of Court, but it cannot block a consumer complaint to DTI or the NPC. The text keeps that.
8. **Duplicate pass refunded; Monthly or Yearly adds time (P5).** Matches your 26 October ruling. Limit: a second period not yet started is refundable under R2, so the text does not keep money for service not delivered.
9. **Data Privacy Act.** The Privacy pointer does not waive RA 10173 rights. The trial code kept after deletion is disclosed in the privacy policy.

## Who this is for

This notice is for teens ages 13 to 17 and for their parents or guardians. Pantas is for people aged 13 and older. It is not available to anyone under 13.

If you are 18 or older, read the Privacy Policy instead.

## Why we ask your age

Your device uses the age you enter to check whether you can use Pantas and whether a parent or legal guardian must confirm. Pantas keeps the number in memory during setup and does not send it to our servers. Your device saves your age group and the version of the age check notice, or a block if you enter an age below 13. If you enter an age below 13, the app blocks setup on this device. If you entered the wrong age, email support@pantas.study. Continue runs the check.

Choosing Continue means you agree to this check. You may go back instead. Pantas requires this check before you can use it. For a teen, Continue shows that you read this notice. It is not the legal basis for the check. "Why we may use your data" gives the basis.

If you send a guardian request, our servers record that your account is in the under-18 group before anyone answers. We also record the age check notice version when your app sends it. We use this record to route the request and to apply the rule that an adult must confirm web purchases for an under-18 account. We use the request record to send and check the request.

If the request expires or your guardian declines, we remove the request's contact details, status and age check notice version during scheduled cleanup. We keep the under-18 group until your account is updated to 18 or older, or your account is deleted. Separate guardian-answer records and guardian page links follow the periods under "How long we keep data."

When you turn 18, open Settings, then Account, and choose "I'm 18 now." Enter your current age and choose Continue. This updates the age you declared. If you are 18 or older and the update succeeds, our servers record that you are 18 or older, your guardian's controls end and the guardian page links stop working.

## What we collect

• Account: an account ID, which Pantas creates even before you sign in. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign in with an email code, we receive your email address.
• Study records: your study settings, answers, results, study times, bookmarked lessons and flagged questions.
• Guardian request: your guardian's name and email address. We send them to our email provider to deliver the request. Our request database keeps the request status, its dates, a partly hidden version of the email address, and protected codes made from the email address and the link. Your device keeps the full name and email address so you can resend the request. Your device deletes them once it learns the guardian has confirmed.
• Guardian answer record: your guardian's answer, when it happened, which versions of our notices and terms were shown, and your account ID. It holds no name or email address. We add a line each time your guardian changes a permission or withdraws it.
• Payments: if someone buys Pro for your account, we keep the payment reference, product, amount, payment status, refunds and access dates, linked to your account. For web purchases, PayMongo handles card payments. For QR Ph, you approve payment in your bank or wallet app. We do not see or keep your card or wallet details. For every web purchase, we also record the declaration the buyer ticks at checkout, with the date, time and Terms version. For web purchases, we record the buyer's declaration that they're your parent or legal guardian, 18 or older, authorized to use the payment method, and agree to the purchase and the Terms and refund policy.
• Free trial: protected codes made from your verified sign-in details, so we can check whether a trial was already used. These codes are pseudonymous data, not anonymous data.
• Share cards: where public sharing is available, if you choose to share a Wrapped card, we keep the card image and a public link to it. Anyone with the link can see the card. You can revoke the link.
• Refund requests: the emails you or your guardian send us about a refund, and, if we refund a QR Ph payment by transfer, the bank account details given to us so we can send the money.
• Question reports: if you report a problem with a question, we keep the message you wrote (up to 1,000 characters), the question, the app build, the time we receive it and your account ID, plus the exam and question version when the app sends them. We also keep counts of your reports, to limit misuse. Your device may save an unsent report and try again later.
• Messages: emails you or your guardian send to support@pantas.study or dpo@pantas.study.
• Technical records: security and account records, such as sign-ins, payment checks and account deletions. When your browser or phone reaches our servers, our hosting and security providers see its network address and basic request details.

## How Pantas uses your answers

Software uses your answers and study timing to choose review dates and show study guidance. It also gives you a study-style label, such as the ones on share cards. These suggestions affect what Pantas recommends next. They do not decide any official exam eligibility or result.

## Optional usage sharing

Sharing starts off. Your guardian must allow it before it can turn on. After that, you can turn it off in Settings. Your guardian can also withdraw that permission on the guardian page.

If it is on, reports include the screens and features you use, your study sessions, question IDs, the answer you chose, and whether it was correct. Account-linked analytics reports are linked to your account ID. Separate visit logs use a random visit ID and no account ID. The phone app can also send error reports.

Leaving it off does not stop you from using Pantas. Your guardian can also email dpo@pantas.study about reports already collected.

## Speed and error reports

To find slow screens and errors, and to learn which screens are used, the web app sends short technical reports. They include the app version, whether this is a first visit, a return visit or the first visit after an update, your browser family, your screen-size group, loading times, and error messages, counts and locations in our code. After the app shows you a notice about it on this device, reports from your later visits also include screen counts: for each screen you open, its name, how many times you opened it in that visit, and whether it was the first or last screen of the visit. Screen counts contain no account ID and show no order of screens beyond first and last. We filter common identifiers, such as email addresses, before a report is sent. Error text may still contain personal information. We do not deliberately include your answers or lesson content.

Reports have no dedicated account ID or IP address field. Sending a report uses your sign-in session, including a guest session, to check the request and to limit how many reports are sent. We exclude this function's request logs from our default log storage. Google processes connection information to deliver requests.

We keep the stored report logs for 30 days. These reports are on by default and are separate from optional usage sharing. The switch in Settings, then About, stops future reports from this browser or device. Repeat this on other devices. Email dpo@pantas.study for help, or for requests about reports already collected.

## Why a guardian confirms

If you are in the under-18 group, Pantas asks a parent or guardian to read the terms and confirm before you continue.

Opening the email link confirms nothing. The adult must tick the agreement box and choose Confirm. They can choose "I Don't Agree" instead.

The response link expires after seven days and accepts one answer. A new request replaces the old link. The email and the confirmation screen may each give a separate link to the guardian page, where your guardian can manage permission. A guardian page link works until you confirm you are 18, or until the account is deleted.

Anyone holding these links can use them to respond or manage permission. Keep them private. The links do not independently prove someone's identity or authority. Contact us if a link is lost, shared or misused.

## What your guardian can do

Your guardian can confirm or decline the request. Confirming does not give them a screen showing your results.

After confirming, your guardian can use the guardian page to:

• allow or withdraw optional usage sharing. It starts off.
• withdraw permission. This closes your account and deletes it, as the section on withdrawing explains.

Your guardian can also email our Data Protection Officer about your data. We may ask for information we reasonably need to check who is asking before we share information or act on instructions. Guardian page links do not limit any other right your guardian has under the law.

## Where your data goes

Some records stay in your browser or on your phone. On the web, your answers and study activity also go to our servers, including on Free. We use Google Cloud to host our app data, including services in Singapore. Pro adds backup and syncing across devices.

We use these providers:

• Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): accounts, our servers and database, security checks that confirm requests come from the real Pantas app (Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA Enterprise), optional usage reports, and speed and error reports.
• Cloudflare: delivers and protects the web app and the Pantas website. It sees the network address and request details of each visit.
• Resend: guardian emails and sign-in codes.
• PayMongo: web payments. PayMongo collects card or wallet details on its checkout page, under its own terms and privacy policy. For web purchases, we give PayMongo the account ID, product and order reference, and we verify payments, refunds and disputes with it.
• RevenueCat and the app store you use: app-store purchases and the Pro access they give. A purchase through an app store follows the store's billing terms.
• Your bank or e-wallet, if we send a refund by transfer.

We use service providers to host, secure, measure and operate Pantas. Some providers process data outside the Philippines. We remain accountable for information we transfer, and we require comparable protection from providers that process it for us. Payment providers, banks and app stores also handle some data under their own notices. We may also share data when the law requires it, or when we need it to deal with fraud or a legal claim. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

## Your rights

You have the right to be informed about how your data is used, including its sources and who receives it, and about any decision made only by automated means. You can ask for a copy of your data, including in a common electronic format where the law allows. The app cannot export data yet, so email dpo@pantas.study. You can object to how it is used, including profiling, ask us to fix wrong details, and ask us to stop using, block or delete data, when the law allows. You can ask for compensation where the law provides it. You can complain directly to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Your guardian can use these rights for you while you are under 18. You can also ask Pantas for help yourself at dpo@pantas.study. Send requests to dpo@pantas.study. We respond as the law requires. We may ask for information we reasonably need to check who you are. If we cannot fully act on a request, we explain why.

## Withdrawing permission or deleting data

Your guardian can withdraw permission for you to use Pantas at any time. It is free. There are two ways:

• Open the guardian page link and choose "Withdraw permission."
• Email dpo@pantas.study with a message such as "I withdraw my permission for my child to use Pantas." Send it from the email address used for confirmation if you can, and include the account ID or sign-in email if you know it.

When permission is withdrawn, we disable the account, revoke its sign-ins, and start deletion in the same way as account deletion. Deletion can take time to finish. We keep the guardian answer record and the record of the withdrawal, for the reasons in the next section. Pantas cannot work without a guardian's permission for study records, so the account cannot continue.

Withdrawing does not undo use of data that was lawful before. It does not cancel an app store subscription. It is not a refund request. Refunds follow the Terms.

You can delete your account yourself. Open Settings, then Account, then the delete option. For an account signed in with Google or an email code, this deletes your study records, question reports and the payment records tied to your account from our servers and clears Pantas data on the device you use. You may need to sign in again first. For an account that is not signed in, it clears data on your device only. To remove server records of such an account, email dpo@pantas.study.

When account deletion finishes, we remove the app's account-linked study records, question reports and the main payment records tied to the account. It is not a refund request. Some records may remain, for the purposes and periods in the next section: trial codes, which we cannot link to you without our secret key; guardian-answer records; records that prevent a deleted account from reopening, and security records; limited records keyed to a payment reference, such as refund, dispute and payment-event records; the business's tax books and receipts, kept outside the app, which can include a payment reference and amount; and records PayMongo and the bank keep under their own rules. Offline copies may remain until the device reconnects or you clear them. Backup copies expire on the schedule of the service that holds them.

## How long we keep data

• Study records and settings: while your account exists. We normally delete an inactive account after about two years without a sign-in or session refresh. We delete it sooner if you delete it or your guardian withdraws permission. We identify inactivity from server-observed sign-in and session activity. Open Pantas while connected to keep the account active. If the account has a verified email address, we send a warning and allow at least 30 days before deletion. An account with active paid access does not count as inactive. Some abandoned guest accounts without an email address are removed sooner, without a warning.
• Guardian requests and links: the response link expires after seven days and accepts one answer. Our hourly cleanup removes expired response links. It removes used, declined or replaced response links seven days after that event.

For an unanswered request, we remove the request's contact details, status and age check notice version during scheduled cleanup after the request expires. For a declined request, we remove them during scheduled cleanup seven days after the answer. Your under-18 group remains until your account is updated to 18 or older, or your account is deleted.

For a confirmed request, we remove contact details during scheduled cleanup seven days after confirmation. We keep the confirmation status, date and sharing choice while your account exists. We also keep the age check notice version if your app sent it. Guardian page links remain until the account is deleted or the links are revoked. Separate guardian-answer evidence follows the period below.
• Guardian evidence: we keep limited confirmation and purchase-declaration evidence while the account exists. This is the guardian answer record, the withdrawal record, and the purchase declaration. When an account is deleted, we copy the guardian's purchase declaration (date, time, Terms version, payment reference and product) into this evidence. After deletion, our cleanup normally removes it about ten years after the latest recorded guardian response or purchase declaration, or uses the deletion date if neither date is available. We chose this period because a contract made by someone under 18 can be challenged until four years after that person turns 18. [Legal-hold exception: state one only if the cleanup can honor it.]
• Free-trial codes: we keep a protected code that lets us check whether the same verified sign-in has already used a trial. The code remains after account deletion. It currently has no scheduled expiry. You or your guardian may object to this at dpo@pantas.study.
• Account payment records in our app (the payment reference, plan dates, refund entries and checkout declarations tied to your account): while your account exists. We delete them when the account is deleted. For an under-18 account, we first copy the guardian's purchase declaration into the guardian evidence above, which stays for the period stated there.
• Tax books, receipts and refund records: five years, starting the day after the filing deadline of the related tax return, or on the actual filing date if the return was filed late, for the tax year of the last entry in the books. The business keeps these outside the app. They are kept even if the account is deleted, because the law requires them. Records may stay longer while a tax matter is unresolved.
• Database security records: we normally clear database audit logs after 730 days, with some delay for scheduled cleanup. We keep the minimum closure record needed to prevent a deleted account from reopening.
• Optional usage reports: Google Analytics event records are kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account [verified period, currently set to 14 months; confirm in the console]. Google begins removing phone error report data after 90 days.
• Speed and error report logs from the web app: 30 days.
• Share cards: where public sharing is available, our share record is scheduled to expire after about 89 days and may remain until cleanup even if you revoke the link sooner. People who received a copy may still have it. We also delete the card when the account is deleted.
• Question reports: [Period to publish. Sol High would defend up to 90 days from receipt, sooner when the review ends. The cleanup does not exist yet, see README.] We delete them when the account is deleted, including when we delete an inactive account or your guardian withdraws permission.

## Why we may use your data

• Age check: your device uses your age without sending it to our servers and keeps only your age group and the version of the age check notice, or a block if you are under 13. We do not rely on a teen's own agreement as the legal basis. The National Privacy Commission has said a minor cannot give valid consent alone (Advisory Opinion No. 2017-049).
• Under-18 group record and guardian evidence: if you send a guardian request, we record that your account is in the under-18 group before anyone answers. We use this record to route the request and to apply the adult purchase requirement. We rely on Section 13(f) where sensitive information is necessary to establish or defend a claim about capacity or authorization, such as whether you can legally agree to the Terms or whether your parent or legal guardian allowed your account or purchase. We rely on this basis before your guardian answers, including if they decline or do not answer. We use Section 12(f) for the ordinary contact information needed to send and protect the request. If your parent or legal guardian gives permission, we use it for the uses they approve from then on (Section 13(a)).
• Study records: we ask your parent or legal guardian to give permission for us to use your answers, results and study records to provide study features (Section 13(a)). Answers and results can relate to education.
• Account and contact data, and payments: we use the buyer's payment details to complete the purchase and resolve payment problems. We use ordinary account and contact data as needed to provide the service requested, answer requests and prevent fraud (Sections 12(b) and 12(f)). Where we rely on legitimate interests, we use only the data needed for those purposes.
• Optional usage sharing: your guardian's separate permission. It can be withdrawn at any time.
• Share cards: if you choose to publish a share card where that feature is available, we use your separate permission to publish it.
• Speed and error reports, security records, trial codes, and a guardian request's contact and request details: our legitimate interest in keeping Pantas working, safe and fair, in learning which screens are used so we can improve them, and in stopping repeat free trials (Section 12(f)). We keep each to what we need.
• Tax books, receipts and refund records: the law requires them (Section 12(c)). We also keep them to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (Sections 12(f) and 13(f)).
• Messages to us: our legitimate interest in answering you or your guardian, handling requests and resolving claims (Section 12(f)).
• Question reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing mistakes in questions, and in using report counts to limit misuse (Section 12(f)). We do not use reports to profile you.
• Checkout declarations: we save what the buyer agreed to, when, and the Terms version, to complete the order (Section 12(b)) and handle disputes (Section 12(f)). Where the record states the buyer's age, we rely on Section 13(f): it is necessary to establish or defend a claim about capacity or authority.

## Keeping data safe

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures to protect your data. When the law requires notice of a data breach, we notify affected people and the National Privacy Commission within the required time. For a teen, we may notify their legal representative as the rules allow.

## Changes to this notice

Before a change to what we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it or who receives it, we tell affected users in the app, and by email if we have an address. For a teen account, we also email the guardian when we still hold a way to reach them. We do not keep the guardian's contact details after confirmation. The change takes effect after that notice. If a change needs new permission, we ask before applying it. Changing the version date alone is not notice or permission.

## Payments for minors

If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian with authority to act for you must buy in their own name and use a payment method they are allowed to use. Every web purchase has a tick box beside the Pay button. When a guardian has confirmed your account, it reads: "I'm 18+ and buying for this student as their parent or legal guardian. I'm authorized to use the payment method and accept the Terms and refund policy." "Terms and refund policy" links to the Terms. For every web purchase, we record the tick with the date, time and Terms version when checkout starts. We may ask for proof of authority before or after payment.

Confirming your account is not a purchase. A refund request follows the Terms. The parent or guardian who bought should make it. They may write from the email address they paid with.

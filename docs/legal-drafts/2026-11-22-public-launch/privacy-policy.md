## Why we ask your age

When you set up Pantas, the age screen asks you to enter your correct current age in full years and explains the check. Your device uses the age you enter to check whether you can use Pantas and whether a parent or legal guardian must confirm. Pantas keeps the number in memory during setup and does not send it to our servers. Your device saves your age group and the version of the age check notice, or a block if you enter an age below 13. If you enter an age below 13, the app blocks setup on this device. If you entered the wrong age, email support@pantas.study.

Choosing Continue means you agree to this check. You may go back instead. Pantas requires this check before you can use it.

## What we collect

- Account: an account ID, which Pantas creates even before you sign in. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign in with an email code, we receive your email address.
- Study records: your study settings, answers, results, study times, bookmarked lessons and flagged questions.
- Payments: if you buy Pro, we keep the payment reference, product, amount, payment status, refunds and access dates, linked to your account. For web purchases, PayMongo handles card payments. For QR Ph, you approve payment in your bank or wallet app. We do not see or keep your card or wallet details. For every web purchase, we also record the declaration the buyer ticks at checkout (that they are 18 or older, or the student's parent or legal guardian buying for the student, are authorized to use the payment method, and accept the Terms and refund policy), with the date, time, Terms version and wording version, to complete the order and handle disputes.
- Refund requests: the emails you send us about a refund, and, if we refund a QR Ph payment by transfer, the bank account details you give us so we can send the money.
- Free trial: protected codes made from your verified sign-in details, so we can check whether a trial was already used. These codes are pseudonymous data, not anonymous data.
- Share cards: where public sharing is available, if you choose to share a Wrapped card, we keep the card image and a public link to it. Anyone with the link can see the card. You can revoke the link.
- Question reports: if you report a problem with a question, we keep the message you wrote (up to 1,000 characters), the question, the app build, the time we receive it and your account ID, plus the exam and question version when the app sends them. We also keep counts of your reports, to limit misuse. Your device may save an unsent report and try again later.
- Messages: emails you send to support@pantas.study or dpo@pantas.study.
- Technical records: security and account records, such as sign-ins, payment checks and account deletions. When your browser or phone reaches our servers, our hosting and security providers see its network address and basic request details.

## How Pantas uses your answers

Software uses your answers and study timing to choose review dates and show study guidance. It also gives you a study-style label, such as the ones on share cards. These suggestions affect what Pantas recommends next. They do not decide any official exam eligibility or result.

## Optional usage sharing

Optional usage sharing helps us improve Pantas. It starts off. You choose whether to turn it on. It is a separate choice from setting up your account, and leaving it off does not stop you from using Pantas.

If you turn it on, reports include the screens and features you use, your study sessions, question IDs, the answer you chose, and whether it was correct. Account-linked analytics reports are linked to your account ID. Separate visit logs use a random visit ID and no account ID. The phone app can also send error reports.

To turn it off, open Settings, then About, and use "Help improve Pantas by sharing usage data." You can also email dpo@pantas.study about reports already collected.

## Speed and error reports

To find slow screens and errors, and to learn which screens are used, the web app sends short technical reports. They include the app version, whether this is a first visit, a return visit or the first visit after an update, your browser family, your screen-size group, loading times, and error messages, counts and locations in our code. After the app shows you a notice about it on this device, reports from your later visits also include screen counts: for each screen you open, its name, how many times you opened it in that visit, and whether it was the first or last screen of the visit. Screen counts contain no account ID and show no order of screens beyond first and last. We filter common identifiers, such as email addresses, before a report is sent. Error text may still contain personal information. We do not deliberately include your answers or lesson content.

Reports have no dedicated account ID or IP address field. Sending a report uses your sign-in session, including a guest session, to check the request and to limit how many reports are sent. We exclude this function's request logs from our default log storage. Google processes connection information to deliver requests.

We keep the stored report logs for 30 days. These reports are on by default and are separate from optional usage sharing. The switch in Settings, then About, stops future reports from this browser or device. Repeat this on other devices. Email dpo@pantas.study for help, or for requests about reports already collected.

## Where your data goes

Some records stay in your browser or on your phone. On the web, your answers and study activity also go to our servers, including on Free. We use Google Cloud to host our app data, including services in Singapore. Pro adds backup and syncing across devices.

We use these providers:

- Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): accounts, our servers and database, security checks that confirm requests come from the real Pantas app (Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA Enterprise), optional usage reports, and speed and error reports.
- Cloudflare: delivers and protects the web app and the Pantas website. It sees the network address and request details of each visit.
- Resend: sign-in codes and emails we send you.
- PayMongo: web payments. PayMongo collects your card or wallet details on its checkout page, under its own terms and privacy policy. For web purchases, we give PayMongo the account ID, product and order reference, and we verify payments, refunds and disputes with it.
- RevenueCat and the app store you use: app-store purchases and the Pro access they give. A purchase through an app store follows the store's billing terms.
- Your bank or e-wallet, if we send you a refund by transfer.

We use service providers to host, secure, measure and operate Pantas. Some providers process data outside the Philippines. We remain accountable for information we transfer, and we require comparable protection from providers that process it for us. Payment providers, banks and app stores also handle some data under their own notices. We may also share data when the law requires it, or when we need it to deal with fraud or a legal claim.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

## Your rights

You have the right to be informed about how your data is used, including its sources and who receives it, and about any decision made only by automated means. You can ask for a copy of your data, including in a common electronic format where the law allows. The app cannot export your data yet, so email dpo@pantas.study. You can object to how it is used, including profiling, ask us to fix wrong details, and ask us to stop using, block or delete data, when the law allows. You can ask for compensation where the law provides it. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Send requests to dpo@pantas.study. We respond as the law requires. We may ask for information we reasonably need to check who you are. If we cannot fully act on a request, we explain why.

## Withdrawing permission or deleting data

You can withdraw a permission you gave, such as optional usage sharing, at any time. Withdrawing is free. It does not undo use of data that was lawful before you withdrew. Withdrawal closes access and starts deletion where the withdrawn permission is needed to run the account. Deletion can take time to finish.

To delete your account, open Settings, then Account, then the delete option. For an account signed in with Google or an email code, this deletes your study records, question reports and the payment records tied to your account from our servers and clears Pantas data on the device you use. You may need to sign in again first. For an account that is not signed in, it clears data on your device only. To remove server records of such an account, email dpo@pantas.study.

Deleting your account does not cancel an app store subscription and is not a refund request. These records may remain after deletion, for the purposes and periods in the next section:

- trial codes, which we cannot link to you without our secret key;
- limited guardian evidence: the guardian's answers, when they gave or took back permission for optional usage sharing, and any withdrawal of permission to use Pantas, and, if a guardian confirmed the account, the date and time the student said they were 18 or older, and, for a purchase a parent or legal guardian declared, the date, time, Terms version, wording version, payment reference and product. We do not copy declarations for a buyer's own account;
- records that prevent a deleted account from reopening, and security records;
- limited records keyed to a payment reference, such as refund, dispute and payment-event records, which we use to verify past transactions;
- the business's tax books and receipts, kept outside the app, which can include your payment reference and amount; and
- records PayMongo and your bank keep under their own rules.

Offline copies may remain until the device reconnects or you clear them. Backup copies expire on the schedule of the service that holds them.

## How long we keep data

- Study records and settings: while your account exists. We normally delete an inactive account after about two years without a sign-in or session refresh. We identify inactivity from server-observed sign-in and session activity. Open Pantas while connected to keep the account active. If the account has a verified email address, we send a warning and allow at least 30 days before deletion. An account with active paid access does not count as inactive. Some abandoned guest accounts without an email address are removed sooner, without a warning.
- Account payment records in our app (the payment reference, plan dates, refund entries and checkout declarations tied to your account): while your account exists. We delete them when you delete the account. Before deletion, we copy limited details from a checkout declaration where the buyer said they are a parent or legal guardian buying for an under-18 student. We copy the date, time, Terms version, wording version, payment reference and product into the guardian evidence that the Notice for teens describes. That evidence stays for the periods stated there. We do not copy declarations for a buyer's own account.
- Tax books, receipts and refund records: five years, starting the day after the filing deadline of the related tax return, or on the actual filing date if the return was filed late, for the tax year of the last entry in the books. The business keeps these outside the app. They are kept even if you delete your account, because the law requires them. Records may stay longer while a tax matter is unresolved.
- Free-trial codes: we keep a protected code that lets us check whether the same verified sign-in has already used a trial. The code remains after account deletion. It currently has no scheduled expiry. You may object to this at dpo@pantas.study.
- Database security records: we normally clear database audit logs after 730 days, with some delay for scheduled cleanup. We keep the minimum closure record needed to prevent a deleted account from reopening.
- Optional usage reports: Google Analytics data about individual users and events expires after two months. Google deletes expired data in a monthly cleanup. Standard reports that combine data can stay available longer. Google begins removing phone error report data after 90 days.
- Speed and error report logs from the web app: 30 days.
- Share cards: where public sharing is available, our share record is scheduled to expire after about 89 days and may remain until cleanup even if you revoke the link sooner. People who received a copy may still have it. We also delete the card when your account is deleted.
- Question reports: we delete each report within 90 days of receipt. We delete reports sooner when you delete your account, including when we delete an inactive account.
- Messages to us, including refund requests: kept as needed to handle the matter, meet legal duties or resolve a documented claim. We use refund destination details to verify and send the refund and keep the minimum record afterward. These limits cover emails to support@pantas.study or dpo@pantas.study and our replies. We delete emails about a payment, refund, purchase complaint or privacy request within two years after the matter closes. We delete other emails within 90 days after the matter closes. We count these periods from the date of the last email about the matter. We keep a short record of each refund we pay under the five-year tax records schedule above.

The schedules above apply to guardian evidence and question reports, with one exception. If a court or another authority with legal power to issue the order gives us a binding order to keep named records, we copy those records by hand and keep the copy for as long as the order requires. We never pause cleanup. We may keep other specific records longer when the law requires it or when we need them for a legal claim.

## Why we may use your data

- Age check: you agree to it at the age step. Your device uses your age without sending it to our servers and keeps only your age group and the version of the age check notice, or a block if you are under 13. For an adult, your agreement is the basis (Data Privacy Act, Section 13(a)). If you turn out to be under 18, the Notice for teens gives the basis.
- Study records: we ask for your permission to use your answers, results and study records to provide study features (Section 13(a)). You give it at setup, in the same step where you accept the Terms and Privacy Policy. Answers and results can relate to education.
- Question reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing mistakes in questions, and in using report counts to limit misuse (Section 12(f)). We do not use reports to profile you.
- Account and contact data, and payments: we use the buyer's payment details to complete the purchase and resolve payment problems. We use ordinary account and contact data as needed to provide the service you asked for, answer requests and prevent fraud (Sections 12(b) and 12(f)). Where we rely on legitimate interests, we use only the data needed for those purposes.
- Optional usage sharing: your separate permission (Sections 12(a) and 13(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
- Share cards: if you choose to publish a share card where that feature is available, we use your separate permission to publish it.
- Speed and error reports, security records and trial codes: our legitimate interest in keeping Pantas working, safe and fair, in learning which screens are used so we can improve them, and in stopping repeat free trials (Section 12(f)). We keep each to what we need.
- Tax books, receipts and refund records: the law requires them (Section 12(c)). We also keep them to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (Sections 12(f) and 13(f)).
- Messages to us: our legitimate interest in answering you, handling requests and resolving claims (Section 12(f)).
- Checkout declarations: we save what the buyer agreed to, when, and the Terms version, to complete the order (Section 12(b)) and handle disputes (Section 12(f)). Where the record states the buyer's age, we rely on Section 13(f): it is necessary to establish or defend a claim about capacity or authority.

## Keeping data safe

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures to protect your data. When the law requires notice of a data breach, we notify affected people and the National Privacy Commission within the required time.

## Changes to this policy

Before a change to what we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it or who receives it, we tell affected users in the app, and by email if we have an address. The change takes effect after that notice. If a change needs new permission, we ask before applying it. Changing the version date alone is not notice or permission.

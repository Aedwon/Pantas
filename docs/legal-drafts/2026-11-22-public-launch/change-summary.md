# What changes in the public-launch legal texts

For Aedwon's check. Compared with the texts live in the app today (version 2026-11-12). Nothing here is published. No lawyer has reviewed it. Codex Sol High will critique it before you sign off.

## What you need to do

Supply the facts in the README list: registered business name, DTI number, BIR details, address choice, business phone, publication date. Then check the list below.

## Terms of Service

- **Reorganized, same rules.** Payments, the 7-day Monthly trial, refunds and bank disputes now have their own sections. The wording is the live wording unless listed here.
- **Refunds now name QR Ph.** PayMongo cannot refund QR Ph. We refund by bank or e-wallet transfer to an account in the buyer's name.
- **Two refund cases added, to match what the system already does:** a Season Pass paid for after its exam sitting passed, and a billing error.
- **Same limits as before:** no refund for change of mind, no partial refund for unused time, 30 days to ask, legal rights untouched.
- **New protections for Pantas:** the user covers loss from their own breach of the Terms; delays caused by providers or outages are not our fault; acceptance is electronic; Pro access has no cash value.
- **Changes to the Terms:** 14 days notice in the app, as before. Changes to what we collect also go by email.
- **Exam dates:** if an exam body moves a sitting, we review the pass in good faith. No promise beyond that.
- **Kept as is:** the liability cap (the greater of ₱1,000 or 12 months of payments), Norala court venue with consumer carve-outs, and the Civil Service Commission disclaimer.

## Privacy Policy

- **Two providers added:** Cloudflare (serves the web app and sees network addresses) and Google's App Check and reCAPTCHA security checks.
- **Payments and refunds:** says what we keep, that refund requests may include bank details, and that card details stay with PayMongo.
- **Deleting your account and payments (M10):** deletion erases the payment records we hold in the app. The five-year tax record lives in the business's books and receipts outside the app, and survives deletion. The live text implied the app kept them. It does not.
- **Age check:** the live text said the click gave "specific permission". The screen says "agree". The text now matches the screen.
- **Data export:** says the app cannot export yet, so users email us.
- **Changes:** we email users before any change to what we collect, why, how long, or who gets it. This is your Nov 15 rule 9.
- **Left out on purpose:** screen counts (switched off), the 730-day trial code expiry (not built), under-13 accounts (separate work).

## Notice for teens

- **Same changes as the Privacy Policy**, plus guardian emails for any change.
- **Age check legal basis, rewritten.** The NPC says a minor cannot give valid consent alone (Opinion 2017-049). So the notice no longer rests the age check on the teen's click. It rests on our need to apply the minimum age and defend legal claims about who could agree to the Terms (Section 13(f)), plus the guardian's permission once given. The age check still runs on the device only, and nothing is saved on our servers.
- **Refunds for under-18 accounts** go through the parent or guardian who bought.
- **Unchanged:** guardian confirmation, withdrawal, retention of guardian evidence for ten years, and the 24-month inactivity deletion.

## Things you should know

- **Version stamps do not make students accept again.** The checklist assumed they would. The email and in-app notice are the only way existing students hear of the change.
- **The email needs sending.** It follows your self-test and wording rules.
- **Weakest point:** the age check basis for teens. It is arguable, not settled. You accepted this risk on Nov 11 (option A) and chose no lawyer on Nov 9.
- **Two promises are yours to keep:** a refund reply within 5 business days, and five years of tax books and receipts outside the app.

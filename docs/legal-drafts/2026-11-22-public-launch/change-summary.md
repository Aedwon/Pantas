## Terms of Service

- **Seller details:** the Terms name you as the operator under the registered trade name, with a full place of business (bracket), phone and email. "Poblacion, Norala" alone is not a place of business.
- **Price line:** the checkout price is the total, including taxes and seller charges. The tax wording stays in brackets until BIR details are known.
- **Invoice:** the sentence that Pantas issues a sales invoice is in brackets until the workflow exists (D3).
- **Refunds start with the law.** The first paragraph says legal remedies for a defective or undelivered service apply and are not limited by our policy or the 30-day deadline.
- **Refund limits kept:** no change of mind, no change of exam, no exam result, no unused time, 30 days to ask.
- **Refund cases tightened:** a duplicate charge is refunded only if confirmed, and a separate purchase that adds time follows the normal rules. "Fix it or refund" no longer sounds like our choice.
- **QR Ph refunds:** the original route first, otherwise a bank transfer to the verified payer. The "PayMongo cannot refund these" and e-wallet promise are gone.
- **Removed promises:** "usually at once", "you were not charged" if PayMongo shows a failure, "a legal refund does not shorten access", and "the access returns" after every bank dispute.
- **Complaints:** a request no longer needs the account email or a subject line. A covered purchase complaint unresolved after seven calendar days counts as exhausted internally. A second review is optional and resets nothing.
- **Under-18 purchases:** a parent or guardian buys in their own name with a payment method they may use. We may ask for proof, and may cancel and refund the verified payer if the purchase lacked authority.
- **Acceptance:** you accept electronically. We ask for acceptance of a change that makes your position worse. Small changes apply by continued use. The 14-day promise stays.
- **Liability cap:** limited to good-faith contract damages the law lets us limit. The ₱1,000 floor and 12-month formula stay.
- **Outages:** excuse us only for events we could not foresee or avoid, and only to that extent. Legal remedies still apply.
- **Venue:** consumer claims may go to any court the law allows. Norala applies only to non-consumer disputes.
- **Account closure for misuse:** we keep payment for access already given and may deduct loss we can prove. The rest is refunded. This replaces automatic forfeiture.
- **Your breach:** we recover loss you caused that we can prove.
- **Bank disputes:** we pause access only when the payment provider reports a formal dispute. A won dispute restores the remaining time.
- **Pro access:** personal and not exchangeable for cash. Refund rights stay.
- **Feature changes:** we may change features and keep the material benefits of the paid plan. If we cannot, legal remedies apply. This replaces the promise to refund the unused part whenever a Pro feature ends.
- **Content ownership:** now "protected by intellectual property laws", with third-party material and legal exceptions recognized.
- **Exam dates:** a pass may follow a moved sitting. The Pro screen shows the sitting end date before payment.
- **Deleting an account:** the Terms separate deleting a signed-in account from clearing guest data.
- **Trial rule:** checked against account history and protected trial codes, not all history.
- **Payment methods:** card and QR Ph are "may include" until live methods are confirmed.

## Privacy Policy

- **Who runs Pantas:** you, trading under the registered name, with a full address and a postal route to the Data Protection Officer.
- **Age check:** rewritten to what the code does. The age stays in memory during setup and is never sent to servers. The device keeps the age group, the notice version, or a block under 13. The Continue sentence stays for adults.
- **Study records permission:** now "we ask for your permission", not a claim that setup alone is consent.
- **Screen counts added.** They are on in production. The first draft said off. The wording is the approved wording, and the purpose is added to the legitimate-interest line.
- **Usage sharing:** only some reports carry your account ID. Visit logs use a random visit ID.
- **Providers:** the claim that every provider "acts for us" is gone. Payment providers, banks and stores have their own notices. We name what PayMongo gets (account ID, product, order reference).
- **Deletion:** the app removes the main payment records. Some payment-keyed records (refunds, disputes, events), the reopening block and security records can stay. The old list was too short.
- **Five-year tax books:** counted from the day after the filing deadline, or the filing date if late.
- **Trial codes:** called pseudonymous, with an honest "no scheduled expiry" and an objection route.
- **Retention periods corrected:** inactive account deletion (about two years, warning only with a verified email), audit logs, Google Analytics (bracket), phone error data (90 days), share cards (about 89 days), and messages.
- **Removed promises:** the 15-day reply to privacy requests, the 180-day backup removal, and "we cannot run your account" on withdrawal.
- **Rights:** added sources and recipients, automated decisions, and the right to object to profiling.
- **Legal bases:** each purpose now has one. Messages get legitimate interest. Share cards get their own permission.
- **Breach notice:** now "when the law requires notice, within the required time".
- **Changes:** we tell users in the app, and by email where we have an address. A new version date alone is not notice.
- **Other:** the Singapore line now says "including services in Singapore". Share cards are described as "where available". "We do not sell your data" stays, and you confirm it.

## Notice for teens

- **Same changes as the Privacy Policy**, plus the points below.
- **Age check:** same rewrite. The notice states that our servers record the under-18 group when a request is sent, before anyone answers, and keep it after a decline or no answer. This is the weakest point (D1). The basis is now Section 13(f) for claims about capacity or authorization, Section 12(f) for contact details, and the guardian's permission once given.
- **Guardian details:** we send the guardian's name and email to our email provider. Our database keeps a masked address and protected codes. The device keeps the full details.
- **Guardian links:** the response link accepts one answer and expires in seven days. The email and the confirmation screen can each give a separate manage link. The old "same link" line was wrong.
- **Guardian evidence:** the ten-year cleanup counts from the latest guardian response or purchase declaration. A legal hold is not promised.
- **Withdrawal:** we revoke sign-ins and start deletion. It can take time. Offline copies can remain.
- **Rights and complaints:** the teen can ask Pantas for help and complain to the NPC directly. Breach notice added, including the legal representative.
- **Purchases and guardian emails:** the purchase box wording matches the Terms. Change emails to the guardian go "when we still hold a way to reach them" (D5).

## Things you should know

- **Weakest point is still the age flag** (D1). The text is now honest about it. Only a flow change removes it.
- **Version stamps do not make students accept again** (D5). The email and in-app notice are the only way existing students hear of a change.
- **Promises that are yours to keep:** a refund reply in [5] business days, five years of tax books, invoices and receipts outside the app, and working support and privacy mailboxes.
- **Left out on purpose:** the critique's suggestion for a short first-email notice to guardians. It belongs in the guardian email, not these texts.

## Codex verdict (verbatim)

1. **CORRECTED.** The three Section 5 triggers are right and require registering all systems. Section 5(A) also covers automated decision-making or profiling “in all instances.” Section 7 gives 20 days from system commencement or the first data protection officer’s appointment taking effect. Section 6 says eligible non-registrants “shall submit a sworn declaration.” If exempt and skipping voluntary registration, file notarized Annex 1 promptly through the NPC portal. I found no initial filing deadline. [Circular 2022-04](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04-1.pdf) · [NPC filing instructions](https://privacy.gov.ph/npc-implements-registration-fees-and-charges-and-submission-of-sworn-declaration-and-undertaking-for-exemption-from-data-processing-system-registration/)

2. **CORRECTED.** RA 10173 §3(l) lists “age” and “education.” Account-linked age bands count. Identifiable practice scores and learning progress likely qualify as education data. The NPC opinion specifically addresses school records. Count distinct people, including children identifiable through guardian accounts. [RA 10173](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act-/) · [NPC opinion](https://www.privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Redacted-Advisory-Opinion-No.-2020-046.pdf)

3. **CORRECTED.** IRR §47 says processing that “is not occasional” triggers registration. Circular 2022-04 omits that trigger from §5 and overrides inconsistent rules, so regularity alone remains uncertain. Check now whether the age gate automatically rejects applicants or processes identifiable minors’ ages. Register by **December 18, 2026** if covered at launch. Annex 1 expressly flags minors, strongly supporting registration once child processing starts. The threshold is 1,000 people whose sensitive data is processed. Annex 1 filers must register within 20 days after exemption ceases. [IRR §47](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/IRR_RA-10173-as-amended.pdf) · [Annex 1](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04-Annex-1.pdf)

4. **CORRECTED.** The December 17 advisory says “PICs must incorporate Child Privacy Impact Assessments (CPIA) as part of their PIAs” before launch. Its definition includes under-18s and certain adults with disabilities or conditions. Complete the adult assessment now and the child assessment before opening child accounts. Keep assessments available for NPC inspection. Provide a child-friendly notice and private defaults. [Advisory 2024-03](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Advisory-2024.12.17-Guidelines-on-Child-Oriented-Transparency-w-SGD.pdf) · [Circular 2023-06](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NPC-Circular-Repeal-16-01-Signed.pdf)

5. **CORRECTED.** Circular 16-03 remains current. Within 72 hours of knowledge or reasonable belief, notify the NPC through its breach portal and affected people if sensitive or identity-fraud-enabling data was reasonably believed acquired without authorization and serious harm is a real risk. Notify both child and guardian. Log all incidents and file the 2026 annual report by **March 31, 2027** under the standing schedule. [NPC breach reporting](https://privacy.gov.ph/pips-and-pics/breach-reporting/) · [November 2026 enforcement statement](https://privacy.gov.ph/statement-of-the-national-privacy-commission-on-reported-data-leaks-involving-government-agencies/)

6. **CORRECTED.** Designate a data protection officer now, even if exempt. If registering, include the officer and any profiling, update system changes within 10 days, display the seal, and renew annually. Initial fees are ₱500–₱2,500 by coverage. The September 23 advisory reinforces existing duties. November Circular 2026-01 expressly covers minors and allows 120 days after effectivity to comply. Newspaper publication date is **UNVERIFIED**. Before collecting child data, document a §13 basis. On these facts, obtain and record specific prior consent from a verified parent or legal guardian. I found no special under-13 cutoff applicable to this app. [NPC registration FAQ](https://privacy.gov.ph/pips-and-pics/faqs/) · [Fees](https://privacy.gov.ph/PIPS-AND-PICS/REGISTER/) · [September advisory](https://privacy.gov.ph/public-advisory-npc-calls-on-digital-platforms-to-ensure-compliance/) · [Circular 2026-01](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/24-Nov-2026-Circular-Updated-Guidelines-on-the-Conduct-of-PIA-newspaper-pub.pdf) · [NPC child privacy FAQ](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/FAQs-Advisory-on-Guidelines-on-Child-Oriented-Transparency.pdf)

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are Filipino students and exam takers evaluating whether Pantas can help them prepare with more direction and less guesswork. Secondary visitors include parents, educators, and people assessing the product's credibility before recommending or paying for it.

## Product Purpose

The public website explains what Pantas is, shows how studying with it works, establishes trust, and gives visitors a clear next action. It should make the product understandable through real learning flows and product evidence instead of generic education marketing.

## Positioning

Pantas is positioned as a focused exam-preparation coach that helps a learner understand what to study next, learn material, practice, review mistakes, and see useful progress signals. The website should communicate guidance and adaptability without presenting the product as a generic AI assistant, a content library, or a gamified study app.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from search, social media, a referral, an app-store listing, or a direct recommendation. They may know very little about Pantas. The landing page must therefore work as a coherent narrative from first impression through product proof, trust, pricing/platform information when current, and the final CTA.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website must use real or production-like Pantas product artifacts when demonstrating features.
- Claims about exam coverage, pricing, availability, privacy, outcomes, or measured performance must come from current product truth. Do not invent or infer figures.
- The app icon, final logo system, final brand accent, and final website typeface are separate brand decisions and remain open until explicitly approved.
- The Flutter app's existing "Ink and Rule" visual identity is not automatically the marketing-site visual identity. The two may harmonize without being identical.
- Accessibility is part of the product quality bar. The website targets WCAG 2.2 AA and should preserve full meaning with keyboard navigation, zoom, assistive technology, and reduced motion.

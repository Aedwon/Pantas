# Brief: transcribe the PSHS NCE reviewer, Tests II to IV

You get 14 page images (attached in order): image pages 8 to 21 of the official DOST-PSHS *Reviewer for the National Competitive Examination* (2024). Image page 8 holds the end of Test I and the start of Test II. Image page 21 holds the answer keys.

Write `docs/content/pshs-nce/reviewer-transcript.md` in the repository. Append each item as you finish it; do not wait for the end.

For every item of "TEST II - VERBAL APTITUDE" (30 items), "TEST III - SCIENTIFIC ABILITY" (20 items) and "TEST IV - ABSTRACT REASONING" (20 items):

1. Heading `### II-<n>`, `### III-<n>` or `### IV-<n>`, and the image page and printed page.
2. The directions that apply to it, quoted once per group.
3. Stem and all five choices A to E, quoted exactly, errors kept. Quote passages in full once, before their first item.
4. Figures: describe each figure and each choice figure precisely enough that a reader could solve the item without the image (shapes, counts, positions, shading, rotation, arrows, labels, table values, graph axes and points).
5. The official key letter from the answer-key page.
6. Your own solution in one to three sentences, and whether you agree with the key. If the scan is too faint to be sure, say "illegible" and what is unsure. Never guess a figure detail.
7. Item type in a few words (our label, not PSHS's), and for science the subject area and the skill (recall, reading a table or graph, experiment design, reasoning from a passage).

Rules: quote, do not paraphrase. Do not claim anything about what the exam covers, its item counts or its weighting; the reviewer only shows samples. End the file with a summary table: item, type, key, agrees (yes, no, unsure).

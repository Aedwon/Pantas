# Approval: q-arithmetic-number-sense-ratio-and-proportion-0018

Approved by the CSE release lead on Nov 23 2026, after an independent re-solve.

The question asks which check disproves the claim that (2, 8) and (4, 6) are
inversely proportional. The products are 2 x 8 = 16 and 4 x 6 = 24. They
differ, so 2(8) != 4(6) disproves the claim. The keyed answer, `$2(8)\ne 4(6)$`,
is correct.

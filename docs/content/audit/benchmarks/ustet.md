# USTET (UST Entrance Test) — Tested Scope

Four areas plus an essay: English, Mathematics, Science, Mental Ability
(Abstract Reasoning).

## Mathematics
- Algebra and quantitative reasoning
- Arithmetic, geometry, word problems

## English
- Spelling, grammar, reading comprehension, verbal reasoning

## Science
- Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth Science concepts

## Mental Ability (Abstract Reasoning)
- Abstract reasoning, problem-solving, logical thinking
- Sequences (arithmetic/geometric), syllogisms, forming figures,
  missing components, pattern finding, analogy

## Essay
- Structured writing; clear expression (out of lesson scope, note only)

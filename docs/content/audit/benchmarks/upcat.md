# UPCAT — Tested Scope

Four subtests: Language Proficiency (English & Filipino), Reading
Comprehension (English & Filipino), Science, Mathematics. Right-minus-wrong
scoring has been used historically; verify per cycle.

## Mathematics
- Real number system, properties, order of operations
- Fractions/decimals/percent, ratio and proportion
- Sets and set notation
- Exponents, roots, radicals
- Algebraic expressions, factoring, polynomials
- Linear equations and inequalities (solving and graphing)
- Quadratic equations
- Systems of equations
- Functions and relations
- Word problems (age, motion, work, mixture, coin, digit)
- Geometry: angles, triangles, quadrilaterals/polygons, circles, measurement, coordinate geometry
- Statistics: central tendency, data/graph interpretation
- Trigonometry: light coverage (ratios, basic identities)

## Science
- Earth Science: layers of the earth, rocks/minerals, weather/atmosphere, plate tectonics, water cycle
- Astronomy: solar system, stars/heavenly bodies, moon phases/eclipses
- Biology: classification, evolution, ecosystems, genetics/heredity, human anatomy, cell
- Chemistry: matter/classification, atomic structure, periodic table trends, mole concept, reactions, acids/bases, light organic
- Physics: statics/dynamics, motion, laws of motion, work-energy-power, forces, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, electricity/magnetism, waves/sound, optics (Grade 11 mechanics-heavy)

## Language Proficiency
- Vocabulary, synonyms/antonyms, spelling, correct usage
- Grammar: parts of speech, subject-verb agreement, parallelism, pronoun agreement
- English and Filipino portions

## Reading Comprehension
- Varied text types under time pressure; main idea, inference, detail, tone/purpose

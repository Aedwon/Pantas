# ACET (Ateneo College Entrance Test) — Tested Scope

Tests aptitude across English, Mathematics, general knowledge, and
abstract reasoning. Aptitude-leaning: reasoning and reading weigh heavily
relative to raw content recall.

## Mathematics
- Arithmetic, algebra, geometry
- Word problems, quantitative reasoning

## English
- Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension

## Abstract Reasoning
- Sequences, analogy, pattern finding, figure logic

## General Knowledge / Reasoning
- Broad reasoning; less pure-science recall than UPCAT/DOST

## Notes
- (unverified) ACET has historically de-emphasized a dedicated science
  subtest relative to UPCAT; confirm the current cycle's structure.

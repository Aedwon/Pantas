# DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship Exam — Tested Scope

Two parts: Intellective and Non-Intellective. The audit concerns the
Intellective (academic) part. Distinctive vs university CETs: a dedicated
**Abstract / Non-Verbal Reasoning** section and **Scientific Ability**
that skews harder on physics and chemistry.

## General Mathematics
- Same algebra/geometry/statistics spine as UPCAT
- Reaches higher difficulty; quantitative reasoning under time

## Science (Scientific Ability)
- Biology, Chemistry, Physics — physics and chemistry weighted heavier
- Concept formation, logical reasoning, pattern recognition, objectivity of observation
- ~50 items, high difficulty ceiling

## English
- Vocabulary, grammar, reading comprehension

## Abstract / Non-Verbal Reasoning (distinctive)
- Pattern recognition, figure series/grouping/matrices
- Non-verbal sequences; rewards pattern skill, not subject knowledge

## Notes
- Scientific Ability rewards STEM-strand candidates; depth matters more
  than for the university CETs.
- (unverified) Exact item counts vary by cycle; treat counts as indicative.

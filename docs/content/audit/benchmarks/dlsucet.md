# DLSUCET / DCAT (De La Salle College Admission Test) — Tested Scope

Assesses aptitude in Mathematics, English, and Science, plus a personality
test and essay.

## Mathematics
- Arithmetic, algebra, geometry
- Word problems, quantitative reasoning

## English
- Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension

## Science
- Basic Biology, Chemistry, Physics concepts

## Essay / Personality
- Writing and self-assessment (out of lesson scope, note only)

## Notes
- (unverified) De La Salle's admission process and reliance on a single
  entrance test have shifted across recent cycles; verify whether DCAT is
  the current instrument before treating this as authoritative.

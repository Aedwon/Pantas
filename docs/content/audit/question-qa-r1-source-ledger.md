question-by-question assessment QA pass. It is intentionally separate from the
older mechanical sweep in `questions.md`: an entry here records a human-reviewed
claim, disposition, and source, not merely a lint finding.

means the item was removed rather than replaced. `LESSON GAP` and
`LESSON/QUESTION CONFLICT` are recorded when the lesson needs follow-up but is
outside this branch's edit scope.

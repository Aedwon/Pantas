## Evidence and classification

The accepted Level 1 inputs are `requirements/*.md`, `requirement-map.json`, `lesson-exam-tags.json`, `inventory-lessons.json`, the stored outline comparison, and the filled profiles. Research-era status headers inside individual requirement files do not override the later requirement acceptance and map review. Source numbers below refer to the named exam's requirement file, not a shared source-number system. Map record IDs identify the exact accepted statement and its qualifiers.

- **Shared in full:** permitted for an explicitly bounded scope statement, such as the identical organizer-named Mathematics subject in UPCAT and USTET. It does not mean identical topics, depth, item language, or shareable lesson bodies.
- **Shared in part:** a positively identified common facet with other facets different or unknown. Broad names and compound preparation records commonly support this classification only as a candidate.
- **Exam-specific evidence:** an organizer names an area more specifically for one exam, or a candidate connects to only one map. This does not establish exclusive exam relevance. Exact exclusivity remains unknown unless another organizer provides a corresponding restriction.
- **Unknown:** no suitable evidence resolves the comparison. Missing organizer lists, absent tags, and unnamed areas are not exclusions.

These are Level 2 scope classifications. Every lesson reference remains a title/summary/outline candidate. No source reviewed here establishes full reuse of any lesson body.

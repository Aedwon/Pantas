## Method and citation keys

This note compares accepted requirements with `requirement-map.json`, the nine `maps/` records, `lesson-exam-tags.json`, and inventory metadata. It preserves source confidence and cycle qualifications. The packets' historical worker status lines do not override the accepted requirement manifest and subsequent Level 1 approval.

**Full named-scope overlap** means identical organizer-named subjects or skills at the stated granularity. It never means identical tested difficulty or fully reusable lesson bodies. **Partial candidate overlap** means a related skill or preparation connection with specific scope, language, cycle, or depth differences. **Pair-specific named scope** means one organizer column names an area differently; it does not prove every underlying skill is excluded elsewhere. **Unknown** preserves organizer silence or inadequate evidence. No numerical overlap measure is calculated.

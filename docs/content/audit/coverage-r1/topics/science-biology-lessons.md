## Findings and recommendations

- Separate the cell-parts/comparison goal from detailed membrane-transport and tonicity reasoning. Keep one shared cell foundation. Evidence: cell-biology lines12-38 versus40-56. Benefit: a focused basic core and a focused movement application. Cost: new identity, moved examples/traps and later tag/practice remapping. This is not a proven exam exclusion.
- Separate inheritance probability and pedigrees from gene expression. Keep gene/allele definitions shared. Evidence: genetics-and-heredity lines12-39,41-55 and59-104. Benefit: coherent cross/pedigree versus molecular-process applications. Cost: foundation placement, new identity and future example/tag/practice remapping. Do not split each genetics term.
- Reorder cellular reproduction to teach chromosome sets, homologous pairs and sister chromatids before division. The body uses these at22-24 and33-40 without defining the difference. No existing lesson was verified as teaching that missing foundation.
- Add short local foundations before flower reproduction and Punnett crosses. Botany38-40 assumes flower-part roles. Genetics16/33/61 uses genotype-state terms and65-66 lists offspring without constructing gametes/square cells. These are teaching gaps, not reasons to invent prerequisite IDs.
- Preserve coherent overview lessons. Ten anatomy systems alone do not justify ten lessons. The anatomy survey needs an integrated transport/feedback example: human-anatomy-physiology12-74 surveys functions,78-138 mostly stays within systems,144-148 only names interactions.
- Ecology teaches chains, assumed transfer calculations, symbiosis and nitrogen distinctions. It does not fully teach branching-web consequences, population/community dynamics, biodiversity effects, or environmental management. Ecology20-59 and63-110 support those local limits. CSE management/protection remains General Information, not a CSE Biology subtest.
- Local missing or thin preparation includes microscope use, comparative biological-molecule classes, enzyme behavior, detailed metabolic pathways, sex-linked crosses, mutation consequences, full pedigree tracing and investigation design/data reading. Each separate facet and evidence appears below and in the appendix. No name/count establishes coverage.

Teaching findings use B03 (required ideas), L01 (goal), L17 (concept-before-application) and L19 (worked reasoning) for Level 3 structure only. No full checklist pass, formatting/voice sweep, quiz review, blind solve or illustration judgment occurred.

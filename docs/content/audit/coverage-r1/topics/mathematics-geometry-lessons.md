## Findings and recommendations

- Split candidate: separate planar perimeter/area and region subtraction from solid surface/volume measurement. They are independent student actions, not automatically different exam requirements. Preserve unit/dimension meanings once. Benefit: clear region versus face/solid modeling. Cost: a new approved identity, moved examples/traps, shared-foundation routing and later linked-practice review. Evidence: measurement16-36,92-117,119-196.
- Retain the other coherent surveys. Named concepts, formulas or example counts alone do not justify a split. Congruence, chord/secant and diagonal applications need development; splitting their current thin teaching would preserve the gap.
- Keep conditional transformations distinct from Reasoning truth-table construction and argument validation. Full comparison found shared conditional/negation and detachment ideas, but different student actions. Centralize short common definitions with contextual recaps. A whole-lesson merge would combine separate goals and require routing/practice rework. Evidence: geometry/logic-and-reasoning12-26,51-101; Reasoning truth-tables12-29 and syllogisms18-44.
- Readiness gaps: the general right-triangle relation appears first in circle and cone examples; conditional chaining appears first in its example. Reorder the needed explanation before application, without imposing redundant whole-lesson prerequisites. Evidence: circles111-169; measurement170-196; logic-and-reasoning91-101. L01/L17/L19 structural use.
- The exact UPCAT curriculum facet of proving the Pythagorean theorem is absent from these eight Geometry bodies. Computing ladder legs is taught; proving the theorem is a separate action. Coordinate distance has a short explanatory connection rather than a developed derivation. Evidence: triangles44-55,149-200; coordinate-geometry14-20. Refs: upcat-L191 and acet-L074. Actual exam proof depth remains unknown.
- Local thin/absent facets include biconditional applications, matching congruence criteria, similarity tests, diagonal/chord/secant applications, cone/pyramid surface-area problems, composite solids, and mixed-unit conversion methods. Detailed separate facet evidence appears once in the appendix. Circle equations and trigonometric functions are absent locally but have existing cross-topic inventory leads; do not duplicate those topics or infer global absence.
- Define simple/convex polygon meanings before their scope-qualified angle rules. Full Geometry reads and whole-inventory search found no existing polygon-scope foundation target. Physics optics uses convex/concave in another meaning. Evidence: quadrilaterals-and-polygons14-22. This is a short missing local foundation, not an invented prerequisite ID.

Evidence: parallel-perpendicular-lines125-142 and figure markers127/133. This records dependence only, not illustration need, sufficiency, quality or production.

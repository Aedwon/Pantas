## 2. Reviewer's topic list (written before reading the author's file)

Confidence key: Official = organizer states it. Curriculum = DepEd curriculum that all takers met by the test date; inferred, not a UPCAT statement. Weak = reviewer sites only.

### Format facts
- Four subtests: Language Proficiency (English and Filipino), Reading Comprehension (English and Filipino), Science, Mathematics. Official (R1, R2, R3).
- Administration about five hours including procedures; answering time 3 hours 20 minutes in the 2023 charter. Official, medium for current cycle (R2, R4, R5).
- Paper and pencil; test materials collected after the test. Official (R2, R4, R5). Answer-sheet layout not published.
- No calculating devices, cellphones, smartwatches, or cameras. Official (R2).
- Scoring: subtest scores standardized and combined with Grades 8 to 11 grades into the UPG. Official (R1, R2). Penalty for wrong answers: not published.
- Item counts, sectional times, choice count, language of Science and Mathematics items: unknown.

### Language Proficiency
- English grammar and usage (agreement, tense, pronouns, modifiers, parallelism), error identification, sentence correction. Weak.
- English vocabulary: word meaning, synonyms, antonyms, words in context. Weak.
- Punctuation and mechanics; spelling. Weak, spelling low.
- Filipino balarila: aspekto ng pandiwa, pang-angkop, pang-ugnay, wastong gamit (ng/nang, din/rin). Weak.
- Filipino talasalitaan, kasingkahulugan, kasalungat; idyoma and sawikain. Weak.

### Reading Comprehension (English and Filipino)
- Main idea, supporting details, inference, conclusions, author's purpose and tone, vocabulary in context. Weak, though bilingual scope is Official.
- Text types: expository, narrative; poetry low. Weak.

### Mathematics
- Number sense, sets, real numbers, arithmetic with no calculator. Curriculum (R9) plus Official device rule.
- Algebra: linear equations and inequalities, systems, exponents and radicals, polynomials and factoring, rational expressions, quadratic equations and functions. Curriculum (R9).
- Functions including rational, exponential, logarithmic. Curriculum (R10).
- Geometry: triangles (congruence, similarity, inequality), polygons, circles, coordinate geometry, area and volume. Curriculum (R9).
- Basic trigonometry (right-triangle ratios). Curriculum (R9).
- Statistics: central tendency, variability, position. Curriculum (R9).
- Combinatorics and probability. Curriculum (R9).
- Logic: propositions, syllogisms. Curriculum (R10).
- Sequences and series. Curriculum (R9, Grade 10 first quarter, competency M10AL-Ib-1, PDF p. 246).
- Word problems. Weak.
- Calculus: low; not core for every strand. Unknown.

### Science
- Biology: cells, body systems, genetics and heredity, ecology, evolution, classification. Curriculum strand (R8) plus Weak detail.
- Chemistry: matter and its classification, atomic structure, periodic table, bonding, reactions, mole concept, acids and bases, gas laws. Curriculum strand (R8) plus Weak detail.
- Physics: motion, forces, work and energy, heat, waves, light, sound, electricity and magnetism. Curriculum strand (R8) plus Weak detail.
- Earth and space: plate tectonics, earthquakes, volcanoes, atmosphere, weather and climate, solar system and astronomy. Curriculum strand (R8) plus Weak detail.
- Topic weights: unknown. DepEd gives each strand one quarter per year in Grades 7 to 10 (R8), which suggests but does not prove a balanced mix.

# CSE teaching gaps to add during the rewrite

Date: October 25, 2026. Source: the accepted coverage study (`lesson-structure-data.json`,
`requirementAccounting` rows for `cse-professional` and `cse-subprofessional`). Each row there
names a teachable part of a CSE requirement, the lesson that should teach it, and a verdict:
explained, thin, named only, or absent. This file takes every CSE part that is not explained.

Every row with its placement is in `cse-teaching-gaps.jsonl`.

- By source strength: official 264, curriculum 78, weak 19. "official" means the CSE
  organizer names the broad area; the finer part itself usually comes from the school

## How the rewrite uses this

- Each lesson's brief lists its gap parts after its audit findings, with the missing ideas the
  study recorded.
- Thin and named-only parts need a fuller explanation or a worked example. Absent parts need new
  teaching.
- Weak parts are added only when they fit naturally; they are never a reason for a new lesson.
- New lessons follow the lesson authoring runbook and get their own questions (at least 20) and
  an independent read like every rewritten lesson.
- The lead owns the prerequisite links for new lessons.

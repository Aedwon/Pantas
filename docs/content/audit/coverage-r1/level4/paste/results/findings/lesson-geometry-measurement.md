## Accuracy concerns

- "summary: "The perimeter, area, surface area, and volume formulas for the figures that appear on every exam."": The claim that these figures appear on every exam is an unsupported absolute and is unlikely to be literally true across all target exams.
- "For shaded regions, do not hunt for a special formula. It is always whole minus hole: outer area minus inner area.": The statement is too broad mathematically because shaded regions can require sums of parts, multiple subtractions, or overlap reasoning rather than one outer-minus-inner subtraction.
- "Computing $\pi r^2 h$ for a cone gives an answer three times too big, and that answer is always among the choices.": The numerical error factor is correct, but the assertion that the distractor is always present among the choices is not supportable as a universal exam fact.

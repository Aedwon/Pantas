## Findings from the chat read

  - Problem: The scope is overstated. Section 4(A) says the norms are standards of personal conduct "in the discharge and execution of official duties," not a blanket rule that all eight must be observed "at all times." Source: https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1989/ra_6713_1989.html

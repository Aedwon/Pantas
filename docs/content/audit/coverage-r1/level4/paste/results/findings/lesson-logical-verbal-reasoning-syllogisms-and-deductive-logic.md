- missing, high priority, concept, code. At: ""All mammals are warm-blooded. A whale is a mammal. Therefore, a whale is warm-blooded."". Draw: Draw nested circles showing whales inside mammals and mammals inside warm-blooded animals, with one whale marked inside the innermost class.. Why: The nested sets make the categorical inclusion and necessary conclusion visible instead of leaving the class relation purely verbal.
- missing, high priority, concept, code. At: ""If a student studies hard, they will pass the exam. Maria passed the exam. Therefore, Maria studied hard."". Draw: Draw a one-way arrow from studies hard to passes, then show the attempted reverse arrow from passes to studies hard crossed out.. Why: The diagram directly shows why affirming the consequent reverses a one-way conditional and is invalid.
- missing, high priority, concept, code. At: ""If the alarm is working properly under these conditions, it will ring during a fire. It did not ring during the fire. Therefore, it was not working properly under those stated conditions."". Draw: Draw a conditional flow from working properly to rings during fire, then trace not ringing backward by modus tollens to not working properly.. Why: A compact flow diagram helps students see the valid direction of modus tollens and distinguish it from reversing the conditional.
- missing, medium priority, concept, code. At: ""All birds can fly. A penguin is a bird. Therefore, a penguin can fly."". Draw: Draw a valid class-inclusion chain from penguin to bird to flyer, while marking the first universal premise as factually false.. Why: The figure separates valid logical structure from the false premise, reinforcing the difference between validity and soundness.
- missing, medium priority, concept, code. At: ""Either the document is in Cabinet A or it is in Cabinet B. It is not in Cabinet A. Therefore, it is in Cabinet B."". Draw: Draw Cabinet A and Cabinet B as two labeled options, cross out A, and highlight B as the remaining disjunct.. Why: The elimination picture makes disjunctive syllogism immediate and contrasts it with the conditional forms taught earlier.

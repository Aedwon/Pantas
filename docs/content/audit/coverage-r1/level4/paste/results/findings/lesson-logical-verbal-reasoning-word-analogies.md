  - Problem: The double analogy has more than one defensible answer: both leaf and branch are parts of a tree, so choices A and B both satisfy the lesson's stated part-to-whole relation.
- **L02** (Major; one read, paste run)
  - Quote: "Natural-language relationships are rarely mathematically unique. Two words can be related in several ways, so the goal is to find the **most specific relationship that the item and answer choices support**, not to assume there is one metaphysically exact relationship between the words."
  - Problem: This teaching prose is not Grade 5 simple English. Phrases such as "mathematically unique" and "metaphysically exact relationship" are unnecessarily advanced for the required reading level.

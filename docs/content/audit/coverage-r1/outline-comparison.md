## Original outline against the app corpus (Science)

The original tab's Science section lists the same 51 lessons as the app, in the
same four topics. Differences are wording only:

- Taxonomy: tab says "Domains, Kingdoms"; app title says "Domains, Ranks".
- Chemical reactions: tab says "Types of Reactions"; app says "Reaction Patterns".
- Gas laws: app adds "Amontons's" beside Gay-Lussac's.
- Mole concept: tab says "Avogadro's Number"; app says "Avogadro Constant".
- Acids and bases: tab says "pH Scale"; app says "pH".
- Nuclear chemistry: tab lists decay types; app says "Radioactive Decay".

The tab's sub-bullets (for example "Organelles and Their Functions" under Cell
Biology) are sections inside lessons, not separate lessons. No Science lesson
exists in one source and not the other.

## Lessons without clear UPCAT requirement support

Science lesson gets a provisional UPCAT tag. These lessons rest on weak or no
topic-level evidence; Task 5 checks depth and Task 8 decides how to present

  formulas), VSEPR within chemical-bonding, solutions (molality),
  stoichiometry (limiting reagents, percent yield), nuclear-chemistry,
  gravity-and-keplers-laws, two-dimensional-motion, fluid-mechanics
  (Bernoulli), units-and-measurements.

None of these is a recommendation to remove content. Relevance to other exams
is checked in the full run.

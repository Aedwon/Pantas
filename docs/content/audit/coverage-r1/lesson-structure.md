## Decision-facing findings so far

Several lessons switch between distinct student actions. Focused splits could separate inheritance from gene expression, reaction quantities from composition, and projectile from circular motion. Most units retain coherent goals. Shared chart, context and central-point teaching can remain one core with distinct applications.

Missing teaching needs development; moving content alone will not repair it. Two independent sample flags removed redundant prerequisites already taught locally. The final existing-lesson graph and missing-foundation register await all readings.

Actual bodies confirm narrow skills, rather than complete compound requirements. Filipino applications remain limited in several language and reading units. Exam depth and student access remain uncertain; practice belongs to Level 5.

This draft records load-bearing figure dependencies only. Illustration judgments belong to Levels 4 and 5. No content changes occurred.

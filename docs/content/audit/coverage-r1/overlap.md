## What full, partial and exam-specific mean here

| Classification | Valid inference at Level 2 | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Full named scope | The organizer names the same subject/domain/skill, with the stated cycle and language conditions | Does not establish equal finer topics, difficulty, instruction or body reuse |
| Partial scope or preparation | A concrete common facet is identified, with another facet different or unknown | Curriculum and weak leads remain candidates; no full-coverage claim |
| Pair-specific named area | The organizer assigns an area to one of two explicitly compared columns, such as CSE Analytical/Clerical | Does not prove the underlying skill is excluded from other areas or other exams |
| Single-map candidate | Exactly one accepted map supplies a positive name-level connection | Not proof of exclusive exam relevance or a required whole lesson |
| Unknown relevance or extent | The accepted evidence does not establish a suitable connection or comparable finer scope | Organizer silence and absent tags are not exclusions |

## 3. Required topics and skills

**Confirmed** means an official or strong secondary source states the requirement. **Supported, curriculum** means common curriculum preparation expected before the test date, inferred from DepEd guides, not a UPCAT statement. It ranks above **Supported, weak**, which means a preparation candidate from reviewer coverage, recollection, or simulated items. Neither supported label establishes a current UPCAT requirement. **Unknown** means the inspected evidence did not resolve the question. Citations beside unknowns identify the sources checked, not evidence of exclusion. [1-3, 6-12, 16, 17]

The skill descriptions under supported topics translate source topic labels into observable actions. Curriculum citations identify school preparation; reviewer-only descriptions remain weak. Neither is an organizer competency list. Unknown does not mean excluded. [6-8, 11, 12, 16, 17]

- Text/visual information: interpret textual aids and move between linear text and tables, graphs, or maps. [9, PDF p. 235; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Literary interpretation: identify tone, mood, technique, and purpose. [9, PDF p. 237; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Claims and evidence: identify details that support or refute a claim and examine bias. [9, PDF p. 231; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Language and organization: use modals and structures of modification; apply cohesion and coherence. [9, PDF pp. 231, 237; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- SHS critical reading: distinguish text-development patterns, identify explicit/implicit claims of fact, value, or policy, evaluate assertions and counterclaims using textual evidence. ### C. Numerical ability / Mathematics

**Supported, curriculum**

- Arithmetic and measurement: work with fractions, decimals, integers, ratios, proportions, percentages, units, rate, speed, area, surface area, and volume. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 6-7 standards; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Number systems and elementary algebra: use sets and real numbers, expressions, and linear equations/inequalities in one variable. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 7; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Algebraic expressions and systems: factor polynomials, manipulate rational expressions, and solve linear equations, inequalities, and systems in two variables. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 8; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Quadratics and related algebra: solve quadratic equations/inequalities and rational equations; interpret quadratic functions, variation, and radicals. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 9; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Sequences and polynomial functions: interpret sequences/series, polynomial equations, and polynomial functions. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 10; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Geometry: reason about polygon angles, triangle congruence/similarity, parallel/perpendicular lines, parallelograms, circles, and coordinate geometry; apply basic trigonometry. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 7-10; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Data and probability: interpret data presentations, central tendency, variability, measures of position, simple-event probabilities, and combinatorics. [7, PDF p. 8, Grade 7-10; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- SHS functions: evaluate/compose functions; interpret domain, range, graphs, and inverses; solve rational, exponential, and logarithmic equations/inequalities. [11; official core curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- SHS financial mathematics: calculate interest and annuities, interpret stocks/bonds, and solve loan problems. [11; official core curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- SHS statistics: interpret random-variable distributions and normal probabilities, sampling, estimation, hypothesis tests, correlation, and regression. Grade 11/12 allocation does not prove completion before testing. [13; official core curriculum, conditional preparation, exam inclusion unknown]

**Supported, weak**

- Mathematics preparation: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, elementary statistics/probability, and word problems. [6; weak]

**Unknown**

- Exam depth, calculator policy, advanced trigonometry, conics, induction, or calculus. Pre-Calculus is explicitly STEM-specialized; its contents cannot become an all-applicant requirement. [1-3, 18; unknown for exam scope, official for STEM classification]

- Chemical change: interpret carbon compounds/biomolecules and gases; balance chemical equations and solve simple mole/mass problems using conservation of mass. [8, PDF p. 10; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Living systems: connect cell structures and organism systems to function; interpret reproduction, inheritance, variation, and evolution. [8, PDF p. 12; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Ecosystems and bioenergetics: interpret populations/communities, energy and matter cycling, photosynthesis/respiration, biodiversity, and human impacts. [8, PDF p. 13; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Motion and forces: interpret distance, speed, acceleration, motion graphs, Newton's laws, work/energy, momentum, and projectile motion. [8, PDF p. 14; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Energy and waves: explain heat transfer, mechanical energy, light/sound, electrical transmission, optical instruments, generators, and motors. [8, PDF p. 14; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Earth processes: interpret faults, earthquakes, volcanoes, map distributions, and plate tectonics. [8, PDF p. 16; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Weather and climate: explain monsoons, breezes, typhoons, weather versus climate, and climate factors. [8, PDF p. 16; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- Space: explain seasons/eclipses, solar-system objects, stellar characteristics, and apparent constellation movement. [8, PDF p. 16; official curriculum, exam inclusion unknown]
- SHS physical science: relate molecular structure to properties; reason about chemical reaction rates and amounts; interpret motion, momentum, light, and astronomical models. - SHS Earth and life science: explain Earth systems, minerals/rocks, resources and hazards; connect cells, energy, reproduction, evolution, and ecological interactions.

### Part 1: Roots, Bases, and Valid Splits

File: `roots-bases-and-valid-splits.md`. ID: `lesson-vocabulary-roots-bases-and-valid-splits`. Ordering: 3.01.

One idea: Roots, Bases, and Valid Splits. Teaching-word estimate: 358.

Key points:
1. Identify roots, bases, affixes, and stems.
2. Check whether word parts explain the whole word.

Core material: A **root** carries a word's main meaning. A **base** is the word or part to which another part is added. In *unhappy*, *happy* is both the root and the base. | An **affix** is a part added to a base to change its meaning or job. A **stem** is the form to which the next affix is added. In *helpfulness*, *helpful* is the stem before *-ness* is added. | A **prefix** comes before the base: *un-* in *unhappy*. A **suffix** comes after it: *-less* in *careless*. | To **infer** a meaning is to work it out from clues. Word parts can help you infer meaning, but a familiar set of letters is not always a real word part. | Ask whether your split leaves a meaningful base. Then check whether the parts explain the whole word. | In *unhappy*, *un-* means "not," and *happy* is a real base. In *uncle*, the first letters are also *un*, but *cle* is not its base. The split does not explain the word..
Examples from original: 5. Traps from original: 1, 3.
Shortcuts moved: Use word parts to narrow the meaning. Then read the whole sentence to check your choice.

### Part 3: Suffixes for Qualities and Absence

File: `suffixes-for-qualities-and-absence.md`. ID: `lesson-vocabulary-suffixes-for-qualities-and-absence`. Ordering: 3.03.

One idea: Suffixes for Qualities and Absence. Teaching-word estimate: 266.

Key points:
1. Use -ful and -less to describe a noun.
2. Use -ness to name a state or quality.

Core material: ### English Suffixes | A **noun** names a person, place, thing, or idea. An **adjective** describes a noun. An **adverb** can describe how an action happens, and a **verb** tells an action or state. | The suffix **-ful** means "full of" or "having." *Helpful* describes someone or something that gives help, so it is an adjective. | The suffix **-less** means "without." *Careless* describes someone who does not take enough care. It is also an adjective. | The suffix **-ness** makes a noun naming a state or quality. *Kind* describes a person, while *kindness* names the quality of being kind..
Examples from original: 4, 11. Traps from original: none.
Shortcuts moved: Ask what a suffix adds and what job the new word does. A meaning clue and a sentence clue should agree.

Core material: ### Mga Panlapi sa Filipino | Ang **salitang-ugat** (root word) ang nagdadala ng pangunahing kahulugan.

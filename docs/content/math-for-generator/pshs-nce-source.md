## Sources

   `http://web.archive.org/web/2025id_/https://pshs.edu.ph/wp-content/downloads/public_bidding/Promotional_Materials_for_2024_NCE/NCE_Reviewer.pdf`
2. **Official, older.** PSHS, *NCE Sample Test Questions (2015)*, 3 pages, 5 Quantitative Ability samples:
   `http://web.archive.org/web/2020id_/http://pshs.edu.ph/downloads/NCE-Sample_Test_Questions-(2015).pdf`

## What the sources state, and what they do not

- States: four tests, "TEST I - QUANTITATIVE ABILITY", "TEST II - VERBAL APTITUDE", "TEST III - SCIENTIFIC ABILITY", "TEST IV - ABSTRACT REASONING"; "a four-hour aptitude test designed to assess higher-order thinking skills"; five choices, A to E.
- States: 20 official Quantitative Ability sample items with answer keys, no worked solutions.
- Does not state: an item count per test, time per test, scoring, a math syllabus, a competency list, or a grade level.

So the math coverage below is "the item types PSHS shows in its official sample", not "what the exam covers". Lessons and questions may say "the official PSHS sample includes items like this". They may not claim a topic list, item count or weighting for the NCE.

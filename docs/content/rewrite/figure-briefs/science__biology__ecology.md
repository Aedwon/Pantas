## 01-energy-pyramid-under-an-assumed.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/biology/ecology/01-energy-pyramid-under-an-assumed.svg`
- Marker: Energy pyramid under an assumed 10% transfer at each step, with producers at the base labeled 100%, primary consumers labeled 10%, secondary consumers labeled 1%, and tertiary consumers at the top labeled 0.1%; label the figure "Illustration of an assumed transfer rate, not an exact rule."
- Purpose: In Food Chains, Food Webs, and Trophic Levels, show how available energy decreases across feeding levels under an assumed 10% transfer at each step.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas with four stacked pyramid tiers on the left and aligned labels on the right. From bottom to top: producers, primary consumers, secondary consumers, tertiary consumers. Make tiers progressively narrower upward, with enough height to distinguish each. Pair each name with its percentage. The widths are schematic, not a quantitative scale. Center the assumption caption below the pyramid.
- Labels (verbatim): "producers / 100%"; "primary consumers / 10%"; "secondary consumers / 1%"; "tertiary consumers / 0.1%"; "Illustration of an assumed / transfer rate, not an exact / rule."
- Focus (warm accent): The assumption caption beneath the pyramid.
- Must not show: Do not imply that 10% transfer is universal or that tier widths precisely encode percentages. Do not include the kilojoule values or calculation answers from Example #3.
- Suggested height: 340

## 02-branching-food-web-with-arrows.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/biology/ecology/02-branching-food-web-with-arrows.svg`
- Marker: Branching food web with arrows grass to rabbits, grass to grasshoppers, grasshoppers to frogs, rabbits to hawks, and frogs to hawks; label arrows "energy moves to" and place a downward arrow beside grasshoppers.
- Purpose: In Example #2: Reading a Branching Food Web, help students trace energy paths and consider how a grasshopper decline could affect linked populations.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas. Place grass at bottom center, rabbits at middle left, grasshoppers at lower right, frogs above grasshoppers, and hawks at top center. Draw five connecting arrows: grass → rabbits, grass → grasshoppers, grasshoppers → frogs, rabbits → hawks, and frogs → hawks. Keep paths clear of organism drawings and labels. Below the web, include a small arrow key labeled “energy moves to” that applies to every connecting arrow. Place a separate downward arrow immediately beside grasshoppers, disconnected from the feeding paths.
- Labels (verbatim): "grass"; "rabbits"; "grasshoppers"; "frogs"; "hawks"; "energy moves to"
- Focus (warm accent): The downward arrow beside grasshoppers.
- Must not show: Do not show population-change arrows beside frogs, rabbits, grass, or hawks, or state the predicted effects. Do not add feeding connections or imply that the downward arrow beside grasshoppers is an energy path.
- Suggested height: 420

## 03-coordinate-grid-with-axes-labeled.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/mathematics/algebra/linear-equations-inequalities/03-coordinate-grid-with-axes-labeled.svg`
- Marker: coordinate grid with axes labeled x and y, a straight line y=2x-3 through labeled points (0,-3), (1,-1), and (2,1); show a rightward step of 1 and an upward step of 2
- Purpose: In Example #4, connect the y-intercept at (0,-3) and the slope of 2 to plotting a line by moving 1 right and 2 up.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas with y=2x-3 centered at the top. Below it, place a square coordinate grid spanning x=-2 to 4 and y=-4 to 2, using equal unit spacing. Label the horizontal axis x at its right arrowhead and the vertical axis y at its top arrowhead. Plot and label (0,-3), (1,-1), and (2,1), offsetting labels clear of the line. Draw a solid straight line through all three points and extend it to the grid edges with arrowheads. Add one connected step from (0,-3): a horizontal arrow to (1,-3) labeled 1, followed by a vertical arrow to (1,-1) labeled 2. Omit numeric tick labels.
- Labels (verbatim): "y=2x-3"; "x"; "y"; "(0,-3)"; "(1,-1)"; "(2,1)"; "1"; "2"
- Focus (warm accent): The connected right-1, up-2 step between the first two plotted points.
- Must not show: Unequal horizontal and vertical unit scales, a dashed graph line, or a plotted point at the step's corner (1,-3).
- Suggested height: 420

## 04-coordinate-grid-with-a-dashed.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/mathematics/algebra/linear-equations-inequalities/04-coordinate-grid-with-a-dashed.svg`
- Marker: coordinate grid with a dashed line through labeled points (0,3) and (3,0), and the side containing the origin (0,0) shaded
- Purpose: In Example #7, let students use a dashed boundary and shading that includes the origin to identify the inequality themselves.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas. Center a square coordinate grid with equal unit spacing, spanning -1 to 4 on both axes. Label the horizontal axis x at its right arrowhead and the vertical axis y at its top arrowhead. Draw a dashed straight boundary through (0,3) and (3,0), extending across the grid. Label both intercept locations without filled point markers. Shade the entire side containing the origin, including its portions beyond the first quadrant. Mark and label (0,0), keeping the axes and grid visible through the shading. Omit numeric tick labels.
- Labels (verbatim): "x"; "y"; "(0,3)"; "(3,0)"; "(0,0)"
- Focus (warm accent): The shaded half-plane containing (0,0).
- Must not show: The answer x+y<3, either boundary equation y=-x+3 or x+y=3, the test calculation 0+0<3, a solid boundary, or shading limited to the first-quadrant triangle.
- Suggested height: 400

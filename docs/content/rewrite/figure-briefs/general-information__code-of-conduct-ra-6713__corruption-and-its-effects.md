# Figure briefs: corruption-and-its-effects

Lesson: `content/lessons/general-information/code-of-conduct-ra-6713/corruption-and-its-effects.md`. Folder: `content/illustrations/general-information/code-of-conduct-ra-6713/corruption-and-its-effects/`.

## 01-center-corruption-with-arms-social.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/general-information/code-of-conduct-ra-6713/corruption-and-its-effects/01-center-corruption-with-arms-social.svg`
- Marker: Center "Corruption" with arms "Social: unfair access, lost trust", "Economic: wasted funds, higher costs", and "Political: weaker accountability, lost election trust".
- Purpose: In the Effects section, help students distinguish the social, economic, and political harms linked to corruption.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas. Place Corruption in a central hub. Place the Social box above it, the Economic box below-left, and the Political box below-right. Draw three outward arrows from the hub, one to each box. Allow generous space for wrapped text.
- Labels (verbatim): "Corruption"; "Social: unfair access, / lost trust"; "Economic: wasted funds, / higher costs"; "Political: weaker / accountability, / lost election trust"
- Focus (warm accent): The Corruption hub.
- Must not show: Do not imply that the three categories are mutually exclusive or that every corrupt act necessarily produces every listed harm. Add no statistics or specific cases.
- Suggested height: 440

## 02-complaint-points-to-ombudsman-acts.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/general-information/code-of-conduct-ra-6713/corruption-and-its-effects/02-complaint-points-to-ombudsman-acts.svg`
- Marker: "Complaint" points to "Ombudsman: acts on complaints"; separate box "Sandiganbayan: tries cases within its legal authority".
- Purpose: In How the State responds, help students distinguish the Ombudsman's complaint-handling role from the Sandiganbayan's role in trying cases within its legal authority.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas with three vertically arranged boxes. Put Complaint at the top and draw a downward arrow to the Ombudsman box beneath it. Place the Sandiganbayan box lower down, separated by a generous gap, with no connecting arrow.
- Labels (verbatim): "Complaint"; "Ombudsman: / acts on complaints"; "Sandiganbayan: / tries cases within / its legal authority"
- Focus (warm accent): The Ombudsman box.
- Must not show: Do not draw an automatic referral from the Ombudsman to the Sandiganbayan, imply that all complaints become court cases, or suggest that a complaint establishes guilt.
- Suggested height: 340

## 03-linked-boxes-inflated-project-cost.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/general-information/code-of-conduct-ra-6713/corruption-and-its-effects/03-linked-boxes-inflated-project-cost.svg`
- Marker: Linked boxes "Inflated project cost", "Fewer classrooms", "Lower trust"; first arrow labeled "economic effect", second "political effect".
- Purpose: Beside Example #6, show how inflated project costs can reduce classroom provision and extend into political harm through lower trust.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide canvas. Stack three centered boxes: Inflated project cost at the top, Fewer classrooms in the middle, and Lower trust at the bottom. Connect each box to the next with a downward arrow. Place economic effect beside the first arrow and political effect beside the second.
- Labels (verbatim): "Inflated project cost"; "Fewer classrooms"; "Lower trust"; "economic effect"; "political effect"
- Focus (warm accent): The Fewer classrooms box.
- Must not show: Do not add monetary amounts, classroom counts, or claims that lower trust is inevitable. Do not add classifications for the later Mixed Effects exercise.
- Suggested height: 360

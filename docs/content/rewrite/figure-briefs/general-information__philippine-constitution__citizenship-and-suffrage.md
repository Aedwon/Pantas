- File: `content/illustrations/general-information/philippine-constitution/citizenship-and-suffrage/01-citizenship-flow-for-status-at.svg`
- Marker: Citizenship flow before any later loss: Filipino father at birth? Yes: Natural-born citizen, no election needed; No: Filipino mother at birth? Yes: Born before January 17, 1973 and covered by Article IV Section 1(3)? Yes: Validly elected citizenship upon majority? Yes: Deemed natural-born; No: Citizenship not established through this route; No to historical election group: Natural-born citizen; Neither parent Filipino: Check citizenship when the Constitution was adopted or naturalization under law; label these exits Citizen, natural-born status needs separate checking and Naturalized citizen respectively.
- Purpose: Help students check parental citizenship before the historical election rule and distinguish the resulting citizenship statuses.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide vertical flow with a scope caption above four decision rectangles. First ask about a Filipino father at birth. Yes ends at Natural-born citizen, no election needed. No leads to the Filipino-mother question. Its Yes branch leads to the birth-date and Section 1(3) question. Its No branch leads to a separate block for checking citizenship at adoption or naturalization under law. In the historical-election question, Yes leads to the valid-election question; No ends at Natural-born citizen. Valid election leads to Deemed natural-born; failed election ends at Citizenship not established through this route. In the separate checking block, use two rows pairing each legal basis with its corresponding exit. Show an exit only when that basis is established. Label decision branches Yes or No. Keep all connectors clear of unrelated outcomes.
- Labels (verbatim): "Citizenship flow / before any later loss"; "Filipino father at birth?"; "Yes"; "No"; "Natural-born citizen, / no election needed"; "Filipino mother at birth?"; "Born before January 17, 1973 / and covered by / Article IV Section 1(3)?"; "Validly elected citizenship / upon majority?"; "Deemed natural-born"; "Citizenship not established / through this route"; "Natural-born citizen"; "Check citizenship when / the Constitution was adopted / or naturalization under law"; "citizenship when the / Constitution was adopted"; "Citizen, natural-born status / needs separate checking"; "naturalization under law"; "Naturalized citizen"
- Focus (warm accent): The Deemed natural-born outcome after valid election under Section 1(3).
- Must not show: Do not send a child with a Filipino father through the election requirement. Do not require election solely because the child was born before January 17, 1973 to a Filipino mother. Do not treat failed election as proof of alien status or automatically send it to naturalization. Do not imply that having neither parent Filipino establishes either remaining citizenship basis. Do not classify all citizens at adoption as natural-born or classify naturalized citizens as natural-born. Do not use birthplace as a citizenship test. Do not add a fixed age for historical election cases, later loss, reacquisition, or named examples.

- Purpose: Help students distinguish citizenship at acquisition from natural-born status in the lesson’s citizenship section.
- Layout: Use a 360-unit-wide vertical flow, with a scope caption at the top and five numbered-by-position decision cards below it. Keep outcomes inside the cards to limit separate elements. Card 1 asks about citizenship at adoption: Yes ends at its citizen outcome; No leads to card 2. Card 2 asks about Section 1(3): Yes leads to card 3; No follows an outer bypass arrow to card 4. Card 3 asks about valid election: Yes connects to one shared natural-born outcome pill; No ends at the separate-legal-basis outcome inside card 3. Card 4 asks about a Filipino parent at birth: Yes connects to the same natural-born pill; No leads to card 5. Card 5 asks about naturalization, with its Yes and No outcomes stacked inside it. Place the shared natural-born pill beside cards 3–4. Label every branch Yes or No; keep bypass arrows clear of all other connectors.
- Labels (verbatim): "status at acquisition, / before any later loss"; "Already a citizen when / the Constitution was / adopted?"; "Yes"; "No"; "Citizen, natural-born / status needs separate / checking"; "Covered by Article IV / Section 1(3), born before / January 17, 1973 to a / Filipino mother?"; "Validly elected upon / majority?"; "Natural-born citizen"; "Citizenship not established / through this route, / check another legal basis"; "Filipino father or mother / at birth?"; "Naturalized under law?"; "Naturalized citizen"; "Citizenship not established / by these routes."
- Focus (warm accent): The shared Natural-born citizen outcome pill.
- Must not show: Do not classify all citizens at adoption as natural-born. Do not send the failed election branch directly to naturalization or treat it as proof of alien status. Do not require election for every child of a Filipino mother. Do not add birthplace, later loss, reacquisition, or named example cases.

## 02-domestic-voter-checklist-with-four.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/general-information/philippine-constitution/citizenship-and-suffrage/02-domestic-voter-checklist-with-four.svg`
- Marker: Domestic voter checklist with four boxes labeled Filipino citizen, At least 18 on election day, At least 1 year in the Philippines, At least 6 months in the voting place; show Pass only if all boxes pass, Fail if any fails; footer: Also check registration and legal disqualifications.
- Purpose: Help students check all four domestic voting minimums together while remembering registration and legal disqualifications in the suffrage section.
- Layout: On a 360-unit-wide figure, stack four equal checklist boxes with empty check squares at the left. Below them, join four thin connectors into one junction to show that every box matters. From the junction, draw two branches: one labeled only if all boxes pass leading to Pass, and one labeled if any fails leading to Fail. Place the footer beneath both outcomes. Keep all checkboxes empty so the figure presents a rule rather than a completed voter assessment.
- Labels (verbatim): "Filipino citizen"; "At least 18 on election day"; "At least 1 year in the / Philippines"; "At least 6 months in the / voting place"; "only if all boxes pass"; "Pass"; "if any fails"; "Fail"; "Also check registration / and legal disqualifications."
- Focus (warm accent): The junction where all four checklist connectors meet.
- Must not show: Do not imply that passing these four boxes alone establishes full voting eligibility. Do not add literacy, property, overseas voting rules, or a completed assessment of an example voter.
- Suggested height: 360

- Marker: Statement chain with arrows from application complete to sent for review to tracking number assigned; show Rico's complete application at the start.
- Purpose: In Example #5, show how matching statements form a conditional chain that can be used with the given fact about Rico's application.

Keep the arrows one-way and the application sheet free of additional text.

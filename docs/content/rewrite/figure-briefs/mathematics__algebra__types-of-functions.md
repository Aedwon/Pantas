## 01-seven-labeled-coordinate-panels-y.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/mathematics/algebra/types-of-functions/01-seven-labeled-coordinate-panels-y.svg`
- Marker: seven labeled coordinate panels: y=x on -3<=x<=3; y=x^2 on -3<=x<=3; y=1/x on -3<=x<=3 excluding 0 with asymptotes x=0 and y=0; y=|x| on -3<=x<=3; y=2^x on -3<=x<=3; y=log_2(x) on 0<x<=4 with asymptote x=0; and y=x for x<0, y=2 for x>=0 on -3<=x<=3 with open point (0,0) and filled point (0,2); label each family and both axes
- Purpose: Help students connect the seven function families in Core Concept with their characteristic graph shapes.
- Layout: At 360 units wide, arrange seven coordinate panels in two columns across four rows; center the last panel. Order left to right: linear, quadratic, rational, absolute value, exponential, logarithmic, then piecewise. Put each family name and formula above its panel. Label every horizontal axis x and vertical axis y. Use the specified input ranges: −3 to 3 except logarithmic, which uses 0<x≤4. Choose vertical ranges that show each shape clearly. Draw the reciprocal as two separate branches with dashed asymptotes coinciding with both axes; draw the logarithm approaching its dashed vertical asymptote at x=0. In the piecewise panel, draw y=x only left of zero, with an open circle at (0,0), and y=2 from zero rightward, with a filled circle at (0,2). Keep numeric tick labels off to preserve space.
- Labels (verbatim): "Linear / y=x"; "Quadratic / y=x^2"; "Rational / y=1/x"; "Absolute Value / y=|x|"; "Exponential / y=2^x"; "Logarithmic / y=log_2(x)"; "Piecewise / y=x for x<0 / y=2 for x>=0"; "x"; "y"
- Focus (warm accent): The filled boundary point (0,2) in the piecewise panel.
- Must not show: No connection between the reciprocal branches, no logarithm at nonpositive inputs, and no connecting segment between the piecewise boundary points. Do not imply that the displayed input ranges are the functions' full domains.
- Suggested height: 480

## 04-editorial-cartoon-with-two-side.svg

- Purpose: In Example #4, students compare exaggerated features in adjacent scenes to understand how caricature and juxtaposition convey criticism.

- Focus (warm accent): The official's extremely large mouth in the left scene.
- Must not show: A real person's likeness, party symbols, readable invented requests, or a caption explaining the criticism.

# Astra stage 1: lead rulings

November 9, 2026. 15 lessons audited (plus the structural-stability cost test) before total Codex room reached the 20% floor; 2 lessons of the stage-1 list wait (parallel-perpendicular-lines, measures-of-variability). Raw results: `results/`. Every quote was checked against the file and found.

Bar: a statement a CET student would apply and get wrong is fixed. An exception that standard school texts and exam keys do not teach is downgraded, and fixed only when one hedge word fixes it.

## Upheld, fix

1. cellular-metabolism, Example 4 "An enzyme is a protein.": contradicts the lesson's own "almost all enzymes are proteins". Word the step so it does not claim every enzyme is a protein.
2. cellular-metabolism, "An **organic molecule** is ... built around chains or rings of carbon and hydrogen": methane has no chain or ring. Define without requiring a chain or ring.
3. cellular-metabolism question 0024 explanation, "Living things make sugars, so glucose is an organic molecule.": the reason is wrong (living things also make CO2 and water). Explain by the carbon-hydrogen structure.
4. characteristics-of-life, "its metabolism is very slow but not zero": dry seeds can reach near-complete metabolic arrest. Drop "but not zero".
5. evolution, "drift is small, though never exactly zero": drift can leave a frequency unchanged. Remove the "never exactly zero" claim.
6. meiosis question 0001 note, "Check that a diploid cell has two alleles of the gene.": fails for X-linked genes in males. Limit to a gene on a non-sex chromosome.
7. chemical-bonding, formal charge "$L$ is the electrons in its lone pairs": must count all nonbonding electrons, including an unpaired one (NO, which the lesson mentions).
8. triangles, "SSA also fixes one triangle when the given side across from the given angle is at least as long as the other given side.": a = b with A = 120 degrees gives no triangle. State the acute-angle condition for the equal case.
9. triangles, "A **Pythagorean triple** is three whole numbers": whole numbers include zero. Say positive whole numbers.
10. pulleys-and-levers, "the effort is half the load": holds only when the load is held still or moves at a steady speed, which question 0013 tests. State the condition.
11. electricity-and-magnetism, "Paper is an insulator, but its atoms still shift.": in polarization the charges inside each atom or molecule shift, not the atoms. Fix the mechanism.
12. work-energy-power, "If a motor or a push from outside adds energy, ... its total energy rises.": not if the same amount leaves. Add the condition.
13. speeches, "A number about the speaker, such as years of service, supports ethos instead.": a personal-loss number is pathos; the number's job decides. Tie ethos to numbers that show the speaker's experience or credibility.

## Downgraded to Minor, fixed with one hedge

14. characteristics-of-life, "a dead leaf or a piece of wood": fresh sapwood has living cells. Name a dry wooden plank or dead wood.
15. evolution, "a male has only one allele for a gene on the X chromosome": pseudoautosomal genes. Say "most genes".
16. evolution, "changes gained during life are not passed on. A mutation ... is the exception.": small-RNA inheritance in worms is research-level. Hedge to "usually not passed on" and "the main exception".
17. chemical-bonding, "Ionic solids conduct ... but not as solids": solid ionic conductors (alpha-AgI) are rare. Hedge to typical ionic solids.
18. chemical-reactions, "The hydrogen of the fuel still forms water.": partial oxidation to H2 is an industrial process, not school incomplete combustion. Hedge with "usually".
19. earths-motions, "We see the stars only when the sky is dark": total eclipses. Hedge to "usually".
20. work-energy-power, efficiency "A real machine wastes some energy ... below 100%": a resistance heater's useful output is heat. Limit to machines whose useful output is work or motion.

## Downgraded to Minor, no change

21. chemical-bonding, CO dipole direction: the sentence describes bond polarity, the standard model; the CO molecular dipole is a known anomaly outside the curriculum.
22. chemical-reactions, combustion "with oxygen": the school and exam definition. Burning in fluorine is outside the curriculum.
23. history-of-the-atom, law of definite proportions and isotopes: Proust's law as every textbook states it; isotope variation is outside the lesson's scope.
24. earths-motions, polar circles and refraction: the standard statement; refraction shifts the boundary by under 1 degree.

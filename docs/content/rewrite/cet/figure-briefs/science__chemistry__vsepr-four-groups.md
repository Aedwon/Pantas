## 02-four-groups-make-three-molecular-shapes.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/chemistry/vsepr-four-groups/02-four-groups-make-three-molecular-shapes.svg`
- Marker: three ball-and-stick models side by side, CH4 tetrahedral with 109.5 degree H-C-H angle, NH3 trigonal pyramidal with one lone pair on N, H2O bent with two lone pairs on O
- Purpose: Compare the shapes of CH₄, NH₃ and H₂O.
- Layout: Use three ball-and-stick panels. For CH₄ and NH₃ use a solid wedge toward the viewer and a broken bond away from the viewer to show the three-dimensional arrangement. CH₄ has four hydrogen bonds and the ideal three-dimensional H–C–H angle of 109.5°. NH₃ has three hydrogen bonds and one lone pair. H₂O has two bonds and two lone pairs.
- Labels (verbatim): "H"; "C"; "CH₄"; "N"; "NH₃"; "O"; "H₂O"; "109.5°"; "tetrahedral"; "trigonal"; "pyramidal"; "bent"
- Focus (accent): The lone pairs and the ideal tetrahedral angle.
- Must not show: A bond to a lone pair or angles for NH₃ and H₂O.
- Suggested height: 264

# Figure briefs: flower parts

Lesson: `content/lessons/science/biology/flower-parts.md`. Folder: `content/illustrations/science/biology/flower-parts/`.

## 01-flower-cross-section-with-labeled-parts.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/biology/flower-parts/01-flower-cross-section-with-labeled-parts.svg`
- Marker: cross-section of one flower, with these parts labeled: petal, sepal, stamen (made of anther and filament), pistil (made of stigma, style and ovary), and an ovule inside the ovary
- Purpose: Locate the parts and show how the stamen and pistil are grouped.
- Layout: Draw petals around the outside and sepals at the base. Show two thin filaments topped with anthers. At the center draw a stigma on a style with an enlarged ovary at its base and an ovule inside. Lead labels to the individual parts. Use a left brace for the stamen and a right brace around the labeled parts of the pistil.
- Labels (verbatim): "petal"; "sepal"; "anther"; "filament"; "stamen"; "stigma"; "style"; "ovary"; "ovule"; "pistil"
- Focus (accent): The cutaway of the pistil.
- Must not show: Pollen transfer, fertilization, or labels describing the jobs of the parts.
- Suggested height: 300

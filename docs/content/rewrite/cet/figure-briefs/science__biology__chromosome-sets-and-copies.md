## 01-one-chromosome-before-and-after-copying.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/biology/chromosome-sets-and-copies/01-one-chromosome-before-and-after-copying.svg`
- Marker: one chromosome drawn as a single rod before copying, then the same chromosome after copying as two identical rods (labeled "sister chromatid") joined at a dot labeled "centromere", with the label "1 chromosome, 2 chromatids"
- Purpose: Show why copying creates two chromatids while the chromosome count stays at one.
- Layout: Show one upright rod on the left. At right, use two mirror-image rods joined at their middle by one dot. Lead the centromere label to the dot and the sister chromatid label to one rod. Put the count below the joined pair.
- Labels (verbatim): "centromere"; "sister chromatid"; "1 chromosome, 2 chromatids"
- Focus (accent): The shared centromere dot and label.
- Must not show: Homolog labels or separated chromatids.

## 02-homologous-chromosome-pairs-before-and-after.svg

- File: `content/illustrations/science/biology/chromosome-sets-and-copies/02-homologous-chromosome-pairs-before-and-after.svg`
- Marker: a cell with 2n = 4. Two homologous pairs, one long pair and one short pair. In each pair one chromosome is colored red (from the mother) and one is blue (from the father). Show each pair before copying (4 single rods) and after copying (4 chromosomes, each with 2 sister chromatids), with labels "homologous pair" and "sister chromatids"
- Purpose: Distinguish a pair of homologous chromosomes from the two copies of one chromosome.
- Layout: Draw two cell outlines. Before copying show a long red and blue pair above a short red and blue pair. After copying show the matching four chromosomes in the same positions and sizes, each with two same-color rods joined at a dot. Bracket the long left pair and both chromatids of the short blue chromosome on the right.
- Labels (verbatim): "before copying"; "after copying"; "homologous pair"; "sister chromatids"; "2n = 4"
- Focus (accent): The bracket linking the two long homologs. Use the marker’s fixed red and blue for parent of origin.
- Must not show: Crossing over, chromosome separation, or added counts.
- Suggested height: 292

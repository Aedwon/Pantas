- Marker: meiosis of a cell with 2n = 4, in four rows: (1) after copying, one cell with 2 homologous pairs, red and blue, each chromosome with 2 chromatids; (2) metaphase I, the 2 pairs lined up as pairs; (3) after meiosis I, 2 cells each with 2 chromosomes of 2 chromatids; (4) after meiosis II, 4 cells each with 2 single-chromatid chromosomes. Label "n = 2" on the final cells
- Purpose: Track chromosomes through both divisions.
- Layout: Use four rows. Put the two copied long and short homologous pairs in one cell. In metaphase I put each pair on opposite sides of the middle line. Keep the same long and short homologs in each daughter cell through both divisions. Use a center crossbar to distinguish the homologs in theme colors. After meiosis I each cell has two copied chromosomes. After meiosis II each cell has two single chromatids.
- Labels (verbatim): "after copying"; "2n = 4"; "metaphase I"; "after meiosis I"; "after meiosis II"; "n = 2"
- Focus (accent): The paired chromosomes at metaphase I.

## Fix these

### 1. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L18|0`
- Rule: L18
- Fix size: medium
- Quote:
> The implementing rules identify circumstances that are not covered by the gift prohibition, including:
- Problem: The learning goal requires keeping gift conditions distinct, but none of the worked examples asks the student to classify a gift, donor relationship, transaction connection, or foreign-government exception.
- Triage reason: Gift exceptions are taught but no example makes the student classify a gift against them, a likely exam item.

### 2. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L02|1`
- Rule: L02
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> Owning, controlling, managing, or accepting specified roles or employment in a private enterprise regulated, supervised, or licensed by their office, unless expressly allowed by law.
- Problem: This is a long sentence packed with uncommon legal and regulatory terms. It is not simple Grade 5 teaching prose and needs to be broken down and explained.
- Triage reason: Statute-copied sentence on outside employment is dense; also vague with 'specified roles', since Sec 7(b)(1) names officer, consultant, counsel, broker, agent, trustee, nominee.

### 3. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L05|1`
- Rule: L05
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> Apply the post-employment restriction under Section 7.
- Problem: This solution lead-in uses the abstract legal phrase "post-employment restriction" without a simple explanation, so the solution language is not at the required simplicity level.
- Triage reason: Post-employment restriction is abstract legal wording in a solution lead-in; say plainly what the rule limits.

### 4. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L25|1`
- Rule: L25
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - Gifts from family members or relatives on family celebrations without an expectation of pecuniary gain or benefit.
- Problem: The condition depends on the uncommon legal term "pecuniary gain or benefit," but the lesson never explains that it means a money or financial benefit.
- Triage reason: Pecuniary gain is an uncommon term; say money or financial benefit.

### 5. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L26|1`
- Rule: L26
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - **Outside employment:** Owning, controlling, managing, or accepting specified roles or employment in a private enterprise regulated, supervised, or licensed by their office, unless expressly allowed by law.
- Problem: The concept is presented as a label plus a compressed rule. The lesson does not explain what the referenced "specified roles" are or how the regulated-enterprise connection makes the prohibition operate.
- Triage reason: Outside employment rule is compressed; naming the roles and what regulated means helps the student apply it.

### 6. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L27|1`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - **Private practice:** Engaging in private professional practice unless authorized by the Constitution or law and the practice does not conflict or tend to conflict with official functions. For 1 year after leaving public office, the professional cannot practice in connection with a matter before the former office.
- Problem: The post-service limitations are incomplete. R.A. 6713 continues the outside-enterprise and recommendation prohibitions for one year after separation, while private practice instead has the distinct one-year former-office matter restriction stated here.
- Triage reason: Verified in Sec 7(b): the one-year ban also continues after separation for the outside-employment and recommendation prohibitions; lesson only says it for practice.

### 1. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L18|1`
- Rule: L18
- Fix size: large
- Quote:
> To help reduce bureaucratic delay, Section 5 gives a practical limit for signatories on official papers and documents. As far as practicable, what is that limit?
- Problem: All three examples are direct recall of a deadline, a one-year period, or a signatory count. They do not cover the many distinct prohibited-act scenarios, annual-report duty, public-access duty, confidential-information cases, or permission and exception conditions, and there is no ramp to application-level exam difficulty.
- Triage reason: All three examples are pure recall; Sec 7 prohibitions, annual report, and public access duties have no worked case or ramp.

### 7. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L27|0`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - **Process documents promptly:** Official papers and documents must be processed and completed within a reasonable time. As far as practicable, they should contain no more than three signatories.
- Problem: The lesson never explains how "reasonable time" is determined. The implementing rules supply criteria and also qualify the three-signatory rule when law or regulation provides otherwise, so students are missing conditions needed to know when the rule applies.
- Triage reason: Sec 5(c) adds a next-in-rank signs-in-absence rule and the implementing rules set reasonable-time rules; one sentence on each fixes the gap.

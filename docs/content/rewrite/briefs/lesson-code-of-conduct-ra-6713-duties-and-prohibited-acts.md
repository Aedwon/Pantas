### 11. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L26|1`

- Problem: The concept is presented as a label plus a compressed rule. The lesson does not explain what the referenced "specified roles" are or how the regulated-enterprise connection makes the prohibition operate.

### 12. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L27|0`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - **Process documents promptly:** Official papers and documents must be processed and completed within a reasonable time. As far as practicable, they should contain no more than three signatories.
- Problem: The lesson never explains how "reasonable time" is determined. The implementing rules supply criteria and also qualify the three-signatory rule when law or regulation provides otherwise, so students are missing conditions needed to know when the rule applies.
- Triage reason: Sec 5(c) adds a next-in-rank signs-in-absence rule and the implementing rules set reasonable-time rules; one sentence on each fixes the gap.

### 13. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-duties-and-prohibited-acts|L27|1`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> - **Private practice:** Engaging in private professional practice unless authorized by the Constitution or law and the practice does not conflict or tend to conflict with official functions. For 1 year after leaving public office, the professional cannot practice in connection with a matter before the former office.
- Problem: The post-service limitations are incomplete. R.A. 6713 continues the outside-enterprise and recommendation prohibitions for one year after separation, while private practice instead has the distinct one-year former-office matter restriction stated here.
- Triage reason: Verified in Sec 7(b): the one-year ban also continues after separation for the outside-employment and recommendation prohibitions; lesson only says it for practice.

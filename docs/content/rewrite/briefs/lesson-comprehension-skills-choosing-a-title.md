### 3. `lesson-comprehension-skills-choosing-a-title|L27|0`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> If the passage discusses multiple distinct points, ideas, or examples, the title must be broad enough to cover all of them, not just the one mentioned first or most memorably.
- Problem: This shortcut is stated too absolutely. A good title should follow the passage's main emphasis and central point; it does not need to literally cover every supporting example or detail, so the needed limitation is missing.
- Triage reason: A title covers the main idea, not every example; the absolute rule misleads when details are examples.

### 1. `lesson-philippine-constitution-bill-of-rights|L02|0`
- Rule: L02
- Fix size: medium
- Quote:
> Rights of an accused person, including the presumption of innocence, the right to be heard, counsel, information about the accusation, speedy and impartial public trial, confrontation of witnesses, and compulsory process.
- Problem: This is a long, dense sentence containing several unexplained legal expressions. The lesson repeatedly uses similarly difficult terms such as quasi-judicial, reclusion perpetua, habeas corpus, ex post facto, and bills of attainder without Grade 5-level wording or short explanations.
- Triage reason: Dense legal bullets with terms like quasi-judicial and reclusion perpetua; simplify wording.

### 2. `lesson-philippine-constitution-bill-of-rights|L27|0`
- Rule: L27
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> Limits on suspension of the privilege of the writ of habeas corpus.
- Problem: The lesson says limits exist but does not state the constitutional conditions for suspension, including invasion or rebellion and public safety. Comparable conditions and exceptions for several other listed protections are also omitted.
- Triage reason: Sec. 15 allows suspension only in invasion or rebellion when public safety requires it; add this.

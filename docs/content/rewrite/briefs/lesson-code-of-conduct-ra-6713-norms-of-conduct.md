### 3. `lesson-code-of-conduct-ra-6713-norms-of-conduct|B01|0`
- Rule: B01
- Fix size: small
- Quote:
> Section 4 of RA 6713 sets forth eight specific norms of conduct that every public official and employee must observe at all times.
- Problem: The scope is overstated. Section 4(A) says the norms are standards of personal conduct "in the discharge and execution of official duties," not a blanket rule that all eight must be observed "at all times." Source: https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1989/ra_6713_1989.html
- Triage reason: RA 6713 Sec. 4 norms apply in the discharge of official duties, not 'at all times'.

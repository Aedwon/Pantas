# Pantas redesign: approved decisions (as of Oct 5, 2026)

> Copied from the design canvas. Notes about how the canvas boards are drawn ("never delete existing boards", the canvas build note about looping keyframes and "no JS frame swapping") are about the boards, not the app. Board names like `ST_Exam` point to files in `boards/`.

Direction B "Clarity": Apple principles, no liquid glass. Geist everywhere; Source Serif 4 only for math in lessons. Tint #0B7A68, one accent per screen. Floating pill navs. Never delete existing boards. Mobile only: never show scroll bars. Home: the TODAY eyebrow never changes; its link reads "View full plan". Animate changes in place (rings fill, checks pop, rows fold); never fade or reload a whole screen to show a state change. Build note: the canvas runtime does not play CSS transitions from state changes, so animate a flow as ONE static screen with looping CSS @keyframes per moving part (a pure CSS timeline). No JS frame swapping. Paused = finished state.

## Exam date edge rules (approved 1 Oct)
- Exam today: allowed; goes to the exam-day screen. Under a week away: allowed, one calm line ("We'll focus on what matters most.").
- Last day of a month / 31 Dec: rows that are fully past are hidden; past days greyed; today marked "Today".
- No year limit (approved). Leap years: Feb 2028 has 29.
- Switching month always clears the day. Coming back to the step restores year, month and day as left.
- Prefilled date already passed: skip the "Is this your exam day?" card, open the picker.
- Study days must include at least one day before the exam; otherwise Continue is off and the card says which days to add.
- Multi-day exams: ask for the first day. Large text: day chips grow taller, never clip.

## Mock exam screens (APPROVED 3 Oct; page "Quiz", rows "Mock exam screens" and "Mock exam · All questions")
- Before you start: the exam's parts in a table (Section, Items, Time) with "250 total items · 3 hr 50 min" on the total row; how it runs is a note under the table; what the app can't include (essays, a personality test) is said there too. - While the clock runs: the clock centered at the top with a thick time bar under it (amber under 5 minutes, red in the last minute), pause on the right (gone in strict mode); the part is named above the question. - Last question of a part: › stays; tapping it asks "End Part 3 now?" (strict mode: dimmed, with a quiet line). Ending early loses the leftover time.
- Time is up (or a part ended early): answered and blank dots only (no flags), and the parts list with an arrow at where you are. The clock stops between parts.
- Pause (strict off): first a warning, "Real exams don't pause.", with "Don't show this again"; then the big hourglass falls, stops and shrinks into place; its sand shows the time left. Resume reverses it. Discard: your answers are deleted, it doesn't count and it doesn't use up a mock attempt. Leaving with strict off pauses, then you can discard.
- Strict mode: a 10-second grace period when leaving the app; after that the mock ends there ("Not finished") and everything answered before it is scored. Crashes are found on the next launch and the mock resumes with the clock having kept running.
- The end, as one animation: Time is up, the dots shrink into an answer sheet, the other parts join, a sweep reveals each answer (teal right, light red wrong or blank), the reds fade in place, the greens spiral into one ball that winds back, launches and hits the gauge, the ring fills, the number rolls up, then the rest rises: Performance breakdown bars grow one by one; scrolling down to Versus past results makes Today appear and connect.

## Core Concept

Speed is distance divided by time:

$$v = \dfrac{d}{t}$$

## Examples

### #1: Basic

A car travels $10$ km in $2$ hours. Find the speed.

**Solution:**

1. Substitute into the formula.

$$v = \dfrac{10}{2} = 5$$

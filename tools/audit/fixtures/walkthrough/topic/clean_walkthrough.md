## Core Concept

### Signed Addition Rule

Add two numbers with care.

:::why
Patterns show why the rule works.
:::

:::pair #sample-trap
Right: $5-(-2)=7$
Wrong: $5-(-2)=3$
Change both signs.
:::

:::check
Find $4-(-1)$.
:::answer
The answer is $5$. Subtracting $-1$ adds $1$.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Try Example

Evaluate $8-(-2)$.

**Solution:**

1. Rewrite the subtraction.

:::try
Finish the last step: $8+2$.
:::answer
The answer is $10$.

**Check:** $8$ increases by $2$ to $10$.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Minus before a negative: change both signs. {#sample-trap}
- Signed addition: follow the sign rule. {#signed-addition-rule}
:::

## Core Concept

### Its vs. It's

**Its** shows that something belongs to or is connected with *it*.

- The office changed its hours.

**It's** is a short form of *it is* or *it has*.

- It's open. (It is open.)
- It's been approved. (It has been approved.)

Try both full forms. If neither fits, the sentence needs *its* to show ownership or a connection.

:::pair #owner-apostrophe
Right: The device revised its settings.
Wrong: The device revised it's settings.
*It is settings* does not make sense. The settings belong to the device, so use *its*.
:::

:::pair #extra-apostrophe
Right: The device revised its settings.
Wrong: The device revised its' settings.
The word *its* never takes an apostrophe after the *s*.
:::

:::check
Choose the correct word: The company lost *(its / it's)* license.
:::answer
The answer is *its*. *It is license* does not fit. The license belongs to the company.
:::

### It's as It Is or It Has

*It's* can mean *it is* or *it has*. Expand it to see which one fits.

:::pair #has-not-owner
Right: It's been confirmed that the company lost its license.
Wrong: Its been confirmed that the company lost its license.
*It has been confirmed* is the first clause, so it needs *it's*. The license belongs to the company, so write *its license*.
:::

:::check
In which sentence does *it's* mean *it has*: "It's ready for review" or "It's been delayed"?
:::answer
The answer is "It's been delayed." It expands to "It has been delayed."
:::

## Examples

### #1: Showing Ownership

The university updated ______ guidelines regarding artificial intelligence.

A) it's  
B) its  
C) its'  
D) they're

**Solution:**

1. Try *it is guidelines*. It does not fit. Try *it has guidelines*. It does not fit.
2. The guidelines belong to the university. This calls for *its*.
3. *Its'* is not a standard form, and *they're* means *they are*. Answer: **B**.

The sentence reads: "The university updated its guidelines regarding artificial intelligence."

**Check:** Swap *its* for *the*: "The university updated the guidelines" keeps the pattern, so a possessive word fits.

### #2: A Difficult Week

Choose the pair that completes the sentence correctly: "______ clear that ______ been a difficult week."

A) Its; its  
B) It's; its  
C) Its; it's  
D) It's; it's

**Solution:**

1. The first blank means *it is*: "It is clear."

:::try
Finish the last step: decide the second blank and choose the pair.
:::answer
The answer is **D**, *It's; it's*. The second blank expands to *it has*: "It has been a difficult week." Nothing belongs to anyone, so *its* does not fit.

**Check:** Expand both: "It is clear that it has been a difficult week." Written short: "It's clear that it's been a difficult week."
:::

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

Expand *it's* to *it is* or *it has*. Read the whole sentence after each replacement.

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- A word before a noun that shows ownership: use *its*, with no apostrophe, as in "its settings." {#owner-apostrophe}
- The word *its* with an apostrophe after the *s*: it is not a word, so write *its*. {#extra-apostrophe}
- A gap that means *it is* or *it has*: use *it's*, as in "It's been confirmed." {#has-not-owner}
:::

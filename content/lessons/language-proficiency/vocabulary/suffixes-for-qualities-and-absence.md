## Core Concept

### English Suffixes

A **noun** names a person, place, thing, or idea. An **adjective** describes a noun. An **adverb** can describe how an action happens, and a **verb** tells an action or state.

The suffix **-ful** means "full of" or "having." *Helpful* describes someone or something that gives help, so it is an adjective.

The suffix **-less** means "without." *Careless* describes someone who does not take enough care. It is also an adjective.

:::pair #ful-less
Right: *Helpful* has *-ful*, which means having. *Careless* has *-less*, which means lacking.
Wrong: *Helpful* has *-ful*, which means without. *Careless* has *-less*, which means full of.
The two suffixes are opposites. *-Ful* adds, and *-less* takes away.
:::

:::check
What does the suffix in *hopeless* mean?
:::answer
The answer is without. *Hopeless* means without hope.
:::

The suffix **-ness** makes a noun naming a state or quality.

- *Kind* describes a person.
- *Kindness* names the quality of being kind.

:::pair #ness-noun
Right: In "her kindness," the suffix *-ness* names a quality.
Wrong: In "her kindness," the suffix *-ness* describes a kind person.
*Kindness* is a noun. It names the quality, and it does not describe a person.
:::

## Examples

### #1: A Suffix That Makes a Noun

In "Her **kindness** comforted the child," what does the suffix in the word in bold do?

A) It adds the meaning "not kind."  
B) It forms a noun naming the quality of being kind.  
C) It forms a verb meaning "to become kind."  
D) It forms an adverb telling how an action happens.

**Solution:**

1. *-Ness* makes a noun naming a state or quality.
2. **Kindness** names the quality of being kind. Answer: **B**.

**Check:** Replace it with "the quality of being kind." "The quality of being kind comforted the child" keeps the meaning.

### #2: A Suffix Meaning Without

The family was **homeless** after the fire. Which phrase best gives the meaning of the word in bold?

A) without a home  
B) full of homes  
C) returning home  
D) building a home

**Solution:**

1. The base is *home*, and *-less* means "without."
2. **Homeless** means without a home. Answer: **A**.

**Check:** "The family was without a home after the fire" keeps the meaning.

:::try
What does *fearless* mean?
:::answer
The answer is without fear. The base is *fear*, and *-less* means "without."

**Check:** "A fearless climber" is a climber without fear.
:::

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

Ask what a suffix adds and what job the new word does. A meaning clue and a sentence clue should agree.

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- -ful and -less: -ful means having, and -less means lacking. {#ful-less}
- -ness: it makes a noun that names a quality, not a describing word. {#ness-noun}
:::

## Core Concept

### Using Time Clues

**Chronology** is time order. Dates, clock times and words such as *before*, *after* and *later* show when events happen. A word such as *afterward* shows that a sentence follows an earlier event. The events, not the word, fix its place.

Put in order:

(A) "By evening, the roof was finished."

(B) "At sunrise, the crew began to lay the tiles."

(C) "Two hours after starting, they had covered half the roof."

1. (B) has "At sunrise," the earliest time.
2. (C) says "Two hours after starting," so it counts from the start in (B).
3. (A) has "By evening," the latest time.
At sunrise, the crew began to lay the tiles. Two hours after starting, they had covered half the roof. By evening, the roof was finished.

:::pair #first-before-next
Right: "First, rinse the rice. Next, add water and cook it."
Wrong: "Next, add water and cook it. First, rinse the rice."
The wrong order puts the step marked *Next* ahead of the step marked *First*.
:::

:::check
Put in order: (A) "Three days after the storm, the road reopened." (B) "A storm closed the main road on Monday." (C) "Crews cleared fallen trees on Tuesday."
:::answer
The answer is B, C, A. The storm is Monday and the clearing is Tuesday. Three days after Monday is Thursday. In order: "A storm closed the main road on Monday. Crews cleared fallen trees on Tuesday. Three days after the storm, the road reopened."
:::

### Using Required Steps

In a procedure, ask what must be done before the next step can work.

Put in order:

(A) "Hang the wet shirt on the line."

(B) "Wash the shirt with soap."

(C) "Fold the dry shirt and put it away."

1. "Hang the wet shirt" needs a shirt that is already wet, so it follows (B).
2. "Fold the dry shirt" needs a shirt that has dried, so it follows (A).
Wash the shirt with soap. Hang the wet shirt on the line. Fold the dry shirt and put it away.

:::pair #prerequisite-step
Right: "Unplug the lamp. Then remove the bulb."
Wrong: "Remove the bulb. Then unplug the lamp."
Removing the bulb is safe only after the plug is out.
:::

:::check
Put in order: (A) "Mail the sealed letter." (B) "Seal the envelope." (C) "Put the letter in the envelope."
:::answer
The answer is C, B, A. Sealing needs the letter inside, and mailing needs the envelope sealed. In order: "Put the letter in the envelope. Seal the envelope. Mail the sealed letter."
:::

## Examples

### #1: Ordering a Procedure

Arrange the steps in the best order.

(A) Finally, submit the signed form to the records desk.

(B) Check the completed fields for errors before signing.

(C) First, write your name and contact details in the form's fields.

(D) After checking the form, sign it at the bottom.

**Solution:**

1. (C) fills the fields.
2. (B) follows because it checks those completed fields.
3. (D) follows the check.
4. (A) submits the signed form, so it comes last.

First, write your name and contact details in the form's fields. Check the completed fields for errors before signing. After checking the form, sign it at the bottom. Finally, submit the signed form to the records desk.

**Check:** Each step needs the one before it.

### #2: Ordering Events by Time

Arrange the sentences in time order.

(A) By noon, the missing parcel had reached its owner.

(B) At opening time that morning, the clerk found an unclaimed parcel.

(C) An hour after finding it, she called the owner named on its label.

**Solution:**

1. (B) has "At opening time," the earliest time.
2. (C) counts from the discovery in (B).
3. (A) has "By noon," the latest time.

At opening time that morning, the clerk found an unclaimed parcel. An hour after finding it, she called the owner named on its label. By noon, the missing parcel had reached its owner.

**Check:** Each event follows the last.

### #3: Equipment at the Field

Arrange the sentences in the best order.

(A) Afterward, the team packed the equipment into the van.

(B) Before dawn, the team set up the equipment at the field.

(C) At nine, the team began timing the runners.

**Solution:**

1. (B) has "Before dawn," the earliest clue.

:::try
Finish the last step: put all three in order.
:::answer
The answer is B, C, A. (C) has "At nine," which is later than dawn. *Afterward* follows the timing in (C), because the team packs the equipment after it has used it. In order: "Before dawn, the team set up the equipment at the field. At nine, the team began timing the runners. Afterward, the team packed the equipment into the van."

**Check:** Packing before the timing would mean packing the equipment before it was used.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Markers such as *First* and *Next*: put the step marked *First* before the one marked *Next* (First, then Next). {#first-before-next}
- A step that needs something done earlier: put the needed step first (unplug, then remove). {#prerequisite-step}
- *Afterward* or *later* alone: find the event it follows, because the events set its place (the event, then the word). {#using-time-clues}
:::

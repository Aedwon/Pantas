## Core Concept

### Numbers Below Zero

A **signed integer** is a whole number with a plus or minus sign, such as $5$, $-3$, or $-12$. On a number line, positive numbers sit to the right of $0$ and negative numbers sit to the left.

The **absolute value** of a number is how far it is from $0$. We write it between two bars. A distance is never negative, so an absolute value is never negative.

Find the absolute values of $-6$ and $6$:

$$|-6|=6$$

$$|6|=6$$

Both numbers are $6$ steps from $0$. They only sit on different sides.

<!-- ILLUSTRATION: A number line from -6 to 6 with 0 in the middle. A bracket runs from 0 left to -6 and is labeled "6 steps". Under it is the label |-6| = 6, showing that the absolute value of -6 is its distance from 0. -->

### Adding Two Numbers with the Same Sign

Think of each number as a walk on the number line. A positive number walks right, and a negative number walks left.

When both walks go the same way, the steps add up.

1. Add the absolute values.
2. Keep the sign the two numbers share.

Find $(-5)+(-3)$. You walk $5$ steps left, then $3$ more steps left:

$$5+3=8$$

Both numbers are negative, so keep the minus sign:

$$(-5)+(-3)=-8$$

### Adding Two Numbers with Different Signs

A positive number and a negative number walk in opposite directions. Part of one walk undoes the other.

Start at $0$ and walk $7$ steps left to $-7$. Then walk $4$ steps right.

You stop at $-3$. The $4$ steps right undid $4$ of the $7$ steps left, so $3$ steps left remain.

<!-- ILLUSTRATION: A number line from -8 to 2. A first arrow runs from 0 left 7 steps to -7 and is labeled "-7". A second arrow runs from -7 right 4 steps to -3 and is labeled "+4". The point -3 is circled, showing the walk stops at -3. -->

1. Ignore the signs. Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one.
2. Keep the sign of the number that is farther from $0$.
3. If both numbers are the same distance from $0$, the sum is $0$.

Find $(-7)+4$:

$$7-4=3$$

The number $-7$ is farther from $0$ than $4$, so the answer is negative:

$$(-7)+4=-3$$

Find $9+(-2)$. Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one:

$$9-2=7$$

The number $9$ is farther from $0$, so the answer is positive:

$$9+(-2)=7$$

Find $(-6)+6$. Both numbers are $6$ steps from $0$, so they cancel:

$$(-6)+6=0$$

:::why
Why keep the sign of the number farther from $0$? The longer walk decides which side of $0$ you end on. The shorter walk only undoes part of it.
:::

### Subtracting a Signed Integer

The **opposite** of a nonzero number is the number the same distance from $0$ on the other side. The opposite of $2$ is $-2$, and the opposite of $-2$ is $2$. The opposite of $0$ is $0$.

Subtracting a number gives the same answer as adding its opposite. So you can turn any subtraction into an addition with two changes:

1. Change the minus sign between the numbers to a plus sign.
2. Change the number after it to its opposite.

Change each subtraction to adding the opposite:

$$3-8=3+(-8)=-5$$

$$(-4)-(-9)=(-4)+9=5$$

:::pair #subtract-negative
Right: $5-(-2)=5+2=7$
Wrong: $5-(-2)=5-2=3$
The wrong line made only one change. It kept the minus sign between the numbers and dropped the minus sign on $2$. When you turn a subtraction into an addition, make both changes.
:::

:::why
Look at the pattern: $5-2=3$, then $5-1=4$, then $5-0=5$. Each time you take away $1$ less, the answer goes up by $1$. Keep going to get $5-(-1)=6$ and $5-(-2)=7$.
:::

:::check
Find $(-8)-(-5)+(-10)$.
:::answer
The answer is $-13$. Change the subtraction first: $(-8)+5=-3$. Then $(-3)+(-10)$ has two negative numbers, so add $3+10=13$ and keep the minus sign.
:::

### Multiplying and Dividing

Work out the number first. Then decide the sign. For nonzero numbers:

- Same signs give a positive answer: $(-4)\times(-5)=20$ and $(-12)\div(-3)=4$.
- Different signs give a negative answer: $(-4)\times5=-20$ and $12\div(-3)=-4$.

:::pair #quotient-sign
Right: $(-28)\div7=-4$
Wrong: $(-28)\div7=4$
The signs are different, so the answer must be negative. The wrong line worked out the number and forgot the sign.
:::

When you multiply more than two nonzero numbers, count the negative signs. An even count gives a positive answer. An odd count gives a negative answer. If any factor is $0$, the product is $0$.

Multiply $(-2)\times(-3)\times(-1)$:

$$(-2)\times(-3)\times(-1)=-6$$

There are three negative signs. Three is odd, so the answer is negative.

:::why
Look at the pattern: $2\times(-4)=-8$, then $1\times(-4)=-4$, then $0\times(-4)=0$. Each answer is $4$ more than the one before. Keep going to get $(-1)\times(-4)=4$ and $(-2)\times(-4)=8$.
:::

One more rule: you can never divide by $0$. An expression such as $5\div0$ has no answer. We say it is **undefined**.

## Examples

"Evaluate" means find the final number answer.

### #1: Subtracting a Negative Product

Evaluate $18-6\times(-3)+8\div(-2)$.

**Solution:**

1. Multiply and divide first, from left to right. Leave adding and subtracting for later.

$$6\times(-3)=-18$$

The signs are different, so the product is negative.

$$8\div(-2)=-4$$

The signs are different, so the answer is negative.

2. Put each answer back where its operation was. Keep every sign that sat between the parts.

$$18-6\times(-3)+8\div(-2)=18-(-18)+(-4)$$

3. Subtracting $-18$ is the same as adding $18$.

$$18-(-18)+(-4)=18+18+(-4)$$

4. Add from left to right.

$$18+18=36$$

$$36+(-4)=32$$

The answer is $32$.

### #2: A Negative Start

Evaluate $-15-(-4)\times3+(-24)\div(-6)$.

**Solution:**

1. Multiply and divide first.

$$(-4)\times3=-12$$

The signs are different, so the product is negative.

$$(-24)\div(-6)=4$$

The signs are the same, so the answer is positive.

2. Put each answer back. Keep the signs between the parts.

$$-15-(-4)\times3+(-24)\div(-6)=-15-(-12)+4$$

3. Subtracting $-12$ is the same as adding $12$.

$$-15-(-12)+4=-15+12+4$$

4. Add $-15+12$. The signs are different, so subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one.

$$15-12=3$$

The number $-15$ is farther from $0$, so the answer is negative.

$$-15+12=-3$$

:::try
Finish the last step: $-3+4$.
:::answer
The answer is $1$. The signs are different, so subtract $4-3=1$. The number $4$ is farther from $0$, so the answer is positive.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- A minus sign right before a negative number: change the minus to a plus and the number to its opposite, so $18-(-18)=18+18=36$. {#subtract-negative}
- Different signs in a product or quotient: the answer is negative, so $(-28)\div7=-4$. {#quotient-sign}
- Adding numbers with different signs: take the sign of the number farther from $0$, so $(-7)+4=-3$. {#adding-two-numbers-with-different-signs}
:::

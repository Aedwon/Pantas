## Core Concept

Several useful properties describe addition and multiplication of real numbers: the **commutative**, **associative**, **distributive**, **identity**, and **inverse** properties.

**1. Commutative Property**
You can swap the order of terms or factors in addition and multiplication.

Addition: $a+b=b+a$

Multiplication: $ab=ba$

**2. Associative Property**
You can change grouping in repeated addition or repeated multiplication without changing the value.

Addition: $(a+b)+c=a+(b+c)$

Multiplication: $(ab)c=a(bc)$

**3. Distributive Property**
Multiplication distributes over addition and subtraction.

$$a(b+c)=ab+ac$$

$$a(b-c)=ab-ac$$

You can also read the distributive property backward. If two products have a **common factor**, meaning the same factor appears in both products, factor it out.

$$ab+ac=a(b+c)$$

**4. Identity Property**
The additive identity is $0$ because adding $0$ keeps a number unchanged.

$$a+0=a$$

The multiplicative identity is $1$ because multiplying by $1$ keeps a number unchanged.

$$a\times1=a$$

**5. Inverse Property**
The additive inverse of $a$ is $-a$ because they add to the additive identity $0$.

$$a+(-a)=0$$

For $a\ne0$, the multiplicative inverse of $a$ is $\dfrac{1}{a}$ because their product is the multiplicative identity $1$.

$$a\times\dfrac{1}{a}=1$$

Zero has no multiplicative inverse because division by zero is undefined.

### Like Terms

A **term** is a number, a variable, or a product of numbers and variables that is separated from other terms by addition or subtraction. In $8x-3x+5$, the terms are $8x$, $-3x$, and $5$.

**Like terms** have the same variable part with the same exponents. The number multiplying the variable part is the **coefficient**.

To combine like terms, add or subtract their coefficients and keep the variable part unchanged.


For example, $8x-3x=5x$. The terms $8x$ and $3y$ are not like terms because their variable parts are different.

In exam questions, **simplify** means write an equivalent expression in a simpler form. **Evaluate** means find the final number answer.

## Examples

### #1: Identifying the Basic Property

Which of the following illustrates the commutative property of multiplication?

- A. $3\times(4\times5)=(3\times4)\times5$
- B. $3\times4=4\times3$
- C. $3\times(4+5)=(3\times4)+(3\times5)$
- D. $3\times1=3$

**Solution:**

1. Choice A changes grouping, so it is associative.
2. Choice B swaps the order of the factors, so it is commutative.
3. Choice C is distributive.
4. Choice D uses the multiplicative identity.
5. The answer is B.

### #2: Inverse Trap

What is the additive inverse of the multiplicative inverse of $-\dfrac{2}{3}$?

- A. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- B. $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- C. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- D. $-\dfrac{2}{3}$

**Solution:**

1. The multiplicative inverse of $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ is $-\dfrac{3}{2}$.
2. The additive inverse of $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
3. The answer is A.

### #3: Using Identity Properties

Simplify $(48+0)\times1+25$.

**Solution:**

1. Use the additive identity inside the parentheses.

$$48+0=48$$

2. Use the multiplicative identity.

$$48\times1=48$$

3. Add the remaining term.

$$48+25=73$$

### #4: Simplifying with the Distributive Property

Simplify $4(2x-3y)-2(x+5y)$.

- A. $6x-22y$
- B. $6x-2y$
- C. $8x-22y$
- D. $8x+2y$

**Solution:**

1. Distribute through both grouped expressions.

$$4(2x-3y)=8x-12y$$

$$-2(x+5y)=-2x-10y$$

2. Combine like terms. The $x$-terms have the same variable part, so subtract their coefficients. Do the same for the $y$-terms.

$$8x-12y-2x-10y=6x-22y$$

3. The answer is A.

### #5: Reverse Distribution

Evaluate $847\times63+847\times37$.

- A. $84,700$
- B. $8,470$
- C. $847,000$
- D. $100,000$

**Solution:**

1. Both products have the common factor $847$. Use the distributive property backward and factor it out.

$$847(63+37)$$

2. Add inside the parentheses.

$$63+37=100$$

3. Multiply.

$$847\times100=84,700$$

4. The answer is A.

### #6: Zero Has No Multiplicative Inverse

Which statement is correct?

- A. The multiplicative inverse of $0$ is $0$.
- B. The multiplicative inverse of $0$ is $1$.
- C. The number $0$ has no multiplicative inverse.
- D. Every real number has a multiplicative inverse.

**Solution:**

1. A multiplicative inverse of $a$ must multiply by $a$ to make $1$.
2. No real number multiplied by $0$ equals $1$.
3. Therefore, $0$ has no multiplicative inverse. The answer is C.

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

**Reverse Distribution.**
When two products share a common factor, factor it out before doing the arithmetic.

$$847\times63+847\times37=847(63+37)$$

**Cancel Additive Inverses.**
In a sum, terms such as $5$ and $-5$ combine to $0$.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Confusing Commutative and Associative

> Commutative changes order. Associative changes grouping.

$$(1+2)+3=(2+1)+3$$

uses commutativity, while

$$(1+2)+3=1+(2+3)$$

uses associativity.

### #2: Applying the Rules to Subtraction and Division

> Subtraction and division are not commutative or associative in general.

For example, $5-3\ne3-5$ and $10\div2\ne2\div10$. Do not swap or regroup these operations as if they were addition or multiplication.

### #3: Dropping the Negative Sign

> A negative factor must multiply every term inside the parentheses.

For example:

$$-2(x-5)=-2x+10$$

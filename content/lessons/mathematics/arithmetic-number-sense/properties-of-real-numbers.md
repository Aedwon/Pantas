## Core Concept

There are rules for how numbers act when you add or multiply them. These rules are called the properties of real numbers. They are the **commutative**, **associative**, **distributive**, **identity**, and **inverse** properties.

Philippine college entrance exams love to test these. They want to see if you can find the shortcut. If you know these rules, you do not have to compute large numbers. You can solve by just looking at the pattern.

Here are the five rules:

**1. Commutative Property (Moving)**
You can swap the order of numbers. The answer stays the same. This only works for addition and multiplication.
Addition: $a + b = b + a$
Multiplication: $a \times b = b \times a$

**2. Associative Property (Grouping)**
You can change the grouping. The parenthesis moves, but the numbers stay in the same place. The answer stays the same. This only works for addition and multiplication.
Addition: $(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)$
Multiplication: $(a \times b) \times c = a \times (b \times c)$

**3. Distributive Property (Sharing)**
You multiply a number outside the parenthesis to everything inside.
$a \times (b + c) = (a \times b) + (a \times c)$

**4. Identity Property (Staying the Same)**
What do you add or multiply to keep a number the same?
Additive Identity is $0$. ($a + 0 = a$)
Multiplicative Identity is $1$. ($a \times 1 = a$)

**5. Inverse Property (Canceling)**
What do you add to get $0$? What do you multiply to get $1$?
Additive Inverse is the negative of the number. ($a + (-a) = 0$)
Multiplicative Inverse is the reciprocal (the flipped fraction). ($a \times \dfrac{1}{a} = 1$)

## Mnemonics & Memory Aids

> **C**ommutative = **C**ommute (Travel/Move)
> When people commute, they move. The numbers swap places.

> **A**ssociative = **A**ssociate (Group/Friends)
> Who do you associate with? You form a group. The parenthesis (the group) changes.

> **D**istributive = **D**istribute (Like playing cards)
> You distribute the outside number to every inside number.

> **I**dentity = **I** (Me)
> The number looks in the mirror and stays itself. Add $0$, or multiply by $1$.

> Inverse = Reverse
> You do the reverse to cancel the number out. Add the negative to get $0$. Multiply by the flipped fraction to get $1$.

## Examples

### #1: Identifying the Basic Property

Which of the following illustrates the commutative property of multiplication?
- A. $3 \times (4 \times 5) = (3 \times 4) \times 5$
- B. $3 \times 4 = 4 \times 3$
- C. $3 \times (4 + 5) = (3 \times 4) + (3 \times 5)$
- D. $3 \times 1 = 3$

**Solution:**

1. Commutative means commuting or moving. The numbers must swap places.
2. Choice A changes the parenthesis. That is associative.
3. Choice B swaps $3$ and $4$. This is the commutative property.
4. The answer is B.

### #2: Inverse Trap

What is the additive inverse of the multiplicative inverse of $-\dfrac{2}{3}$?
- A. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- B. $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- C. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- D. $-\dfrac{2}{3}$

**Solution:**

1. Take it one step at a time. Read from right to left.
2. First, find the multiplicative inverse of $-\dfrac{2}{3}$.
3. Multiplicative inverse means flip the fraction. Keep the sign. It becomes $-\dfrac{3}{2}$.
4. Now, find the additive inverse of that result.
5. Additive inverse means change the sign. The negative becomes positive.
6. The answer is $\dfrac{3}{2}$. The correct choice is A.

### #3: Simplifying with the Distributive Property

Simplify the expression: $4(2x - 3y) - 2(x + 5y)$
- A. $6x - 22y$
- B. $6x - 2y$
- C. $8x - 22y$
- D. $8x + 2y$

**Solution:**

1. Use the distributive property. Deal the outside number to the inside terms.
2. For the first part: $4$ times $2x$ is $8x$. $4$ times $-3y$ is $-12y$.

$$4(2x - 3y) = 8x - 12y$$


3. For the second part, deal the $-2$: $-2$ times $x$ is $-2x$. $-2$ times $5y$ is $-10y$.

$$-2(x + 5y) = -2x - 10y$$


4. Put them together and combine like terms.

$$(8x - 12y) - 2x - 10y$$


$$8x - 2x = 6x$$


$$-12y - 10y = -22y$$


5. The final answer is $6x - 22y$. The correct choice is A.

### #4: The DOST-SEI Mental Math Pattern

Evaluate: $847 \times 63 + 847 \times 37$
- A. $84,700$
- B. $8,470$
- C. $847,000$
- D. $100,000$

**Solution:**

1. Do not compute this the long way. Look for a pattern.
2. Both parts share the number $847$.
3. This is the distributive property in reverse. You can pull out the $847$.

$$847 \times (63 + 37)$$


4. Add the numbers inside the parenthesis first.

$$63 + 37 = 100$$


5. Now multiply.

$$847 \times 100 = 84,700$$


6. The correct choice is A.

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

**Reverse Distribution**.
When you see two big multiplication problems added together, check if they share a number. If they do, pull that number out. Add the remaining numbers. They usually add up to $10$, $100$, or $1,000$. This makes the final multiplication very easy.

**Cancel Opposites Immediately**.
In long addition problems, find numbers that are additive inverses of each other (like $5$ and $-5$). They equal zero. Cross them out immediately. You do not need to add them.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Confusing Commutative and Associative

Students see a parenthesis and automatically guess "associative."
Look closely at the numbers. Did the numbers swap order, or did only the parenthesis move?
$(1 + 2) + 3 = (2 + 1) + 3$ is commutative because the $1$ and $2$ swapped places.
$(1 + 2) + 3 = 1 + (2 + 3)$ is associative because the grouping moved.

> Always check if the numbers walked to a new spot (commutative) or just formed a new friend group (associative).

### #2: Subtraction and Division have no Properties

Commutative and associative properties only work for addition and multiplication.
$5 - 3$ is not the same as $3 - 5$.
$10 \div 2$ is not the same as $2 \div 10$.

> If a choice uses division or subtraction for commutative or associative rules, it is a trick.

### #3: Dropping the Negative Sign

When you distribute a negative number, you must multiply the negative sign to every term inside the parenthesis. Students often forget to flip the sign on the second term.

> Distributing $-2$ into $(x - 5)$ gives $-2x + 10$, not $-2x - 10$.

## Core Concept

Fractions, decimals, and percentages are three ways to represent the same quantities.

A **fraction** compares a numerator with a denominator, where the denominator is not zero. A **decimal** represents values using powers of $10$. A **percentage** means "per hundred" or "out of 100."

To turn a percentage into a decimal, divide by $100$. To turn a decimal into a percentage, multiply by $100$. To turn a fraction into a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator; then multiply the decimal by $100$ if you need a percentage.

## Mnemonics & Memory Aids

Use the Alphabet Rule to remember which way to move the decimal point. The letter **D** (Decimal) comes before **P** (Percent).

> **D** to **P**: multiply by $100$, so the decimal point moves $2$ places right.

> **P** to **D**: divide by $100$, so the decimal point moves $2$ places left.

The percent sign ($\%$) can remind you that percent means "per 100."

## Examples

### #1: Basic Conversion

Express $0.125$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

**Solution:**

1. The last digit is in the thousandths place, so:

$$0.125 = \dfrac{125}{1000}$$

2. Divide numerator and denominator by $125$.

$$\dfrac{125}{1000} = \dfrac{1}{8}$$

### #2: Mixed Operations

Evaluate $\dfrac{3}{4} + 0.50 - 25\%$ and express the answer as a decimal.

**Solution:**

1. Convert the terms to decimals.

$$\dfrac{3}{4}=0.75$$

$$25\%=0.25$$

2. Evaluate from left to right.

$$0.75 + 0.50 - 0.25 = 1.00$$

3. The answer is $1.00$.

### #3: "Of" Translation

If $40\%$ of a number is $72$, what is $15\%$ of the same number?

**Solution:**

1. Let $x$ be the number.

$$0.40x = 72$$

2. Solve for $x$.

$$x = \dfrac{72}{0.40} = 180$$

3. Find $15\%$ of $180$.

$$0.15 \times 180 = 27$$

4. The answer is $27$.

### #4: Complex Fraction to Percent

What percent of $\dfrac{5}{8}$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$?

**Solution:**

1. Let $P$ be the decimal form of the unknown percent.

$$P \times \dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}$$

2. Divide by $\dfrac{5}{8}$.

$$P = \dfrac{1}{4} \times \dfrac{8}{5} = \dfrac{2}{5}=0.4$$

3. Convert to a percentage.

$$0.4 \times 100\% = 40\%$$

### #5: Successive Percentages

A retail store applies a $20\%$ discount on a jacket. During a clearance sale, an additional $25\%$ discount is applied to the discounted price. What is the total equivalent percentage discount from the original price?

**Solution:**

1. Use $100$ as a convenient original price.
2. After a $20\%$ discount, $80$ remains.
3. A $25\%$ discount on $80$ removes:

$$0.25 \times 80 = 20$$

4. The final price is $60$.
5. The total drop from the original $100$ is $40$, so the equivalent discount is $40\%$.

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

**The Percent Switcheroo.**
For real numbers $x$ and $y$, $x\%$ of $y$ equals $y\%$ of $x$ because both equal $\dfrac{xy}{100}$. For example, $16\%$ of $25$ equals $25\%$ of $16$, which is $4$.

**Memorize Common Fractions.**

* $\dfrac{1}{2} = 50\%$
* $\dfrac{1}{3} = 33\dfrac{1}{3}\% \approx 33.3\%$
* $\dfrac{1}{4} = 25\%$
* $\dfrac{1}{5} = 20\%$
* $\dfrac{1}{8} = 12.5\%$

**Find $10\%$ First.**
To find $15\%$ of a number mentally, find $10\%$, then half of that amount for $5\%$, and add them. For $80$, those amounts are $8$ and $4$, so $15\%$ of $80$ is $12$.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Adding Discounts Together

> Do not simply add successive discounts.

A $20\%$ discount followed by a $30\%$ discount is applied to two different base amounts, so it is not a $50\%$ total discount.

### #2: Moving the Decimal Point Only Once

> $5\%$ is $0.05$, not $0.50$.

Dividing a percent by $100$ moves the decimal point two places left.

### #3: Confusing "Percent Of" with "Percent More"

> "$120\%$ of a number" is not the same as "$120\%$ more than a number."

If a salary is $120\%$ of last year's salary, multiply by $1.20$. If it is $120\%$ more than last year's salary, the new amount is $220\%$ of the original, so multiply by $2.20$.

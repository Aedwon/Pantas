## Core Concept

### Multiplying Into Parentheses

The **distributive property** lets you multiply a sum or difference one term at a time. Think of $4$ gift bags, each holding $3$ pens and $7$ stickers. Together, you have $4\times 3=12$ pens and $4\times 7=28$ stickers.

Multiply the outside factor by *every* term inside the parentheses, as in $4(3x+7)$:

$$4(3x+7)=4(3x)+4(7)$$

$$4(3x)+4(7)=12x+28$$

Picture a rectangle $4$ tall and $3x+7$ wide. Here $x$ is a positive length.

<!-- ILLUSTRATION: a rectangle 4 tall and 3x + 7 wide, cut into two parts. The left part is 3x wide and has area 4 × 3x = 12x. The right part is 7 wide and has area 4 × 7 = 28. The whole rectangle is 4(3x + 7) = 12x + 28, so the 4 multiplies each part and not only the first. The widths are not drawn to scale. -->

In symbols:

$$a(b+c)=ab+ac$$

$$a(b-c)=ab-ac$$

:::pair #first-term-only
Right: $4(3x+7)=12x+28$
Wrong: $4(3x+7)=12x+7$
The wrong line multiplied only $3x$ by $4$ and left $7$ alone. Multiply every term inside the parentheses by the outside factor.
:::

### Distributing a Negative Factor

When the outside factor is negative, keep its minus sign for every product inside. Multiplying two negative numbers gives a positive product, and multiplying a negative by a positive gives a negative product.

Find $-2(x-5)$:

$$-2(x-5)=(-2)(x)+(-2)(-5)$$

$$(-2)(x)+(-2)(-5)=-2x+10$$

Find $-3(2x-4y)$:

$$-3(2x-4y)=(-3)(2x)+(-3)(-4y)$$

$$(-3)(2x)+(-3)(-4y)=-6x+12y$$

:::pair #negative-times-negative
Right: $-2(x-5)=-2x+10$
Wrong: $-2(x-5)=-2x-10$
Here $-2$ multiplies $-5$. Same signs give $+10$, not $-10$.
:::

"Expand" means multiply out the parentheses.

:::check
Expand $-4(3a-2b+5)$.
:::answer
The answer is $-12a+8b-20$. Multiply all three terms by $-4$: $(-4)(3a)=-12a$, $(-4)(-2b)=+8b$, and $(-4)(5)=-20$.
:::

### Factoring Out a Common Factor

You can also read the distributive property backward. A **common factor** is a factor that appears in every product.

To **factor out** a common factor, write it once outside parentheses and place the remaining parts inside.

$$ab+ac=a(b+c)$$

Find $17\times 99+17\times 1$. The factor $17$ appears in both parts, so $17\times 99+17\times 1=17(99+1)$.

$$99+1=100$$

$$17\times 100=1,700$$

This works with three or more products too. Pull out $87$ from every part: $87\times (-14)+87\times 114-87\times 100=87(-14+114-100)$.

$$-14+114-100=0$$

$$87\times 0=0$$

:::why
Why does pulling out $17$ work? Buying $99$ notebooks at $17$ pesos each, plus $1$ more at $17$ pesos, costs the same as buying $99+1=100$ notebooks at $17$ pesos each.
:::

:::pair #keep-one-inside
Right: $17(99)+17(1)=17(99+1)$
Wrong: $17(99)+17(1)=17(99)+1$
Both purchases use the $17$-peso price. Leaving $1$ outside forgets that the extra notebook also costs $17$ pesos.
:::

## Examples

In exam questions, "simplify" means write an equal expression in a simpler form, and "evaluate" means find the final number answer.

### #1: Simplifying with the Distributive Property

Simplify $4(2x-3y)-2(x+5y)$.

**Solution:**

1. Multiply $4$ into the first parentheses.

$$4(2x-3y)=8x-12y$$

2. Multiply $-2$ into the second parentheses, keeping the minus sign on both products.

$$-2(x+5y)=-2x-10y$$

3. Put the parts together. The $x$ terms are like terms, and so are the $y$ terms, so group each kind together.

$$8x-12y-2x-10y=(8x-2x)+(-12y-10y)$$

4. Combine the $x$ terms, then the $y$ terms.

$$8-2=6$$

$$-12-10=-22$$

$$(8x-2x)+(-12y-10y)=6x-22y$$

The answer is $6x-22y$.

### #2: Reverse Distribution

Evaluate $847\times 63+847\times 37$.

**Solution:**

1. Factor out the common factor $847$:

$$847\times 63+847\times 37=847(63+37)$$

2. Add inside the parentheses first.

$$63+37=100$$

:::try
Finish the last step: multiply $847\times 100$.
:::answer
The answer is $84,700$. Multiplying by $100$ appends two zeros.
:::

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

When products share a common factor, factor it out before multiplying if the leftover numbers add or subtract to $10$, $100$, or $0$. That makes the multiplication quick.

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Multiplying a number into parentheses: multiply every term inside, not only the first, so $4(3x+7)=12x+28$. {#first-term-only}
- A negative factor outside parentheses: multiply every term inside the parentheses by that negative factor, so $-2(x-5)=-2x+10$. {#negative-times-negative}
- Factoring out a common factor when a term equals that factor: keep $1$ inside the parentheses, so $17(99)+17(1)=17(99+1)$. {#keep-one-inside}
:::

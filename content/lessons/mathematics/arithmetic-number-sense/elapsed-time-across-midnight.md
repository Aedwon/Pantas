## Core Concept

For an end on the next day, you can add $24$ to its $24$-hour clock hour before subtracting. That larger hour is for the calculation. Change back to a clock time when naming a time of day.

## Examples

### #1: Elapsed Time Across Midnight

A bus leaves at $10{:}50$ pm on Friday and arrives at $1{:}35$ am on Saturday. How long is the trip?

**Solution:**

1. Count from $10{:}50$ pm to $11{:}00$ pm.

$$60-50=10\text{ minutes}$$

2. Count whole hours from $11{:}00$ pm to $1{:}00$ am. Reaching midnight takes $1$ hour. Reaching $1{:}00$ am takes another $1$ hour.

$$1+1=2\text{ hours}$$

3. Count the last $35$ minutes. Add the minute parts.

$$10+35=45\text{ minutes}$$

The trip lasts $2$ hours $45$ minutes.

4. Check with $24$-hour time. The start is $22{:}50$.

   The end is $01{:}35$ on the next day. Add $24$ to the ending hour for the subtraction.

$$1+24=25$$

5. Use $25$ hours $35$ minutes as the end. Borrow $1$ hour.

$$25\text{ h }35\text{ min}=24\text{ h }95\text{ min}$$

$$95-50=45\text{ minutes}$$

$$24-22=2\text{ hours}$$

This confirms $2$ hours $45$ minutes. The calculation hour $25$ does not belong on the arrival clock. The arrival clock still reads $01{:}35$.

## Core Concept

### Turning Age Facts Into Equations

Word problems give age facts in sentences. Turn each fact into math, solve the equation, and check what the question asks.

Use this four-step plan:

1. Pick a letter for one unknown age and say what it means.
2. Write each person's age using that letter.
3. Solve the equation.
4. Read the question again to match your number to the right person and time.

Try this example: find Maya's age from the total and the gap. Ben is $4$ years older than his sister Maya, and the sum of their ages is $24$. Let $m$ be Maya's age now. Then Ben is $m+4$.

$$m+(m+4)=24$$

$$2m+4=24$$

$$2m=20$$

$$m=10$$

Maya is $10$ years old now, and Ben is $10+4=14$ years old.

:::pair #wrong-person-or-time
Right: the question asks for Maya's age, so the answer is $m=10$ years old.
Wrong: Ben's age is $10+4=14$, so the answer is $14$ years old.
Match your final answer to the exact person and time in the question. Here $m$ stands for Maya, while $14$ is Ben's age.
:::

### Shifting Ages Forward and Back

Time passes at the same rate for everyone. If Ana is $a$ years old now, then in $6$ years she will be $a+6$. Every other person's age also goes up by $6$ in those $6$ years.

For an age $4$ years ago, subtract $4$ from every person's age.

Try this problem: find Ana's age now from the two age ratios. A mother is $4$ times as old as her daughter Ana. In $6$ years, the mother will be $3$ times as old as Ana. Let $a$ be Ana's age now, so her mother is $4a$. Both ages gain $6$ years:

$$4a+6=3(a+6)$$

$$4a+6=3a+18$$

Subtract $3a$ from both sides:

$$a+6=18$$

Subtract $6$ from both sides:

$$a=12$$

Ana is $12$ years old now.

:::why
Why does the gap between two ages stay the same? Each year adds $1$ to both people, so $(4a+6)-(a+6)=4a-a=3a$. The ratio changes as time passes.
:::

:::pair #change-both-ages
Right: $4a+6=3(a+6)$, so $a=12$
Wrong: $4a=3(a+6)$, so $a=18$
The wrong equation added $6$ to Ana's age and forgot the mother. Time passes for both people, so add $6$ to each person's age before applying the new ratio.
:::

:::check
Carlo is $c$ years old now, and his father is $56-c$. What will the sum of their ages be in $7$ years?
:::answer
The answer is $70$. Add $7$ to each age: $(c+7)+(56-c+7)=c+7+63-c=70$.
:::

### Checking Which Person and Time Are Asked

Solving for the letter often gives the younger person's age, or an age at another time. Always check the last question before choosing an answer.

Suppose $b$ is Ben's age now, $b=6$, and Ana is $3b$. Find Ana's age in $4$ years: put $6$ in for $b$, then add $4$ years.

$$3(6)+4$$

$$=18+4$$

$$=22$$

Ana will be $22$ years old in $4$ years.

## Examples

### #1: Future Age Ratio

Ana is $3$ times as old as Ben. In $6$ years, Ana will be twice as old as Ben. How old is Ana now?

**Solution:**

1. Let $b$ be Ben's age now. Then Ana's age now is $3b$.
2. Add $6$ to both ages to write the relationship in $6$ years.

$$3b+6=2(b+6)$$

3. Multiply both terms inside the parentheses by $2$.

$$3b+6=2b+12$$

4. Subtract $2b$ from both sides.

$$b+6=12$$

5. Subtract $6$ from both sides.

$$b=6$$

6. Find Ana's age now.

$$3(6)=18$$

Ana is $18$ years old now.

### #2: Past and Future Ages

Five years ago, Leo was $4$ times as old as his daughter Mia. In $7$ years, Leo will be twice as old as Mia. How old is Mia today?

**Solution:**

1. Let $m$ be Mia's age today and $L$ be Leo's age today.
2. Subtract $5$ from both ages to write the relationship five years ago.

$$L-5=4(m-5)$$

3. Multiply both terms inside the parentheses by $4$.

$$L-5=4m-20$$

4. Add $5$ to both sides to isolate $L$.

$$L=4m-15$$

5. Add $7$ to both ages to write the relationship seven years from now.

$$L+7=2(m+7)$$

6. Multiply both terms inside the parentheses by $2$.

$$L+7=2m+14$$

7. Subtract $7$ from both sides to isolate $L$.

$$L=2m+7$$

8. Set the two expressions for $L$ equal to each other.

$$4m-15=2m+7$$

9. Subtract $2m$ from both sides.

$$2m-15=7$$

10. Add $15$ to both sides.

$$2m=22$$

:::try
Finish the last step: divide $22$ by $2$ to find Mia's age today.
:::answer
The answer is $11$ years old. Dividing $22\div 2=11$ gives $m=11$.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Time shifts into the future or past: add or subtract the same years for every person, so $4a+6=3(a+6)$. {#change-both-ages}
- Reading the final question: check whose age and which time the question asks for before answering. {#wrong-person-or-time}
:::

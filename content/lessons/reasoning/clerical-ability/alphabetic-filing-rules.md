## Core Concept

Alphabetic filing organizes records according to established indexing rules letter by letter.

### Rules of Indexing

1. **Individual Names:** Inverted as **Unit 1: Last Name**, **Unit 2: First Name**, **Unit 3: Middle Name or Initial**.
   - Example: *Maria Santos Cruz* is indexed as **Cruz, Maria Santos**.
2. **Identical Names:** Arranged alphabetically by city or location if names are identical.
3. **Prefixes:** Surnames with prefixes (such as *De*, *Del*, *De la*, *San*, *Van*) are treated as a single unit without spaces.
   - Example: *De la Cruz* is indexed under **Delacruz**.
4. **Seniority Titles:** Titles like *Jr.*, *Sr.*, *III* are placed in the final unit.

## Examples

### #1: Indexing an Individual Name

How should the name "Juan Antonio Santos" be indexed into units for alphabetic filing?

**Solution:**
Apply Unit 1 (Last Name), Unit 2 (First Name), Unit 3 (Middle Name).

1. Unit 1 is the Last Name: Santos.
2. Unit 2 is the First Name: Juan.
3. Unit 3 is the Middle Name: Antonio.
4. Therefore, the indexed name is Santos, Juan Antonio.

### #2: Handling Surnames with Prefixes

Which comes first alphabetically when filing individual names: "Maria De la Cruz" or "Clara Delgado"?

**Solution:**
Apply the prefix indexing rule.

1. Prefixes such as "De la" are combined into a single indexing unit: "Delacruz".
2. "Clara Delgado" is indexed as "Delgado, Clara".
3. Compare Unit 1 letter by letter: "Delacruz" versus "Delgado".
   - First four letters: D-E-L-A vs D-E-L-G.
   - Letter "a" comes before "g" in the alphabet.
4. Therefore, "De la Cruz, Maria" comes before "Delgado, Clara".

### #3: Sorting Identical Names by Location

Two records have the identical person name "Robert Smith", one located in Chicago and one located in Boston. Which record should be filed first?

**Solution:**
Apply the rule for identical names.

1. When individual names are completely identical, records are arranged alphabetically by city or location.
2. Compare location names: Boston versus Chicago.
3. Letter "B" comes before "C" in the alphabet.
4. Therefore, the record for Robert Smith in Boston is filed before the record for Robert Smith in Chicago.

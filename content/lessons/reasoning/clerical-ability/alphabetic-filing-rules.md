## Core Concept

Alphabetic filing organizes records according to established indexing rules letter by letter.

## Rules of Indexing

1. **Individual Names:** Inverted as **Unit 1: Last Name**, **Unit 2: First Name**, **Unit 3: Middle Name or Initial**.
   - Example: *Maria Santos Cruz* is indexed as **Cruz, Maria Santos**.
2. **Identical Names:** Arranged alphabetically by city or location if names are identical.
3. **Prefixes:** Surnames with prefixes (such as *De*, *Del*, *De la*, *San*, *Van*) are treated as a single unit without spaces.
   - Example: *De la Cruz* is indexed under **Delacruz**.
4. **Seniority Titles:** Titles like *Jr.*, *Sr.*, *III* are placed in the final unit.

## Core Concept

### Climate Change Act and Commission

**RA $9729$** is the **Climate Change Act of $2009$**. Under Section $4$, it created the **Climate Change Commission**, attached to the Office of the President, to guide and coordinate government climate policy.

**Climate change** means lasting shifts in climate patterns over time, not $1$ unusual day. Climate policy uses $2$ main responses:

- **Adaptation:** adjusting practices or structures to reduce harm from climate effects.
- **Mitigation:** cutting human-caused greenhouse gas releases or increasing their removal from air.

Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere. Keep the Climate Change Commission distinct from DENR and the disaster council.

Compare $2$ national agencies:

1. An office links climate plans across agriculture, energy, and health.
2. Another office inspects factory smokestacks and issues water permits.

Result: The Climate Change Commission coordinates climate policy, while DENR handles pollution enforcement.

:::pair #climate-act-vs-disaster-act
Right: Match the Climate Change Commission and climate policy to RA $9729$, the Climate Change Act.
Wrong: Naming the disaster risk reduction act as the law creating the Climate Change Commission.
RA $9729$ governs climate policy and its commission, while RA $10121$ governs disaster management.
:::

:::pair #ccc-leads-climate-policy
Right: Assign RA $9729$ climate-policy guidance and coordination to the Climate Change Commission.
Wrong: Naming DENR as the special commission created by RA $9729$ for climate coordination.
DENR holds broad environmental duties, whereas RA $9729$ created the Climate Change Commission.
:::

:::check
A city sees $1$ record hot afternoon and asks whether $1$ day proves climate change under RA $9729$. Does it?
:::answer
The answer is **No**. Climate change means lasting shifts in climate patterns over time, not $1$ unusual day.
:::

### Original and Amended National Frameworks

Check whether a question cites original **RA $9729$** or its amendment under **RA $10174$**.

Original Section $11$ of RA $9729$ required a **Framework Strategy on Climate Change**. Section $8$ of RA $10174$ amended Section $11$ to require a **National Strategic Framework on Climate Change**. Match the title to the version cited.

The national framework guides climate planning, research, public information, monitoring, and climate financing. It sets risk-based priorities at the national level, never acting as a local building license or household permit.

:::why
Why keep a national climate framework above local permits? National agencies and towns need $1$ shared risk-based plan to align budgets, research, and local action.
:::

Compare $2$ instruments:

1. A national strategy aligns agency research, risk priorities, and climate financing under amended Section $11$.
2. A town issues an ordinary building license for a market stall.

Result: Amended RA $9729$ requires the National Strategic Framework on Climate Change, not a local permit.

:::pair #national-framework-not-local-permit
Right: Identify the National Strategic Framework on Climate Change as the national policy guide under amended RA $9729$.
Wrong: Treating a municipal building license as the country's national climate-change strategy.
Building licenses regulate local construction, while the framework guides national climate policy.
:::

:::check
A $2010$ report cites original RA $9729$ Section $11$, while a $2015$ memo cites RA $10174$ Section $8$. Name the $2$ framework titles.
:::answer
The answer is **Framework Strategy on Climate Change in original RA $9729$ and National Strategic Framework on Climate Change under RA $10174$**. Matching title to version prevents mix-ups.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Matching the Commission and Law

A national memo cites the $2009$ body that coordinates government climate-change policy across departments. Name the body and law.

**Solution:**

1. National climate-policy coordination differs from disaster rescue or pollution permitting.
2. Section $4$ of the $2009$ climate statute created that coordinating commission.

Answer: The Climate Change Commission under RA $9729$, the Climate Change Act of $2009$.

**Check:** Link the Climate Change Commission to RA $9729$ Section $4$.

### #2: Adaptation Versus Mitigation

A town plants mangroves to absorb carbon dioxide and raises its health clinic above future storm tides. Classify both actions.

**Solution:**

1. Absorbing carbon dioxide cuts or removes greenhouse gases, which is mitigation.
2. Raising a clinic lowers expected flood harm, which is adaptation.

Answer: Planting mangroves for carbon storage is mitigation, and raising the clinic is adaptation.

**Check:** Match gas removal to mitigation and harm reduction to adaptation.

### #3: Amended Climate Framework Claim

A planner cites RA $10174$ Section $8$ and names the **Framework Strategy on Climate Change**. The planner also calls it a municipal construction permit. Identify the $2$ errors.

**Solution:**

1. Check the exact framework title required after RA $10174$ Section $8$ amended RA $9729$ Section $11$.
2. Check whether that framework is a local construction permit or a national policy guide.

:::try
Finish the last step: correct the amended framework title and its policy scale.
:::answer
The answer is **RA $10174$ requires the National Strategic Framework on Climate Change, and it is a national policy plan rather than a local permit**. Original RA $9729$ used Framework Strategy on Climate Change, while amended Section $11$ guides national planning and financing.

**Check:** Verify the RA $10174$ Section $8$ title and its national policy scope.
:::

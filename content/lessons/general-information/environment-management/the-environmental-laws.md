## Core Concept

Key Philippine environmental laws examined in the Civil Service exam include several landmark Republic Acts, Presidential Decrees, and primary governing executive agencies.

### Core Environmental Statutes

- **RA 9003:** Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000 (segregation at source, waste diversion, ban on open dumpsites).
- **RA 8749:** Philippine Clean Air Act of 1999 (air quality standards, emission limits, ban on municipal waste incineration).
- **RA 9275:** Philippine Clean Water Act of 2004 (water quality management, wastewater discharge permits).
- **RA 9729:** Climate Change Act of 2009 (creates Climate Change Commission, national framework strategy).
- **RA 10121:** Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010 (creates NDRRMC, shift to risk reduction).
- **PD 1586:** Environmental Impact Statement System (requires Environmental Compliance Certificate / ECC).
- **RA 9147:** Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act (preservation of protected species).
- **RA 7586:** National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS) Act (designation of protected areas).

### Governing Agencies

- **Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR):** The lead executive department enforcing most environmental laws.
- **Climate Change Commission:** Attached to the Office of the President under RA 9729.

## Examples

### #1: Matching Waste Management to Republic Act Number

A municipality is establishing a mandatory waste segregation program and banning open dumpsites. Under which Republic Act is this solid waste management framework enacted?

**Solution:**
Match the subject of ecological solid waste management to its statutory title and number.

1. RA 8749 governs clean air.
2. RA 9275 governs clean water.
3. RA 9003 is the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000.
4. Therefore, the waste segregation program is mandated under RA 9003.

### #2: Identifying the Law Governing Disaster Risk Reduction

Which law shifted the Philippine disaster management framework from reacting after disasters to disaster risk reduction and management?

**Solution:**
Identify the act that established the modern disaster risk framework.

1. RA 10121 is the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010.
2. It created the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC).
3. It established the paradigm shift from post-disaster response to proactive risk reduction.
4. Therefore, the law is RA 10121.

### #3: The Lead Agency for Environmental Laws

Which executive department serves as the lead agency responsible for enforcing most Philippine environmental laws?

**Solution:**
Recall the lead environmental regulatory body.

1. The Climate Change Commission is attached to the Office of the President under RA 9729 for climate strategy.
2. The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) is the primary lead agency enforcing most environmental statutes, including clean air, clean water, and solid waste regulations.
3. Therefore, the lead agency is the DENR.

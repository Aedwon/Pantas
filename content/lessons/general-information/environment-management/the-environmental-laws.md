## Core Concept

The Civil Service Examination's General Information scope includes Environment Management and Protection. These major Philippine environmental laws provide useful legal context for that area.

### Core Environmental Statutes

- **RA 9003:** Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000. It requires measures such as segregation at source and waste diversion and prohibits open dumps.
- **RA 8749:** Philippine Clean Air Act of 1999. It establishes an air-pollution control framework and prohibits incineration, as defined by the Act, of municipal, biomedical, and hazardous wastes when the process emits poisonous and toxic fumes, subject to the Act's stated exceptions.
- **RA 9275:** Philippine Clean Water Act of 2004. It provides a water-quality management framework that includes Water Quality Management Areas and regulation of wastewater discharges.
- **RA 9729:** Climate Change Act of 2009. It created the Climate Change Commission and requires a National Strategic Framework on Climate Change.
- **RA 10121:** Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010. It established the modern disaster risk reduction and management framework and the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC).
- **PD 1586:** Established the Philippine Environmental Impact Statement System. Projects covered by the system must secure the required Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) before implementation.
- **RA 9147:** Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act. It provides for the conservation and protection of wildlife resources and their habitats.
- **RA 7586, as amended by RA 11038:** The National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS) Act of 1992 was amended and expanded by the Expanded NIPAS Act of 2018, which governs the establishment and management of protected areas under the system.

### Governing Agencies

- **Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR):** The national executive department with broad responsibilities for environmental and natural-resources governance. Its Environmental Management Bureau implements major pollution-control and environmental-impact laws, including PD 1586, RA 8749, RA 9003, and RA 9275.
- **Climate Change Commission:** The lead policy-making body on climate change created by RA 9729 and attached to the Office of the President.

## Examples

### #1: Matching Waste Management to Republic Act Number

A municipality is establishing a mandatory waste segregation program and enforcing the prohibition on open dumps. Under which Republic Act is this solid-waste management framework enacted?

**Solution:**
Match the subject of ecological solid-waste management to its statutory title and number.

1. RA 8749 governs air pollution control.
2. RA 9275 governs water quality management.
3. RA 9003 is the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000.
4. Therefore, the waste-management framework is under RA 9003.

### #2: Identifying the Law Governing Disaster Risk Reduction

Which law established the Philippine disaster risk reduction and management framework and the NDRRMC?

**Solution:**
Identify the act that established the modern disaster-risk framework.

1. RA 10121 is the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010.
2. It established the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC).
3. It emphasizes risk reduction through prevention and mitigation, preparedness, response, and rehabilitation and recovery.
4. Therefore, the law is RA 10121.

### #3: Identifying the Environmental Management Bureau's Parent Department

The Environmental Management Bureau implements major pollution-control and environmental-impact laws. Which national executive department is it part of?

**Solution:**
Recall the government structure for environmental regulation.

1. The Environmental Management Bureau is a bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources.
2. It implements major environmental laws including the Clean Air Act, Clean Water Act, Ecological Solid Waste Management Act, and Philippine EIS System.
3. Therefore, the bureau's parent department is the DENR.

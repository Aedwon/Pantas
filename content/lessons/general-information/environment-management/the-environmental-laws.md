## Core Concept

The Civil Service Examination's General Information scope includes Environment Management and Protection. These laws help connect an environmental problem to the rule or agency that handles it.

### Core Environmental Statutes

**RA $9003$: Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of $2000$.** **Segregation at source** means separating waste where it is produced. **Waste diversion** means reducing or removing waste that would otherwise go to disposal facilities, such as through reuse, recycling, composting, and other resource recovery. The law also prohibits open dumps.

**RA $8749$: Philippine Clean Air Act of $1999$.** It controls air pollution. Its incineration ban applies to the burning of municipal, biomedical, and hazardous waste when the process emits poisonous and toxic fumes. The stated exceptions include traditional small-scale community or neighborhood sanitation burning called *siga*, traditional agricultural, cultural, health, and food-preparation burning, and crematoria.

**RA $9275$: Philippine Clean Water Act of $2004$.** It manages water pollution through tools such as Water Quality Management Areas, water-quality standards, and discharge permits. A discharge permit states the quantity and quality of regulated wastewater a facility may release, plus its compliance and monitoring requirements.

**RA $9729$: Climate Change Act of $2009$.** It created the Climate Change Commission. As first enacted, RA $9729$ required a **Framework Strategy on Climate Change** to guide climate planning, research, development, and monitoring. RA $10174$ later amended this part and uses the name **National Strategic Framework on Climate Change**.

**RA $10121$: Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of $2010$.** It set the modern disaster risk reduction and management framework. The existing National Disaster Coordinating Council became known as the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC).

**PD $1586$: Philippine Environmental Impact Statement System.** A **project proponent** is the person, company, or agency that wants to carry out a project. A covered project must go through the required environmental review and secure the required Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) before implementation.

**RA $9147$: Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act.** It protects wildlife resources and habitats. It also provides for critical habitats outside protected areas where threatened species are found.

**RA $7586$, as amended by RA $11038$: National Integrated Protected Areas System.** These laws provide the NIPAS framework. Protected areas are managed through long-term plans that may include zoning, buffer zones, habitat conservation, and rehabilitation.

### Environmentally Critical Projects and Areas

PD $1586$ works with proclamations that identify **Environmentally Critical Projects (ECPs)** and **Environmentally Critical Areas (ECAs)**. The two labels answer different questions.

An ECP is based on the **type of project**. Proclamation $2146$ includes specified heavy industries, major mining and quarrying projects, major dams, major power plants, major reclamation projects, and major roads and bridges.

An ECA is based on the **location**. Proclamation $2146$ includes areas such as national parks and wildlife sanctuaries, habitat of endangered or threatened Philippine wildlife, hazard-prone areas, critical slopes, prime agricultural lands, aquifer recharge areas, and certain water bodies, mangroves, and coral reefs.

An **Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC)** is a decision document issued after environmental review under the EIS System. For covered projects, it records the environmental clearance and conditions that the proponent must follow before and during the project.

### Water Quality Management

Under RA $9275$, the DENR can designate **Water Quality Management Areas** using units such as watersheds, river basins, or water-resource regions. Areas may be grouped when they share similar water conditions, pollution concerns, or development problems.

Each management area has a governing board. The board coordinates policy and monitors the area's action plan.

Facilities that discharge regulated wastewater must secure a discharge permit. The permit states how much wastewater may be released, the allowed quality of the discharge, the compliance schedule, and monitoring requirements.

There is no single discharge number for every facility. DENR sets effluent standards, and the permit applies the relevant limits and conditions to the discharge.

### Governing Agencies

The **Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR)** is the primary national department for the conservation, management, development, and proper use of the country's environment and natural resources. It makes and enforces environmental rules, manages natural resources, controls pollution, and sets standards for water and air quality.

DENR regional offices carry out environmental and natural-resource laws and programs in their regions. The **Environmental Management Bureau (EMB)** handles major pollution-control and environmental-impact work, including the EIS System.

Some environmental laws also give local bodies a role. Under RA $9275$, DENR may gradually devolve some water-quality administration to capable local governments or management boards.

The **Climate Change Commission** is the policy body created by RA $9729$ to coordinate and guide government action on climate change.

## Examples

### #1: Matching Waste Management to RA $9003$

A municipality requires households to separate recyclables, compostable waste, and other waste where the waste is produced. It also expands recycling and composting so less waste reaches disposal facilities. Which law most directly governs these actions?

**Solution:**

1. Separating waste where it is produced is segregation at source.
2. Reducing the amount sent to disposal facilities is waste diversion.
3. Both are central parts of the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act.
4. Therefore, the law is RA $9003$.

### #2: Identifying the DRRM Law

Which law set the modern Philippine disaster risk reduction and management framework and changed the name of the existing National Disaster Coordinating Council to the NDRRMC?

**Solution:**

1. RA $10121$ is the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of $2010$.
2. Under this law, the existing National Disaster Coordinating Council became known as the NDRRMC.
3. Therefore, the answer is RA $10121$.

### #3: Identifying EMB's Parent Department

The Environmental Management Bureau carries out major pollution-control and environmental-impact work. Which national executive department is it part of?

**Solution:**

1. EMB is part of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources.
2. DENR has the broad national mandate for environmental and natural-resource governance.
3. Therefore, EMB's parent department is the DENR.

### #4: Applying the Clean Air Act's Incineration Rule

A question asks which law contains the ban on burning municipal, biomedical, and hazardous waste when the process emits poisonous and toxic fumes. Which law should you identify?

**Solution:**

1. The subject is air-pollution control and a specific incineration rule.
2. RA $8749$ is the Philippine Clean Air Act of $1999$.
3. Therefore, the law is RA $8749$.

### #5: Recognizing Clean Water Act Regulation

A factory releases regulated wastewater into a river. The government requires a document that states the allowed quantity and quality of the discharge and the monitoring requirements. Which law provides this discharge-permit system?

**Solution:**

1. The problem is about regulated wastewater discharge.
2. RA $9275$ requires discharge permits for facilities that release regulated effluents.
3. The permit can state discharge limits, a compliance schedule, and monitoring requirements.
4. Therefore, the law is RA $9275$, the Philippine Clean Water Act of $2004$.

### #6: Matching Climate Policy to RA $9729$

Which law created the Climate Change Commission and required a national framework to guide government climate planning and action?

**Solution:**

1. The key clues are the Climate Change Commission and national climate planning.
2. RA $9729$ is the Climate Change Act of $2009$.
3. It created the Commission and required a climate framework for planning, research, development, and monitoring.
4. Therefore, the law is RA $9729$.

### #7: Distinguishing an ECP from an ECA

A proposed major dam is not inside a wildlife sanctuary or another listed sensitive location. Why can it still fall within the Philippine EIS System under Proclamation $2146$?

**Solution:**

1. An Environmentally Critical Area is based on the sensitivity of the location.
2. An Environmentally Critical Project is based on the project type.
3. Major dams are listed among the environmentally critical project types.
4. Therefore, the project can be covered because it is an ECP even without the stated ECA location.

### #8: Identifying the ECC Requirement

A proposed project is covered by the Philippine EIS System. What document must the project proponent secure through the environmental review process before implementation?

**Solution:**

1. PD $1586$ established the Philippine EIS System.
2. A covered project goes through the required environmental review.
3. The required decision document is the Environmental Compliance Certificate.
4. Therefore, the answer is the ECC.

### #9: Matching Wildlife Protection to RA $9147$

A threatened species is found in an important habitat outside an existing protected area. Which law directly provides for designating and protecting critical habitat for threatened species?

**Solution:**

1. The key clues are threatened wildlife and critical habitat outside a protected area.
2. RA $9147$ is the Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act.
3. It provides for critical habitats where threatened species are found.
4. Therefore, the law is RA $9147$.

### #10: Matching Protected-Area Management to NIPAS

A question describes a protected area managed through a long-term plan that can use zoning, buffer-zone management, habitat conservation, and rehabilitation. Which legal framework best matches the description?

**Solution:**

1. The problem is about formal protected-area establishment and management.
2. RA $7586$ created NIPAS, and RA $11038$ amended and expanded that system.
3. NIPAS management plans can use zoning and habitat-protection tools.
4. Therefore, the best match is RA $7586$ as amended by RA $11038$.

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

Match the law to the main problem first. Then check the details in the question.

- Waste and open dumps point to RA $9003$.
- Air pollution points to RA $8749$.
- Water pollution and discharge permits point to RA $9275$.
- Climate policy and the Climate Change Commission point to RA $9729$.
- Disaster risk reduction and the NDRRMC point to RA $10121$.
- EIS, ECP, ECA, and ECC point to PD $1586$.
- Wildlife and critical habitat point to RA $9147$.
- NIPAS and protected-area management point to RA $7586$ as amended by RA $11038$.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Saying RA $10121$ Created the NDRRMC from Nothing

> RA $10121$ did not create the council from nothing.

The law says the existing National Disaster Coordinating Council would henceforth be known as the NDRRMC. A question may test that exact wording.

### #2: Treating the Clean Air Act as a Ban on Every Kind of Burning

> The RA $8749$ incineration rule is conditional and has stated exceptions.

The ban concerns the Act's defined incineration of municipal, biomedical, and hazardous waste when the process emits poisonous and toxic fumes. The law lists exceptions, including *siga* and certain traditional uses and crematoria.

### #3: Mixing Up ECP and ECA

> ECP asks what kind of project it is. ECA asks where the project is located.

A major project type can be an ECP even when the question does not say it is inside an ECA. A project may also be covered because its location is an ECA.

### #4: Treating an ECC as the End of Environmental Duties

> An ECC does not cancel other environmental laws or permit conditions.

A project may still need to follow pollution-control rules, protected-area rules, permit conditions, and other laws that apply to its activity and location.

### #5: Confusing Wildlife Protection with Protected-Area Management

> RA $9147$ and NIPAS can both protect nature, but they focus on different legal tools.

RA $9147$ directly covers wildlife resources and critical habitats. RA $7586$, as amended by RA $11038$, provides the protected-area system and its management framework.

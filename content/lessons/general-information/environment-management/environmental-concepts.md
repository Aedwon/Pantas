## Core Concept

Key operational environmental protection principles and concepts examined in Civil Service tests include:

### Principles and Concepts

- **Segregation at Source:** The mandatory practice of separating different categories of waste (biodegradable, recyclable, non-recyclable, special) at the point of generation.
- **Waste Diversion:** The activity of keeping solid waste out of disposal facilities through reduction, reuse, recycling, and composting.
- **Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC):** A document issued by the DENR certifying that a proposed project has complied with the Environmental Impact Statement System under PD 1586.
- **Disaster Risk Reduction:** The paradigm shift formalized under RA 10121 prioritizing proactive risk assessment and mitigation over reactive post-disaster response.

## Examples

### #1: Meaning of Segregation at Source

A household sorts its kitchen waste, paper, and plastic containers into separate trash bins before garbage collection. Which environmental concept does this practice demonstrate?

**Solution:**
Match the practice to its environmental concept.

1. Segregation at source means separating waste at the location where it is produced rather than sorting it later at a central disposal facility.
2. Sorting materials into separate bins at home is separation at the point of generation.
3. Therefore, this practice demonstrates segregation at source.

### #2: Understanding Waste Diversion

A community composting program turns organic waste into fertilizer, reducing the total amount of waste sent to landfills. What environmental management principle does this describe?

**Solution:**
Identify the process of keeping waste out of landfills.

1. Waste diversion is the practice of keeping solid waste out of disposal facilities through reduction, reuse, recycling, and composting.
2. Turning organic waste into fertilizer prevents that material from entering landfills.
3. Therefore, this process describes waste diversion.

### #3: Environmental Compliance Certificate Requirement

Before starting construction on a major commercial development, a developer must obtain a specific certificate from the DENR confirming compliance with the Environmental Impact Statement System. What is this certificate called?

**Solution:**
Identify the document required under PD 1586.

1. Under Presidential Decree 1586, projects undergoing environmental impact assessment require official clearance before proceeding.
2. The required document issued by the DENR is the Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC).
3. Therefore, the required document is an Environmental Compliance Certificate.

## Core Principles and Concepts

- **Segregation at Source:** The mandatory practice of separating different categories of waste (biodegradable, recyclable, non-recyclable, special) at the point of generation.
- **Waste Diversion:** The activity of keeping solid waste out of disposal facilities through reduction, reuse, recycling, and composting.
- **Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC):** A document issued by the DENR certifying that a proposed project has complied with the Environmental Impact Statement System under PD 1586.
- **Disaster Risk Reduction:** The paradigm shift formalized under RA 10121 prioritizing proactive risk assessment and mitigation over reactive post-disaster response.

## Core Concept

### NIPAS Categories and Purposes

**RA $7586$**, amended by **RA $11038$** (Expanded NIPAS Act), sets up the **National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS)** for long-term land and water protection.

NIPAS has $7$ categories: strict nature reserves, natural parks, natural monuments, wildlife sanctuaries, protected landscapes and seascapes, resource reserves, and natural biotic areas.

Focus on $3$ tested purposes:

- **Natural monument:** protects a notable natural feature like a rare cave or rock arch.
- **Wildlife sanctuary:** protects natural conditions needed by resident or migratory wildlife.
- **Protected landscape or seascape:** recognizes how people and land or sea shaped each other over time.

Match $2$ sites by main purpose:

1. A wave-carved limestone sea arch fits a **natural monument**.
2. Fishing coves managed with coastal villages fit a **protected seascape**.

Result: Pick the NIPAS category from the exact feature or relationship shown.

:::check
A valley holds ancient stone rice terraces shaped by centuries of farming alongside forested peaks. Which NIPAS category fits best?
:::answer
The answer is **Protected landscape**. It recognizes places where people and the land shaped each other over time.
:::

### Zoning, Buffer Zones, and Rehabilitation

An approved **management plan** guides protected-area care with $3$ tools:

- **Zoning:** divides the inside of a park into parts with different activity rules.
- **Buffer zone:** lies outside and beside the park under special care to block outside harm.
- **Habitat rehabilitation:** repairs damaged plants, soil, or water so wildlife can return.

Apply all $3$ at a forest park:

1. Rangers zone the inner ridge off-limits for eagle nests and open lower picnic loops.
2. Rules curb spraying on bordering buffer-zone farms and replant native trees on logged scars.

Result: Internal zoning, external buffer care, and habitat repair work together under the plan.

:::pair #buffer-zone-protects-not-unrestricted
Right: A buffer zone sits beside a park under special care to block outside harm.
Wrong: Treating a buffer zone as an unrestricted strip for activities banned inside the park.
Dumping or clearing on the border defeats the buffer zone's protective role.
:::

:::pair #zoning-versus-buffer-zone
Right: Zoning sets activity rules inside a park, while buffer zones protect borders outside it.
Wrong: Confusing internal visitor and strict zones inside a park with external buffer-zone management.
Location splits them: zoning works inside the boundary, while buffer zones sit outside.
:::

:::check
A marine park plan opens a cove for snorkeling and bans anchors in a seagrass lagoon inside the park boundary. Zoning or buffer zone?
:::answer
The answer is **Zoning**. Setting different activity rules inside the protected area boundary is internal zoning.
:::

### NIPAS Areas Versus Critical Habitats

NIPAS areas under **RA $7586$ and RA $11038$** differ from **critical habitats under RA $9147$, Section $25$**, which protect threatened species outside existing NIPAS sites.

Living outside NIPAS never leaves a site unprotected or makes harmful wildlife use lawful.

Compare $2$ wetlands:

1. Wetland A is a statutory NIPAS wildlife sanctuary with internal zones.
2. Wetland B lies outside NIPAS, so DENR designates it a critical habitat under RA $9147$ for endangered ducks.

Result: Both wetlands gain legal protection through different statutory tools.

:::why
Why keep NIPAS and RA $9147$ critical habitats separate? NIPAS protects broad landscapes, while critical habitats shield threatened species outside park borders.
:::

:::pair #outside-nipas-still-protected
Right: Sites outside NIPAS can get RA $9147$ critical-habitat protection without joining NIPAS first.
Wrong: Claiming a site must become a NIPAS wildlife sanctuary before critical-habitat protection can apply.
Section $25$ of RA $9147$ specifically protects threatened wildlife habitats outside NIPAS.
:::

:::check
A planner says a marsh outside NIPAS holding endangered herons cannot be protected until Congress makes it a NIPAS sanctuary. Correct?
:::answer
The answer is **No**. Under RA $9147$, Section $25$, officials can designate critical habitats outside existing NIPAS areas.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Matching Two Sites to NIPAS Categories

Site A holds a tall volcanic basalt column. Site B is a tidal mudflat protected to keep nesting and feeding safe for rare shorebirds. Match each site to its category.

**Solution:**

1. Site A centers on a notable natural rock formation: natural monument.
2. Site B protects living conditions for wildlife: wildlife sanctuary.

Answer: Site A is a natural monument, and Site B is a wildlife sanctuary.

**Check:** Match notable formations to natural monuments and wildlife needs to sanctuaries.

### #2: Farms Bordering a Protected Forest

Rangers spot erosion and pesticide runoff from vegetable plots on private land right outside a park fence. The plan adds soil and spray controls on that strip. Identify the tool.

**Solution:**

1. The plots sit outside and adjacent to the protected area boundary.
2. Special controls there block erosion and runoff from harming the park inside.

Answer: This tool is a buffer zone.

**Check:** Confirm the strip sits outside and beside the park boundary.

### #3: Protecting Inside, Beside, and Outside NIPAS

A protected seascape plans a no-anchor coral nursery inside its boundary and fertilizer limits on bordering coastal farms right outside its boundary. Two kilometers farther down the coast, a mangrove bay outside NIPAS shelters endangered hawksbill turtles. Match each site to its legal management tool.

**Solution:**

1. Compare the no-anchor coral nursery inside the seascape with the fertilizer strip right outside its boundary.
2. Check which statutory tool under RA $9147$, Section $25$, can protect the non-NIPAS mangrove bay for endangered turtles.

:::try
Finish the last step: name the management or legal tool for all $3$ coastal sites and state whether the mangrove bay must join NIPAS first.
:::answer
The answer is **Zoning covers the coral nursery, a buffer zone covers the bordering farms, and RA $9147$ critical-habitat designation covers the mangrove bay.** Zoning sets rules inside the park, buffer zones curb border harm, and Section $25$ protects threatened habitats outside NIPAS without joining NIPAS first.

**Check:** Separate internal zoning (inside), buffer zones (bordering outside), and RA $9147$ critical habitats (separate sites outside NIPAS).
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Buffer care: manage border strips with special care, never as unrestricted dumping or clearing areas. {#buffer-zone-protects-not-unrestricted}
- Inside vs. outside: separate internal zoning inside park boundaries from external buffer zones beside them. {#zoning-versus-buffer-zone}
- Outside NIPAS: apply RA $9147$ critical-habitat protection without waiting for NIPAS status. {#outside-nipas-still-protected}
:::

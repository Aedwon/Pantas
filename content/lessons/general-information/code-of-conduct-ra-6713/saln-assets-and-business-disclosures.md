## Core Concept

Section $8$ of RA $6713$ requires covered public officials and employees to file financial declarations under oath. A declaration **under oath** is a statement sworn to be true. The documents are the **Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth (SALN)** and the **Disclosure of Business Interests and Financial Connections**.

### What the Declarations Contain

**Assets** are things a person owns, such as land, a home, savings, and investments. **Liabilities** are debts. **Net worth** is total assets minus total liabilities.

$$
\text{Net worth} = \text{assets} - \text{liabilities}
$$

For real property, such as land and buildings, report its improvements, acquisition cost, assessed value, and current fair market value. Improvements include buildings added to land.

**Acquisition cost** is what was paid to acquire the property. **Assessed value** is the value used for property tax. **Fair market value** for SALN is the market value shown in the property's latest available tax declaration.

If the tax declaration is not readily available, use other government sources, such as the local Assessor's Office. If it has no market value, use other government sources, such as the Assessor's Office or the Bureau of Internal Revenue's zonal value. If the property has no tax declaration, enter "N/A" for assessed and fair market values.

The declarations also list personal property and its acquisition cost. Other assets include cash, investments, stocks, and bonds. List debts as well, so the statement shows what is owned and what is owed.

A **business interest** is an ownership stake or other interest in a private business. **Shareholdings** are shares owned in a company. A **financial connection** is a financial link to a business, such as paid work as its consultant or adviser.

Report all required business interests and financial connections. The declarations cover property, other assets, debts, and business links.

## Examples

### #1: Net Worth

A covered employee's reported total assets are ₱$800{,}000$. Total liabilities are ₱$250{,}000$. What is the net worth?

**Solution:**

1. Subtract total liabilities from total assets.

$$
800{,}000 - 250{,}000 = 550{,}000
$$

2. The net worth is **₱$550{,}000$**.

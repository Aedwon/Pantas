## Core Concept

### CSC Enforcement, Ombudsman Investigation, and Court Trials

Under **Section $12$** of RA $6713$, distinct bodies handle enforcement, investigation, prosecution, and trial:

1. **Civil Service Commission (CSC)**: administers and enforces the Code, refers criminal cases, and starts lawful administrative action.
2. **Ombudsman**: under **RA $6770$, Section $15(1)$**, investigates and prosecutes cases, leading cases within Sandiganbayan jurisdiction.
3. **Special Prosecutor**: under **RA $6770$, Section $11(4)$**, investigates and prosecutes Sandiganbayan cases under Ombudsman control.
4. **Sandiganbayan and regular courts**: courts try criminal cases within their authority; not every Code case goes to the Sandiganbayan.

A **preliminary investigation** checks whether basis exists to file criminal charges. It never decides guilt.

Each House of Congress also keeps power to discipline its own members for disorderly behavior.

Trace a suspected criminal kickback by a bureau chief under Section $7(a)$:

1. The CSC checks records and refers the criminal matter for prosecution.
2. The Ombudsman, through its authorized Special Prosecutor, investigates and files charges.
3. The Sandiganbayan tries the case and decides guilt.

Result: The CSC refers, the Ombudsman investigates and prosecutes, and the court tries.

:::why
Why divide enforcement, prosecution, and trial across separate bodies? Separating investigators from trial judges prevents one body from acting as both accuser and judge.
:::

:::pair #csc-refers-ombudsman-investigates
Right: The CSC enforces and refers criminal cases; the Ombudsman investigates; the court decides guilt.
Wrong: Treating the CSC as deciding criminal guilt while the Sandiganbayan only forwards complaints.
The CSC handles administrative enforcement and referral, whereas courts decide criminal guilt.
:::

:::pair #preliminary-investigation-not-verdict
Right: An Ombudsman preliminary investigation checks basis to charge without deciding guilt.
Wrong: Treating an Ombudsman preliminary investigation as establishing criminal guilt without a court trial.
A charging probe checks probable basis to prosecute; only a trial court decides guilt.
:::

:::check
A municipal treasurer is investigated for a Section $8$ crime outside Sandiganbayan jurisdiction. If an Ombudsman probe finds basis to charge, is the treasurer convicted?
:::answer
The answer is **No**. A preliminary investigation checks basis to file charges in the proper trial court, which decides criminal guilt.
:::

### Protecting Citizen Reporters

Section $12$ directs the **Ombudsman** to take steps to protect citizens who report Code violations by public workers.

This duty never grants automatic immunity from every legal claim related to the report. A threatened citizen contacts the Ombudsman to request protective measures.

Apply Section $12$ when a contractor reports an inspector soliciting gifts and receives warning calls:

1. Responsible body: Section $12$ assigns citizen-reporter protection to the Ombudsman, not the CSC or court.
2. Scope: the Ombudsman takes protective steps without granting blanket immunity or declaring guilt early.

Result: The contractor requests protective steps from the Ombudsman.

:::pair #ombudsman-protects-reporters
Right: Section $12$ directs the Ombudsman to take steps to protect citizens who report Code violations.
Wrong: Claiming the CSC must give a citizen reporter automatic immunity from every legal claim.
The Ombudsman takes protective steps under Section $12$, without granting automatic legal immunity.
:::

:::check
A clerk reports a director's secret business interest and receives threats. The clerk asks the Sandiganbayan to convict the director immediately for safety. Does the court do that, or which duty applies?
:::answer
The answer is **No; the Ombudsman must take protective steps**. Reports never prove guilt before trial, and Section $12$ directs the Ombudsman to protect reporters.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Routing a High-Level Graft Matter

During an audit, the CSC finds evidence of a Section $7(a)$ conflict by a senior official under Sandiganbayan authority. How do the CSC, Ombudsman, Special Prosecutor, and Sandiganbayan share the work?

**Solution:**

1. CSC: enforce administrative compliance and refer the criminal case for prosecution.
2. Ombudsman and Special Prosecutor: investigate and prosecute the Sandiganbayan case under Ombudsman control.
3. Sandiganbayan: try the case and decide criminal guilt.

Answer: The CSC refers, the Ombudsman and Special Prosecutor investigate and prosecute, and the Sandiganbayan tries.

**Check:** Keep referral, investigation, and judicial trial assigned to their three institutions.

### #2: Threats After Reporting Solicitation

A supply driver reports a supervisor for soliciting cash under Section $7(d)$. Strangers then follow the driver home. The driver demands automatic CSC immunity from every lawsuit. Is that right?

**Solution:**

1. Protector: Section $12$ directs the Ombudsman, not the CSC, to protect citizen reporters.
2. Scope: protective steps address threats without promising automatic immunity from every legal claim.

Answer: No, the driver should ask the Ombudsman for protection, which excludes automatic immunity.

**Check:** Match reporter protection to the Ombudsman under Section $12$ without blanket immunity.

### #3: Preliminary Findings and House Discipline

Two matters arise in one week. First, a Special Prosecutor finishes a preliminary investigation against a port director and files charges in the Sandiganbayan. Second, a House member acts disorderly at a hearing. The director says the probe convicted him. Check both matters.

**Solution:**

1. Port director's claim: test whether a preliminary investigation decides guilt or only checks basis to prosecute.
2. House matter: identify which body disciplines a member of Congress for disorderly behavior.

:::try
Finish the last step: rule on the port director's claim and state who disciplines the House member.
:::answer
The answer is **The probe did not decide guilt, and the House disciplines its member**. Preliminary investigation checks basis to charge before trial, while each House of Congress disciplines its members.

**Check:** Separate charging review from verdict, and preserve congressional discipline over members.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Institutional roles: let the CSC refer, the Ombudsman investigate, and the court decide guilt. {#csc-refers-ombudsman-investigates}
- Preliminary investigation: treat an Ombudsman probe as a charging step, never a conviction. {#preliminary-investigation-not-verdict}
- Reporter protection: direct protection requests to the Ombudsman instead of claiming automatic CSC immunity. {#ombudsman-protects-reporters}
:::

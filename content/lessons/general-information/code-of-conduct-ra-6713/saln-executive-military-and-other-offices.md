## Core Concept

### National Executive and Senior Military Officers

Submit your SALN to your agency's human resource office or equivalent, which sends the original to the statutory repository under Section $8$(A).

Section $8(A)(2)$ and $8(A)(4)$ route senior executive and senior military filers to the **Office of the President**:

1. **National executive officials**: Cabinet members, undersecretaries, assistant secretaries, foreign service officers, and heads of government-owned or controlled corporations (GOCCs).
2. **Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) officers** from the rank of **colonel or naval captain** and above.

Route a Finance undersecretary and a Philippine Navy captain (the Navy rank equivalent to Army colonel):

1. The Finance undersecretary is a national executive official, so the SALN goes to the Office of the President.
2. A naval captain meets the colonel-or-naval-captain threshold, so that SALN also goes to the Office of the President.

:::pair #national-exec-goes-to-op
Right: National executive officials file with the Office of the President, not the residual Civil Service Commission.
Wrong: Routing a Cabinet member or national executive official to the Civil Service Commission.
Match national executive posts to the Office of the President before checking any residual rule.
:::

:::pair #afp-rank-cutoff
Right: AFP officers at colonel or naval captain and above file with the Office of the President; lower ranks file with the regional Deputy Ombudsman.
Wrong: Sending every commissioned AFP officer's SALN to the Office of the President regardless of rank.
Check military rank first: colonel or naval captain is the dividing line in Section $8(A)(4)$.
:::

:::check
A Department of Trade assistant secretary and an Air Force brigadier general file their annual SALNs. Which statutory repository receives both filings?
:::answer
The answer is **The Office of the President.** Assistant secretaries are national executive officials, and a brigadier general outranks a colonel.
:::

### Regional, Local, Junior Military, and Other Filers

Section $8(A)(3)$, $8(A)(4)$, and $8(A)(5)$ assign the remaining categories to two distinct offices:

1. **Deputy Ombudsman in the proper region**: regional and local officials and employees (including state university employees), and AFP officers **below** colonel or naval captain.
2. **Civil Service Commission (CSC)**: all other covered public officials and employees who fit none of the specific statutory categories above.

Match a provincial engineer in Cebu, an Army captain in Davao, and a covered official outside all listed categories:

1. The provincial engineer is a local employee, so the regional Deputy Ombudsman receives the SALN.
2. An Army captain ranks below colonel, so the regional Deputy Ombudsman receives that SALN.
3. Only the official outside every named category goes to the CSC.

:::why
Why is the CSC not the repository for everyone? The law assigns named groups to the Ombudsman, Congress, courts, or President, leaving the CSC only for unlisted covered roles.
:::

:::pair #regional-local-vs-residual-csc
Right: Regional and local employees file with their regional Deputy Ombudsman, while only remaining uncategorized filers go to the CSC.
Wrong: Sending a regional or local employee's SALN to the Civil Service Commission instead of the regional Deputy Ombudsman.
Regional and local workers have a specific statutory office: the Deputy Ombudsman of their region.
:::

:::check
A state university registrar in Iloilo and a Navy lieutenant (below naval captain) file their SALNs. Which statutory repository receives both?
:::answer
The answer is **The Deputy Ombudsman of their respective region.** Both state university employees and AFP officers below naval captain file with the regional Deputy Ombudsman.
:::

## Examples

### #1: GOCC Heads and Foreign Service

The president of a government-owned power corporation and a foreign service officer posted abroad submit their SALNs. Which repository receives both?

**Solution:**

1. Identify both roles: heads of GOCCs and foreign service officers are national executive officials under Section $8$(A).
2. Match the statutory list: national executive officials file with the Office of the President.

Answer: Both file with the Office of the President.

**Check:** National executive officials go to the Office of the President, never the CSC.

### #2: AFP Ranks Across Three Officers

A Navy commander (below naval captain), a Marine colonel, and an Army lieutenant file their annual SALNs. Match each officer to the right repository.

**Solution:**

1. Marine colonel: meets the colonel-or-naval-captain rank line, so the SALN goes to the Office of the President.
2. Navy commander and Army lieutenant: both rank below naval captain or colonel, so both go to the Deputy Ombudsman in their proper region.

Answer: Office of the President for the Marine colonel; regional Deputy Ombudsman for the Navy commander and Army lieutenant.

**Check:** Only colonel, naval captain, and higher ranks file with the Office of the President.

### #3: Local, Executive, and Remaining Filers

A municipal treasurer, a Cabinet secretary, and a covered employee outside every named statutory category submit their SALNs. Where does each filing go?

**Solution:**

1. Municipal treasurer: local officials and employees file with the Deputy Ombudsman in their proper region.
2. Cabinet secretary: national executive officials file with the Office of the President.

:::try
Finish the last step: assign the employee outside every named category to the right statutory repository and explain why.
:::answer
The answer is **The Civil Service Commission (CSC).** Section $8(A)(5)$ assigns all other covered officials and employees to the CSC only when no named category applies.

**Check:** Local goes to the regional Deputy Ombudsman, Cabinet to the Office of the President, and unlisted roles to the CSC.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- National executive roles: route Cabinet members and national executives to the Office of the President, not the CSC. {#national-exec-goes-to-op}
- AFP rank line: send colonel or naval captain and above to the Office of the President, and lower ranks to the regional Deputy Ombudsman. {#afp-rank-cutoff}
- Regional and local workers: send them to their regional Deputy Ombudsman, keeping the CSC strictly for unlisted roles. {#regional-local-vs-residual-csc}
:::

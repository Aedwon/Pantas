## Core Concept

### Nationalism and Patriotism

**Nationalism and patriotism** is the sixth norm under Section $4$(A)(f). Officials and employees must at all times be loyal to the Republic and to the Filipino people.

This norm includes concrete duties beyond a loyalty pledge. Officials must promote locally produced goods, resources, and technology, and encourage pride in country and people. They must also defend Philippine **sovereignty** (the country's independent control over its affairs) against foreign intrusion.

Examine a public hospital director choosing Philippine-developed diagnostic software over an imported system of equal price and performance:

1. Check the trigger: the director chooses local technology without sacrificing quality.
2. Apply Section $4$(A)(f): promoting locally produced goods, resources, and technology is part of Nationalism and patriotism.

:::pair #nationalism-local-goods
Right: Match loyalty to the Republic, local goods and technology, pride of country, and defending sovereignty to Nationalism and patriotism.
Wrong: Assigning promotion of locally produced goods and technology to Commitment to public interest.
Commitment to public interest guards funds and property, while Section $4$(A)(f) specifically names local products and national sovereignty.
:::

:::check
A school division buys locally woven classroom curtains and Philippine-made bamboo desks when they meet quality rules. Which Section $4$(A) norm supports that choice?
:::answer
The answer is **Nationalism and patriotism**. Section $4$(A)(f) expressly directs officials to promote locally produced goods, resources, and technology.
:::

### Commitment to Democracy

**Commitment to democracy** is the seventh norm under Section $4$(A)(g). Officials and employees must commit themselves to the democratic way of life and values.

They must maintain **public accountability** (answering to the people for public power and resources) and show civilian supremacy over the military. They must also uphold the Constitution and put loyalty to country above loyalty to persons or party.

Trace an agency accountant who refuses a political patron's request to hide an audit report from public view:

1. Check competing loyalties: the patron asks for personal or party protection over constitutional transparency.
2. Apply Section $4$(A)(g): answering to the people and putting country above person or party expresses Commitment to democracy.

:::why
Why separate country over party from general patriotism? Commitment to democracy targets constitutional rule, civilian supremacy, public accountability, and refusing personal or party loyalty.
:::

:::pair #democracy-vs-nationalism
Right: Match upholding the Constitution, public accountability, and country over persons or party to Commitment to democracy.
Wrong: Assigning constitutional loyalty above persons or party to Nationalism and patriotism.
Look for the Constitution, accountability, civilian supremacy, or country-above-party clues to pick Commitment to democracy.
:::

:::pair #accountability-to-people
Right: Public officials must answer to the people for public power and resources under Commitment to democracy.
Wrong: Claiming officials may use public power without answering to the people.
Section $4$(A)(g) makes public accountability and constitutional duty binding on every public servant.
:::

:::check
A provincial engineer rejects an influential ally's demand to skip constitutional bidding rules, stating that loyalty belongs to the nation. Which norm guides her?
:::answer
The answer is **Commitment to democracy**. Section $4$(A)(g) requires upholding the Constitution and placing loyalty to country above loyalty to persons or party.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Defending Sovereignty Without Using the Word Loyalty

A maritime agency declines an unapproved foreign request to inspect protected domestic reef data and buys Filipino-built sensor buoys for monitoring. Which norm fits, even though the scenario never mentions loyalty?

**Solution:**

1. Spot the clues: protecting national control over domestic affairs defends Philippine sovereignty, and buying Filipino-built sensors promotes local technology.
2. Match the norm: Section $4$(A)(f) links defending sovereignty and using local technology directly to Nationalism and patriotism.

Answer: Nationalism and patriotism.

**Check:** Local goods, technology, national pride, and sovereignty all point to Section $4$(A)(f).

### #2: Country Above a Party Leader

A city budget officer is pressured by her party chair to withhold quarterly spending sheets showing cost overruns. She releases the required public records and cites the Constitution. Which norm governs her decision?

**Solution:**

1. Identify the issue: the officer puts public accountability and constitutional duty ahead of party loyalty.
2. Match the norm: Section $4$(A)(g) requires maintaining public accountability and placing loyalty to country above persons or party.

Answer: Commitment to democracy.

**Check:** Accountability for public power and resources plus country-over-party loyalty belong to Commitment to democracy.

### #3: Pairing Local Technology With Public Accountability

A health bureau equips rural clinics with Philippine-made solar vaccine coolers and publishes monthly repair logs so citizens can track how public funds were spent. Which Section $4$(A) norm matches each action?

**Solution:**

1. Check the first action: identify what choosing Philippine-made solar coolers supports under Section $4$(A).
2. Check the second action: identify what letting citizens track public spending upholds under Section $4$(A).

:::try
Finish the last step: match both actions to their exact Section $4$(A) norms and state why neither switches places.
:::answer
The answer is **Nationalism and patriotism for the local coolers, and Commitment to democracy for publishing the logs.** Section $4$(A)(f) promotes local goods and technology, while Section $4$(A)(g) requires public accountability to the people.

**Check:** Match local production to Section $4$(A)(f) and answering to the public for funds to Section $4$(A)(g).
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- Local goods and technology: assign them to Nationalism and patriotism instead of Commitment to public interest. {#nationalism-local-goods}
- Constitution and country over party: match them to Commitment to democracy, not Nationalism and patriotism. {#democracy-vs-nationalism}
- Answering to the people: keep public accountability and equal service above personal or party power. {#accountability-to-people}
:::

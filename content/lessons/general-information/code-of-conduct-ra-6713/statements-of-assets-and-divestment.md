## Core Concept

Section 8 of RA 6713 requires public officials and employees to file a sworn Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth (SALN) and disclosure of business interests and financial connections.

### Coverage and Disclosures

- **Coverage:** Includes the official, their spouse, and unmarried children under eighteen years of age living in their household.
- **Relatives in government:** Must disclose relatives in government service up to the fourth civil degree of consanguinity or affinity.

### Key Deadlines for SALN Filing

1. **Assumption of office:** Within 30 days, reckoned from the first day of service.
2. **Annual filing:** On or before April 30, reckoned as of the end of the preceding year.
3. **Separation from service:** Within 30 days, reckoned from the last day of service.

### Where SALN is Filed

- **Office of the Ombudsman:** National officials and constitutional officials.
- **Secretary of the Senate or House:** Members of Congress.
- **Clerk of Supreme Court / Court Administrator:** Justices and judges.
- **Office of the President:** Cabinet members, undersecretaries, assistant secretaries, foreign service officers, and heads of GOCCs.
- **Deputy Ombudsman of the Region:** Regional and local officials, state university employees.
- **Civil Service Commission:** All other public officers and employees.

### Public Access and Divestment

- **Public access:** SALN documents become available for public inspection after 10 working days, and remain available for 10 years after which they are destroyed unless needed in an ongoing investigation.
- **Divestment rules:** A conflict of interest must be resolved by resigning from a private position within 30 days or divesting shareholdings within 60 days from assumption of office or occurrence of conflict.

## Examples

### #1: SALN Filing Deadline on Assumption of Office

A newly appointed government employee starts working on June 1. By what deadline must the employee file their initial Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth (SALN)?

**Solution:**
Identify the specific SALN deadline for assumption of office under Section 8 of RA 6713.

1. Under RA 6713, an initial SALN must be filed upon assumption of office.
2. The deadline is within 30 days, reckoned from the first day of service.
3. Therefore, the employee must file the initial SALN within 30 days of their first day of service.

### #2: Divestment Deadlines for Private Interests

A newly appointed bureau director owns shares in a private firm regulated by their new office and serves on its board. What are the legal deadlines for resigning the board position and divesting the shareholdings?

**Solution:**
Apply the divestment timeframes specified in RA 6713.

1. Conflict of interest must be resolved by resigning private positions and divesting shareholdings.
2. The official must resign from the private board position within 30 days.
3. The official must divest shareholdings within 60 days.
4. Therefore, the deadlines are 30 days for resignation and 60 days for divestment.

### #3: Public Access and Document Retention Period

A journalist requests to inspect the SALN of a local official filed 8 years ago. How many days after filing does a SALN become accessible to the public, and how long is it retained?

**Solution:**
Review the public availability and retention rules under RA 6713.

1. SALN documents become available for public inspection after 10 working days from filing.
2. Filed SALN documents remain available for public inspection for 10 years.
3. Because 8 years is within the 10-year retention window, the document is still retained and publicly accessible.

## SALN Filing Requirements

Section 8 of RA 6713 requires public officials and employees to file a sworn Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth (SALN) and disclosure of business interests and financial connections.

- **Coverage:** Includes the official, their spouse, and unmarried children under eighteen years of age living in their household.
- **Relatives in government:** Must disclose relatives in government service up to the fourth civil degree of consanguinity or affinity.

## Key Deadlines for SALN Filing

1. **Assumption of office:** Within 30 days, reckoned from the first day of service.
2. **Annual filing:** On or before April 30, reckoned as of the end of the preceding year.
3. **Separation from service:** Within 30 days, reckoned from the last day of service.

## Where SALN is Filed

- **Office of the Ombudsman:** National officials and constitutional officials.
- **Secretary of the Senate or House:** Members of Congress.
- **Clerk of Supreme Court / Court Administrator:** Justices and judges.
- **Office of the President:** Cabinet members, undersecretaries, assistant secretaries, foreign service officers, and heads of GOCCs.
- **Deputy Ombudsman of the Region:** Regional and local officials, state university employees.
- **Civil Service Commission:** All other public officers and employees.

## Public Access and Divestment

- **Public access:** SALN documents become available for public inspection after 10 working days, and remain available for 10 years after which they are destroyed unless needed in an ongoing investigation.
- **Divestment rules:** A conflict of interest must be resolved by resigning from a private position within 30 days or divesting shareholdings within 60 days from assumption of office or occurrence of conflict.

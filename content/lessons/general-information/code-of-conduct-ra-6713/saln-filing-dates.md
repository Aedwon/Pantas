## Core Concept

### Filing Dates

File within $30$ days after assuming office. Annual filings are due on or before April $30$ every year after that. File again within $30$ days after leaving government service.

For a period stated as days after an event, begin counting on the next day. Only the copying rule says working days. Another part of this lesson covers that rule. Do not change every deadline into working days.

Covered filers must also give the Ombudsman authority to obtain government records of their finances. This authority is due within $30$ days after assumption of office. It allows access to records from agencies such as the Bureau of Internal Revenue.

## Examples

### #1: Initial Filing Date

A covered employee assumes office on June $1$. By what date must the initial SALN be filed?

**Solution:**

1. The period is $30$ days after assumption of office. Start counting on June $2$.
2. Count the days left in June, then the days needed in July.

$$
30 - 1 = 29 \text{ days in June}
$$

$$
30 - 29 = 1 \text{ day in July}
$$

3. The deadline is **July $1$**.

### #2: Annual and Separation Filings

A covered employee stays in service through April and leaves on September $15$ of the same year. What annual and separation deadlines apply?

**Solution:**

1. The annual filing is due on or before **April $30$**.
2. The separation filing is due within $30$ days after September $15$. Begin counting on September $16$.
3. Count the days left in September, then the days needed in October.

$$
30 - 15 = 15 \text{ days in September}
$$

$$
30 - 15 = 15 \text{ days in October}
$$

4. The separation deadline is **October $15$**. It does not replace the annual filing.


## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Confusing Filing Dates With Copying Dates

> Only the copying period here is stated in working days.

Initial and separation filings use $30$ days. April $30$ is the annual deadline. Under RA 6713, the copying period is $10$ working days from filing. For Ombudsman repositories, the earliest release is $10$ working days after the final agency submission deadline, once the SALN has been processed and recorded.

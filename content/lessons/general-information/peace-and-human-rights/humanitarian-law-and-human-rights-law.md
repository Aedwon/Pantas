## Fundamental Distinction

Understanding the distinction between International Human Rights Law (HRL) and International Humanitarian Law (IHL) is a central concept in Civil Service examinations.

## Scope of Application

- **Human Rights Law (HRL):** Applies at all times, during both peacetime and situations of armed conflict.
- **International Humanitarian Law (IHL):** Applies specifically during situations of **armed conflict** (law of armed conflict).

## Bound Entities

- **Human Rights Law:** Primarily places legal duties and obligations upon state authorities regarding their treatment of people.
- **International Humanitarian Law:** Binds **all parties** to an armed conflict, including both state armed forces and non-state armed groups.

## Primary Purpose of IHL

International Humanitarian Law seeks to limit the effects of armed conflict by protecting persons who are not or are no longer participating in hostilities, and by restricting the means and methods of warfare.

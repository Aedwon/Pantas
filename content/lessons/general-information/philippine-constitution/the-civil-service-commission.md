## Core Concept

Article IX, Section B of the 1987 Constitution establishes the Civil Service Commission as the central personnel agency of the Philippine Government.

### Composition and Qualifications

- **Composition:** Composed of a **Chairman and two Commissioners**.
- **Citizenship:** Must be **natural-born citizens** of the Philippines.
- **Age:** Must be **at least $35$ years of age** at the time of appointment.
- **Qualifications:** Must possess proven capacity for public administration.
- **Elective disqualification:** Must **not have been candidates for any elective position in the election immediately preceding** their appointment.

### Appointment and Tenure

- **Appointing authority:** Appointed by the **President with the consent of the Commission on Appointments**.
- **Term of office:** Appointed for a term of **seven years, without reappointment**.

## Examples

### #1: Composition of the Commission

A student is asked how many members make up the Civil Service Commission under Article IX-B of the 1987 Constitution. What is the composition?

**Solution:**
Recall the official leadership structure.

1. The Civil Service Commission consists of a Chairman and two Commissioners.
2. 1 Chairman + 2 Commissioners = 3 members in total.
3. Therefore, the Commission is composed of a Chairman and two Commissioners.

### #2: Disqualification Based on Prior Candidacy

An applicant for Civil Service Commissioner ran as a candidate in the local elections held last year. Is this applicant qualified for appointment under Article IX-B?

**Solution:**
Check the disqualification rules for elective candidates.

1. Article IX-B requires that appointees must not have been candidates for any elective position in the election immediately preceding their appointment.
2. Since the applicant ran in the immediately preceding election, the applicant is disqualified.
3. Therefore, the applicant cannot be appointed to the Civil Service Commission.

### #3: Term Length and Reappointment

A Commissioner completes a full seven-year term on the Civil Service Commission. Can the President reappoint this Commissioner for a second seven-year term?

**Solution:**
Review the appointment and tenure rules under Article IX-B.

1. Members are appointed by the President with consent of the Commission on Appointments for a term of seven years.
2. Article IX-B explicitly specifies that appointments are without reappointment.
3. Therefore, the Commissioner cannot be reappointed for another term.

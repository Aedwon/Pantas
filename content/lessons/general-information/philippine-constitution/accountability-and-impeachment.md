## Core Concept

Article XI, Section 1 states that public office is a public trust. Public officers and employees must at all times be accountable to the people, serve them with utmost responsibility, integrity, loyalty, and efficiency, act with patriotism and justice, and lead modest lives.

### Closed List of Impeachable Officers

Under Article XI, Section 2, only the following public officials may be removed from office by impeachment:

1. The President
2. The Vice-President
3. The Members of the Supreme Court
4. The Members of the Constitutional Commissions (CSC, COMELEC, COA)
5. The Ombudsman

### Closed List of Grounds for Impeachment

Article XI, Section 2 restricts the grounds for impeachment exclusively to:

1. Culpable violation of the Constitution
2. Treason
3. Bribery
4. Graft and corruption
5. Other high crimes
6. Betrayal of public trust

All other public officers and employees may be removed from office as provided by law, but not through impeachment.

## Examples

### #1: Identifying Impeachable Officers

A petition seeks to remove a Regional Trial Court judge from office through impeachment. Is a Regional Trial Court judge an impeachable officer under Article XI?

**Solution:**
Check the closed list of impeachable officers under Article XI, Section 2.

1. The closed list includes: President, Vice-President, Members of the Supreme Court, Members of the Constitutional Commissions, and the Ombudsman.
2. Trial court judges are not included in this closed list.
3. Therefore, a trial court judge is not an impeachable officer and must be removed through regular legal processes instead.

### #2: Evaluating Grounds for Impeachment

A member of a Constitutional Commission is accused of betrayal of public trust. Is this a valid ground for impeachment under Article XI?

**Solution:**
Verify the ground against the closed list of constitutional grounds.

1. The six exclusive grounds are: culpable violation of the Constitution, treason, bribery, graft and corruption, other high crimes, and betrayal of public trust.
2. Betrayal of public trust is explicitly listed as item six.
3. Therefore, betrayal of public trust is a valid constitutional ground for impeachment.

### #3: Non-Impeachable Officials

Which of the following officials is NOT subject to removal by impeachment under Article XI: the Ombudsman, a Member of the Supreme Court, or a Cabinet Secretary?

**Solution:**
Compare each title against the five impeachable offices.

1. The Ombudsman is on the closed list.
2. Members of the Supreme Court are on the closed list.
3. Cabinet Secretaries are not on the closed list.
4. Therefore, a Cabinet Secretary is not subject to removal by impeachment.

## Core Concept

### Protections in Sections 9 to 13

- Protection against taking private property for public use without just compensation.
- Non-impairment of obligations of contracts.
- Free access to courts and quasi-judicial bodies and adequate legal assistance regardless of poverty.

<!-- REWRITE: the sentence below was written for Sections 12 to 22; adjust it to the sections this part covers -->

Sections 12 to 22 include several protections connected with investigation, prosecution, punishment, and personal liberty:
- Rights under custodial investigation, including the right to remain silent and to competent and independent counsel.
- Right to bail, subject to the constitutional exception for persons charged with offenses punishable by reclusion perpetua when evidence of guilt is strong.

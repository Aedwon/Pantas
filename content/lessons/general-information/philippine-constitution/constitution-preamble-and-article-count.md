## Core Concept

The $1987$ Constitution has a Preamble and $18$ Articles. An **article** is a major division of the Constitution. Its title helps you find a topic.

A title tells where to look. It does not tell every rule, power, requirement, or exception inside the article. For a question about what the government may do, read or recall the exact rule too.

### The Preamble: Shared Aims

The **Preamble** is the opening statement. It explains why the Filipino people adopted the Constitution and the kind of country they seek. It is not one of the $18$ Articles.

A **just and humane society** treats people fairly and with care for human dignity. The **common good** means the good of the community, not only the advantage of one person or group.

**Patrimony** means the nation's shared heritage and resources. Conserving and developing it means caring for that inheritance while using it for the country's good.

The Preamble also seeks independence, democracy, truth, justice, freedom, love, equality, and peace. The **rule of law** means that public power must follow the law. An official's personal wish does not replace a legal rule.

These are broad aims. They do not by themselves answer every question about a power or a legal procedure.

## Examples

### #1: Counting the Articles

How many Articles are in the $1987$ Constitution, excluding the Preamble?

**Solution:**

1. The Articles run from I through XVIII.
2. XVIII is the Roman numeral for $18$.
3. The answer is $18$ Articles. The Preamble is not included.

### #2: Interpreting a Preamble Aim

Which aim of the Preamble is most directly served by a policy designed to benefit the community as a whole instead of giving a private advantage to one favored group?

**Solution:**

1. The policy's stated purpose is the good of the whole community.
2. This matches the Preamble's aim of promoting the common good.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: The Preamble Is Not Article I

> Do not add the Preamble to the article count.

The Preamble gives broad aims. Article I concerns National Territory. The Constitution has $18$ Articles.

## Core Concept

### Who may hold each office

The **Commission on Elections (COMELEC)** enforces election laws. It has a Chair and $6$ Commissioners. Members must be natural-born, at least $35$ at appointment, and college graduates.

A majority, including the Chair, must be lawyers with at least $10$ years of law practice (Article IX-C, Section $1$). This does not require every Commissioner to be a lawyer.

The **Commission on Audit (COA)** checks government accounts. It has a Chair and $2$ Commissioners. Members must be natural-born and at least $35$ at appointment.

They must also be "of proven probity and independence" (Article IX-D, Section $1(1)$). This means they must have shown honest conduct and freedom from improper influence.

A member must be a certified public accountant, or **CPA**, with at least $10$ years of auditing experience, or a lawyer with at least $10$ years of practice. CPAs are licensed accounting professionals; auditing means checking financial records. All Members cannot belong to the same profession (Article IX-D, Section $1$).

The **Ombudsman** investigates complaints against public officials. The Ombudsman must be natural-born, at least $40$ at appointment, a lawyer, and known for upright conduct and independence. At least $10$ years as a judge or practicing lawyer in the Philippines is required (Article XI, Section $8$).

These three offices bar candidates in the election immediately before appointment. See The Civil Service Commission for CSC qualifications. Regular full terms of Constitutional Commission members and the Ombudsman are $7$ years without reappointment (Article IX-B, IX-C, and IX-D, Section $1(2)$; Article XI, Section $11$).

## Examples

### #1: Other Constitutional Offices

A natural-born college graduate is $35$, has practiced law for $10$ years, and was a candidate in the immediately preceding election. Can he be appointed to COMELEC or COA? Does he meet the Ombudsman's age rule?

**Solution:**

1. Prior candidacy bars both commission appointments despite his age and credentials.
2. He is below the Ombudsman's minimum age of $40$.

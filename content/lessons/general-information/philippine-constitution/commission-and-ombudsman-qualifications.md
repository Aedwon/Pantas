## Core Concept

### COMELEC: Composition and Qualifications

The **Commission on Elections (COMELEC)** enforces election laws and has $7$ members: a Chair and $6$ Commissioners (Article IX-C, Section $1(1)$).

Members must be natural-born, at least $35$ at appointment, and college graduates. Candidates in the election immediately preceding appointment are barred.

A **majority** of the $7$ members (at least $4$), **including the Chair**, must be lawyers with $10$ years of practice. Full terms for all three Commissions and the Ombudsman are $7$ years without reappointment (see The Civil Service Commission).

Check a $7$-member COMELEC slate with a $12$-year lawyer Chair and $3$ Commissioners who are $10$-year lawyers:

1. Qualified lawyers total $1 + 3 = 4$, a majority of $7$.
2. Because the Chair joins that majority, the other $3$ Commissioners need not be lawyers.

:::pair #comelec-chair-in-majority
Right: COMELEC has $7$ members, and at least a majority ($4$), including the Chair, must be $10$-year lawyers.
Wrong: Requiring all $7$ COMELEC members to have $10$ years of law practice.
Article IX-C, Section $1(1)$ requires a majority of $4$ qualified lawyers including the Chair, not all $7$ members.
:::

:::check
Suppose $5$ COMELEC nominees are non-lawyers and only $2$ (including the Chair) are $10$-year lawyers. Does this $7$-member slate pass?
:::answer
The answer is **No**. Two lawyers out of $7$ is under a majority; at least $4$ qualified lawyers (including the Chair) are required.
:::

### COA and the Ombudsman

The **Commission on Audit (COA)** is an independent constitutional commission (Article IX-A, Section $1$) that checks government accounts. It has $3$ members: a Chair and $2$ Commissioners (Article IX-D, Section $1(1)$). Members must be natural-born, at least $35$ at appointment, and not candidates in the election immediately preceding appointment.

Each COA member must be a **CPA** (certified public accountant) with $10$ auditing years, or a **lawyer** with $10$ practice years. **All Members cannot belong to the same profession.**

The **Ombudsman** investigates complaints against public officials (Article XI, Section $13(1)$). Under Article XI, Section $8$, the Ombudsman must be natural-born, at least $40$ at appointment, and of recognized probity and independence. The Ombudsman must be a Philippine Bar member with $10$ years as a judge or lawyer here. Prior candidates in the immediately preceding election are barred.

Screen a CPA nominee with $8$ years of auditing experience for a COA seat while both sitting members are $10$-year lawyers:

1. Adding a CPA avoids putting all $3$ COA members in the same profession.
2. Eight years of auditing falls short of the $10$-year minimum under Section $1(1)$, so the nominee fails.

:::why
Why mix professions on COA? Having both CPAs and lawyers prevents blind spots when auditing government funds.
:::

:::pair #coa-no-same-profession
Right: COA's $3$ members must never all belong to the same profession under Article IX-D, Section $1(1)$.
Wrong: Naming a third CPA when the two sitting COA members are already CPAs.
With two CPAs serving, the third seat must go to a $10$-year lawyer to keep professions mixed.
:::

:::pair #ombudsman-prior-candidacy
Right: Any candidate in the immediately preceding election is barred from COMELEC, COA, and Ombudsman posts.
Wrong: Limiting the prior-candidacy bar to losing candidates only.
Winning or losing does not matter; running in the immediately preceding election disqualifies the nominee.
:::

:::check
A $38$-year-old natural-born CPA with $12$ years of auditing experience ran for city councilor in the immediately preceding election. Can she be appointed to COA?
:::answer
The answer is **No**. Article IX-D, Section $1(1)$ bars anyone who ran for an elective post in the immediately preceding election.
:::

## Examples

### #1: Other Constitutional Offices

A natural-born college graduate is $35$, has practiced law for $10$ years, and ran in the immediately preceding election. Can he join COMELEC or COA? Does he meet the Ombudsman's age rule?

**Solution:**

1. Under Articles IX-C and IX-D, Section $1(1)$, prior candidacy bars appointment to COMELEC and COA.
2. Under Article XI, Section $8$, age $35$ is below the Ombudsman's $40$-year minimum.

Answer: No to COMELEC or COA for prior candidacy; no to the Ombudsman age rule ($35 < 40$).

**Check:** Credentials never erase prior candidacy, and commissions start at $35$ while Ombudsman starts at $40$.

### #2: COMELEC Chair Practice Rule

A $7$-member COMELEC has $3$ Commissioners with $12$ practice years and $3$ non-lawyer graduates. The Chair is an $8$-year lawyer. All meet general rules. Why does it fail?

**Solution:**

1. Under Article IX-C, Section $1(1)$, the Chair needs $10$ practice years.
2. Only $3$ of $7$ qualify as $10$-year lawyers, missing both the $4$-lawyer majority and Chair rule.

Answer: The Chair lacks $10$ practice years, leaving $3$ qualified lawyers instead of $4$.

**Check:** COMELEC requires at least $4$ of $7$ members, including the Chair, to have $10$ practice years.

### #3: Checking an Ombudsman and a COMELEC Nominee

Nominee X is a $39$-year-old natural-born Philippine Bar member with $12$ years of practice, nominated as Ombudsman. Nominee Y is a $37$-year-old non-lawyer graduate nominated as a fourth non-lawyer on the $7$-member COMELEC. Does either qualify?

**Solution:**

1. Check Nominee X under Article XI, Section $8$: compare age $39$ against the Ombudsman minimum age.
2. Check Nominee Y under Article IX-C, Section $1(1)$: count how many of $7$ COMELEC seats remain for $10$-year lawyers.

:::try
Finish the last step: rule on Nominee X and Nominee Y and give the constitutional rule each fails.
:::answer
The answer is **Neither qualifies: Nominee X is under age $40$, and Nominee Y blocks COMELEC's majority-lawyer rule.** Article XI, Section $8$ sets age $40$ for the Ombudsman. Four non-lawyers leave at most $3$ of $7$ seats for $10$-year lawyers, short of the $4$-member majority.

**Check:** Nominee X fails $39 < 40$, and $3$ lawyers is under a majority of $7$.
:::

## Traps to Watch For

:::checklist
- COMELEC slate: $7$ members total, with at least $4$ ($10$-year lawyers) including the Chair. {#comelec-chair-in-majority}
- COA professional mix: verify $10$ qualifying years and never put all $3$ in one profession. {#coa-no-same-profession}
- Prior-candidacy bar: running in the immediately preceding election blocks appointment, even for winners or veteran lawyers. {#ombudsman-prior-candidacy}
:::

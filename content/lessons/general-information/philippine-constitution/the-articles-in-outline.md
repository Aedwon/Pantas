## Core Concept

The $1987$ Constitution of the Republic of the Philippines has a Preamble and $18$ Articles. The outline helps you find where a topic belongs.

An article title is only a location guide. It tells you where to look, but it does not give every rule, power, qualification, or procedure inside that article.

### Preamble

The **Preamble** explains why the Filipino people adopted the Constitution and the kind of country they seek to build. It speaks of a just and humane society, the common good, national patrimony, independence and democracy, the rule of law, and values such as truth, justice, freedom, equality, and peace.

The Preamble is not one of the $18$ Articles. Use it to recognize the Constitution's broad aims, not as a substitute for a rule found in an Article.

### Article Outline

- **Article I: National Territory.** It defines the territory over which the Philippines has sovereignty or jurisdiction.
- **Article II: Declaration of Principles and State Policies.** It states basic rules and goals of the State. For example, the Philippines is a democratic and republican State, and government authority comes from the people.
- **Article III: Bill of Rights.** It protects rights such as due process, equal protection, privacy, speech, religion, travel, and other liberties.
- **Article IV: Citizenship.** It states who are citizens of the Philippines and explains natural-born citizenship.
- **Article V: Suffrage.** It states the basic constitutional rules on who may vote.
- **Article VI: The Legislative Department.** It places legislative power in Congress, made up of the Senate and the House of Representatives.
- **Article VII: The Executive Department.** It places executive power in the President and contains rules on the President and Vice-President.
- **Article VIII: The Judicial Department.** It places judicial power in the courts and states the role of the Supreme Court and lower courts.
- **Article IX: Constitutional Commissions.** It covers the independent Constitutional Commissions: the Civil Service Commission, Commission on Elections, and Commission on Audit.
- **Article X: Local Government.** It contains constitutional rules on provinces, cities, municipalities, barangays, and local autonomy.
- **Article XI: Accountability of Public Officers.** It states that public office is a public trust and contains rules on accountability and impeachment.
- **Article XII: National Economy and Patrimony.** It covers the national economy, natural resources, and rules tied to national patrimony.
- **Article XIII: Social Justice and Human Rights.** It covers social justice, labor, agrarian and urban reform, health, women, people's organizations, and human rights.
- **Article XIV: Education, Science and Technology, Arts, Culture and Sports.** It sets major State duties and policies in these fields.
- **Article XV: The Family.** It recognizes the Filipino family as the foundation of the nation and gives constitutional protection to family life.
- **Article XVI: General Provisions.** It contains rules on matters such as the national flag, the armed forces, and other general constitutional subjects.
- **Article XVII: Amendments or Revisions.** It explains how changes to the Constitution may be proposed and ratified.
- **Article XVIII: Transitory Provisions.** It contains temporary rules used in the change from the old constitutional system to the $1987$ Constitution.

### Article IX Sub-parts

Article IX has four parts:

- **Article IX-A:** Common Provisions
- **Article IX-B:** Civil Service Commission
- **Article IX-C:** Commission on Elections
- **Article IX-D:** Commission on Audit

The Civil Service Commission is therefore found in **Article IX-B**. Article IX-B is a part of Article IX, not a separate Article after Article IX.

For exam questions, use the outline in two steps. First, locate the Article. Then recall or read the exact rule inside it.

## Examples

### #1: Total Number of Articles

How many Articles are in the $1987$ Constitution, excluding the Preamble?

**Solution:**

1. The Constitution begins with a Preamble.
2. The Articles run from Article I through Article XVIII.
3. Roman numeral XVIII equals $18$.
4. Therefore, the Constitution contains $18$ Articles.

### #2: Identifying the Article for Public Officer Accountability

Which Article contains the constitutional rules on public officer accountability and impeachment?

**Solution:**

1. Article IX covers the Constitutional Commissions.
2. Article X covers Local Government.
3. Article XI covers Accountability of Public Officers.
4. Therefore, the correct location is Article XI.

### #3: Locating the Civil Service Commission

Which main Article and sub-part cover the Civil Service Commission?

**Solution:**

1. Article IX covers the Constitutional Commissions.
2. Its parts are IX-A, IX-B, IX-C, and IX-D.
3. The Civil Service Commission is covered by Article IX-B.

### #4: Applying a State Principle

A statement says that sovereignty resides in the people and all government authority comes from them. Which Article should you review?

**Solution:**

1. The statement is a basic principle about the Philippine State.
2. Article II contains the Declaration of Principles and State Policies.
3. Therefore, the statement belongs in Article II.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: A Title Is Not the Full Rule

> Knowing an Article title does not mean you know every rule inside that Article.

Use the title to find the topic. For a question about a power, qualification, exception, or procedure, you still need the actual constitutional provision.

### #2: Do Not Count the Preamble as an Article

> The Preamble comes before the Articles but is not Article I.

The Constitution has a Preamble and $18$ Articles. Counting the Preamble as another Article gives the wrong total.

### #3: Article IX-B Is a Sub-part

> Do not treat Article IX-B as a separate Article outside Article IX.

Article IX is the Article on Constitutional Commissions. Part B of that Article is the Civil Service Commission.

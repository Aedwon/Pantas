## Core Concept

Article IX-B of the $1987$ Constitution covers the **Civil Service Commission**, or CSC. It is the government's **central personnel agency**. This means it leads the system for managing government workers.

### What the CSC Does

The CSC builds the **career service**, where people can develop a lasting government career based on their skills and work. It strengthens the system for choosing qualified workers and rewarding good service. Personal ties should not replace proof that someone can do the job.

The Commission brings together training programs for workers at different levels. Training can help a worker learn better ways to serve the public and prepare for greater duties. Career development means helping workers build these skills over time.

The CSC also promotes honest, efficient, helpful, and courteous service. It works to make public officials answer for how they do their jobs. These duties go beyond giving civil service examinations.

## Examples

### #1: Merit in Hiring and Career Development

An agency compares applicants' relevant skills and work records. It also trains current workers to handle greater duties. How do these actions relate to the CSC's role?

**Solution:**

1. Comparing skills and records supports appointments based on merit and fitness.
2. Training helps workers build skills for their careers.
3. Both actions support the CSC's personnel and career-development duties.

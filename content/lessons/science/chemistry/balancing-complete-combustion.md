## Core Concept

### Combustion

**Combustion** is burning: a fast reaction with oxygen that gives off energy. A **hydrocarbon** is a compound of only carbon and hydrogen, such as methane $CH_4$ and propane $C_3H_8$.

When there is plenty of oxygen, a fuel that has both carbon and hydrogen, and at most oxygen besides, burns **completely**. The products are carbon dioxide and water. For a hydrocarbon, the form is $C_xH_y+O_2\rightarrow CO_2+H_2O$.

A fuel with no hydrogen, such as carbon, gives no water.

## Examples

### #1: Complete Combustion of Propane

Write and balance the equation for the complete combustion of propane, $C_3H_8$.

**Solution:**

1. Complete combustion gives $CO_2$ and $H_2O$. The unbalanced equation is $C_3H_8+O_2\rightarrow CO_2+H_2O$.
2. Balance C first. Put $3$ before $CO_2$.
3. Balance H next. Put $4$ before $H_2O$.
4. O on the right is $3\times2+4=10$. Put $5$ before $O_2$.

$$C_3H_8+5O_2\rightarrow3CO_2+4H_2O$$

### #2: Combustion That Gives a Fraction

Balance the complete combustion of octane, $C_8H_{18}$.

**Solution:**

1. Put $8$ before $CO_2$ and $9$ before $H_2O$.
2. O on the right is $8\times2+9=25$. So $O_2$ needs the coefficient $\dfrac{25}{2}$.
3. Multiply every coefficient by $2$ to clear the fraction.
4. Check. C: $16$ on each side. H: $36$ on each side. O: $50$ on each side.

$$2C_8H_{18}+25O_2\rightarrow16CO_2+18H_2O$$

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

For a hydrocarbon in oxygen, balance C, then H, then O. If the O on the right is odd, the $O_2$ coefficient is a fraction, so double everything.

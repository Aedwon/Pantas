## Core Concept

### How separation works

A separation method uses a physical property in which the parts differ. Common properties are particle size, boiling point, solubility, magnetism, and how strongly a part is held by a surface. Choose the method by asking what is different between the parts.

## Examples

### #1: A Three-Part Mixture

A mixture has iron filings, sand, and salt. Describe a way to get all three.

**Solution:**

1. Pass a magnet over the dry mixture. The iron filings are attracted and removed. Sand and salt are not.
2. Add water and stir. The salt dissolves, but the sand does not.
3. Filter. The sand stays on the paper as residue.
4. Evaporate the water from the filtrate to get the salt.

## Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

Ask which physical property differs, then match the method:

- A magnetic part: a magnet. This works when some parts are magnetic and others are not.

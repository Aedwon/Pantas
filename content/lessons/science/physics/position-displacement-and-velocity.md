## Core Concept

**Kinematics** describes how things move. It does not ask what forces cause the motion. This lesson covers motion along a straight line.

### Position, Displacement, and Distance

Pick an origin and a positive direction, such as "east is positive." The **position** $x$ of an object is its place on that line, with a sign. A negative position is on the other side of the origin.

**Displacement** is the change in position. The Greek letter $\Delta$ (delta) means "change in", final value minus starting value. Write $x_i$ for the initial (starting) position and $x_f$ for the final (ending) position:

$$\Delta x=x_f-x_i$$

Displacement has a size and a direction. In one dimension the direction is the sign.

**Distance** is the total length of the path, and it is never negative. On a straight line, if the object never turns around, the distance equals the size of the displacement. The displacement itself is negative if the motion is in the negative direction. If the object turns around, the distance is bigger than the size of the displacement.

On a curved path, the distance is bigger than the size of the displacement even if the path never turns back.

### Speed and Velocity

**Average velocity** is displacement divided by the time taken. **Average speed** is distance divided by the time taken. Here $\Delta t$ is the change in time, the time taken:

$$v_{\text{avg}}=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$$

$$\text{average speed}=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\Delta t}$$

Velocity has a sign, so it shows direction. Speed has no sign. The units are meters per second, $\mathrm{m/s}$.

**Instantaneous velocity** is the velocity at one moment. Its size is the speed at that moment.

## Examples

### #1: Distance, Displacement, Speed, and Velocity

A runner goes $40$ m east and then $10$ m back west. The whole run takes $20$ s. East is positive. Calculate the distance, the displacement, the average speed, and the average velocity.

**Solution:**

1. Distance: $40+10=50$ m.
2. Displacement: $x_f-x_i=+30$ m, so $30$ m east.
3. Average speed:

$$\dfrac{50}{20}=2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$$

4. Average velocity:

$$\dfrac{+30}{20}=+1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$$

The sign is positive, so the direction is east.

## Traps to Watch For

### #1: Mixing Up Distance and Displacement

> Displacement is final position minus initial position. Distance is the path length.

In one dimension, the distance equals the size of the displacement when the object never turns around. The displacement is negative for motion in the negative direction, but the distance is not. For example, $10$ m west with east positive gives distance $10$ m and displacement $-10$ m. If the object turns around, the distance is bigger than the size of the displacement.

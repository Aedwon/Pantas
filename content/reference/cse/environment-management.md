# Environment Management and Protection

**Reference file for the fourth General Information area.**

**The laws below are named with their Republic Act numbers and titles, which are facts.** Their
operative provisions are **not** captured here yet. **Fetch a law before writing about what it
requires.**

---

## The laws in scope, by number and title

| Act | Title | What it governs |
|---|---|---|
| **RA 9003** | Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000 | Segregation at source, waste diversion, the ban on open dumpsites |
| **RA 8749** | Philippine Clean Air Act of 1999 | Air quality standards, emission limits, the ban on incineration |
| **RA 9275** | Philippine Clean Water Act of 2004 | Water quality management, discharge permits |
| **RA 9729** | Climate Change Act of 2009 | The Climate Change Commission, the national framework strategy |
| **RA 10121** | Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010 | The NDRRMC, and the shift from response to risk reduction |
| **PD 1586** | Environmental Impact Statement System | The Environmental Compliance Certificate |
| **RA 9147** | Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act | Protected species |
| **RA 7586** | National Integrated Protected Areas System Act, NIPAS | Protected areas |

**The lead agency across most of these is the Department of Environment and Natural
Resources.** The Climate Change Commission under RA 9729 is attached to the Office of the
President.

**The commonest exam item in this area is matching a law to what it covers**, which the table
above supports directly and safely.

---

## The concepts that are safe to write from

- **Segregation at source** is the principle that waste is separated where it is produced,
  rather than later.
- **Waste diversion** is keeping waste out of disposal facilities through reduction, reuse,
  recycling and composting.
- **Risk reduction rather than response** is the shift RA 10121 is known for: preparing for and
  reducing disaster risk instead of only reacting after an event.
- An **Environmental Compliance Certificate** is the document a project needs before proceeding,
  under the Environmental Impact Statement System.

---

## What must be fetched before it is written about

**Do not write from memory on any of these**, because they are exactly the details that get
misremembered:

- **Any numeric threshold.** Diversion rate targets, emission limits, water quality standards,
  fines and penalty amounts. **Every one of these must come from the law's text.**
- **Any deadline** set by any of the acts above.
- **The composition of any council or commission.**
- **Which specific wastes or substances are banned or regulated**, and under which act.

---

## Rules for anyone writing questions from this file

1. **Match-the-law-to-its-subject items are safe** and are the backbone of this area. Write
   those first.
2. **Never state a threshold, a fine, a percentage or a deadline that is not in this file.**
   Fetch the act, or write a different question.
3. **Never write an item about a current official, a current programme, or a figure that
   changes**, such as a present-day diversion rate.
4. **Prefer the principle to the number.** "What does segregation at source mean" is durable.
   "What is the diversion target for year X" is fragile and needs the text.
